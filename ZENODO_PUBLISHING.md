## Suggested abstract

The Lifelong Opportunity Guides are a free multilingual library of 101 practical career and education guides, numbered 00 through 100, created and directed by Alberto “Al” Leiva. The collection is designed for students, career changers, older adults, people with disabilities, workers seeking advancement, families, and community organizations. It emphasizes plain-language career pathways, free and low-cost learning options, accessibility, responsible use of artificial intelligence, current-source verification, and clear warnings against employment, income, admission, funding, licensing, or promotion guarantees. Editions are provided in English, neutral Latin American Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese. This dated record preserves the controlled 2026 revision snapshot through Guide 96.
