## Suggested release notes

The Lifelong Opportunity Guides are a free multilingual library of 101 practical career and education guides, numbered 00 through 100, created and directed by Alberto “Al” Leiva.

This release preserves the completed 2026 controlled revision of the full collection. All 101 guides are closed through Publication PASS and Release Audit PASS under the project’s evidence-supported release standard, with zero final blockers. Final collection QA validated 303 controlled PDF edition links: 101 English, 101 neutral Latin American Spanish (`es-419`), and 101 Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), with no missing linked PDF files.

The collection emphasizes plain-language career pathways, free and low-cost learning options, accessibility and disability-support considerations, responsible use of artificial intelligence, current-source verification, and clear warnings against guarantees of employment, income, admission, funding, licensing, or promotion.
