# Lifelong Opportunity Guides

Free, practical career and education guides designed to help people of all ages, abilities, backgrounds, and income levels explore realistic pathways to work and lifelong learning.

**Created and directed by:** Alberto “Al” Leiva  
**License:** [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](./LICENSE.md)  
**Languages:** English, neutral Latin American Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese

## Complete guide library

The repository contains **101 guides, numbered 00 through 100**. Every guide has:

- English DOCX and searchable PDF
- Latin American Spanish DOCX and searchable PDF
- Brazilian Portuguese DOCX and searchable PDF
- A guide-level README with direct download links
- A language-level README for each edition

Browse the [complete catalog](./CATALOG.md) or begin with [Guide 00 — Foundation Guide](./00-foundation-guide/).

## Important quality distinction

File availability does not automatically mean that an edition is an exact line-by-line translation or that it received independent human linguistic review. Consult each edition's metadata and the quality records under [project/](./project/) before making that claim.

## Project mission

This project helps people investigate realistic education and career paths using plain language, accessibility, ethical AI guidance, free-first learning options, employer-supported education, and clear warnings against guarantees or predatory programs.

The guides are designed for students, career changers, older adults, people with disabilities, workers seeking advancement, families planning education, and community organizations helping people find opportunity.

## Core commitments

- No employment, income, admission, funding, licensing, or promotion guarantees
- Free and low-cost learning options before expensive programs
- Careful review of accreditation, licensing, contracts, repayment terms, and total cost
- Accessibility and disability-support guidance
- Ethical, private, and responsible use of AI
- Verification through current official sources
- Plain language and practical next steps

## About the author

I am Alberto “Al” Leiva, and I began this project at age 70. My experience with dysgraphia and the autism spectrum helped shape its accessibility focus.

ChatGPT assisted with research support, organization, editing, translation support, and document preparation under my direction. I remain responsible for the project's content and editorial decisions.

## Important notice

The guides provide education and general career-planning information. They are not legal, tax, medical, immigration, financial, licensing, or individualized professional advice.

Requirements, wages, employment outlook, credentials, costs, and benefits vary by country, state, locality, employer, and time. Verify important decisions through current official sources and qualified professionals.
