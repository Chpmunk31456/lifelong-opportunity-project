# Revision Standard for Guides 00-100

## Scope

This revision program updates the Lifelong Opportunity Guides sequentially from Guide 00 through Guide 100.

## Required workflow for each guide

1. Review and correct the English source.
2. Verify spelling, grammar, punctuation, consistency, accessibility, and natural human readability.
3. Add or strengthen funding, free and low-cost training, scholarships, grants, employer support, apprenticeships, internships, co-ops, traineeships, and work-based learning.
4. Cover the United States, Canada, and Latin America, with Colombia pathways where relevant.
5. Add a carefully qualified compensation section using official labor data as the baseline and current non-government estimates as supporting evidence.
6. Distinguish base pay, total compensation, overtime, premiums, commissions, bonuses, contractor rates, self-employment revenue, and take-home pay.
7. Verify licensing, certification, accreditation, privacy, safety, and jurisdictional limitations.
8. Add practical search terms in English, neutral Latin American Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.
9. Produce and review neutral Latin American Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese editions from the approved English source.
10. Run terminology, structure, link, DOCX, PDF, metadata, accessibility, and publication QA.

## Editorial standard

Writing must be accurate, specific, useful, and natural. It should sound like Al explaining a subject carefully to a real person.

Review for:

- Spelling, grammar, punctuation, and consistency
- Natural sentence and paragraph rhythm
- Concrete facts and occupation-specific examples
- Plain language where technical jargon is unnecessary
- Repetition, inflated language, generic transitions, and formulaic conclusions
- Unsupported claims, vague attribution, invented citations, placeholders, and broken markup
- Excessive headings, bolding, tables, em dashes, and repetitive three-part phrasing

The goal is publication quality and reader trust, not defeating automated detection systems.

## Compensation research standard

Use official government or recognized labor-market data first where available. Supplement it with current evidence such as:

- Glassdoor
- Indeed Salaries
- PayScale
- Salary.com
- Talent.com
- LinkedIn salary information where available
- Current employer job postings with disclosed compensation
- Union wage schedules, collective agreements, public pay scales, and professional surveys where relevant

Label the location, date checked, pay basis, base versus total compensation, entry or experience level, and source limitations. Crowdsourced estimates are supporting evidence, not guarantees.

## Funding and pathway standard

Each guide must distinguish:

- Free training
- Subsidized or low-cost training
- Grants and scholarships
- Forgivable aid with conditions
- Employer-paid or reimbursed learning
- Paid apprenticeships, co-ops, traineeships, and work-based learning
- Unpaid internships or volunteer experience
- Loans and other repayable financing

Loans are not the first resource. Readers should exhaust grants, public funding, employer support, apprenticeships, scholarships, libraries, and low-cost alternatives before borrowing.

Every listed opportunity should identify the country or region, provider, support type, basic eligibility, application status where known, official link, and last-verified date.

## Translation and accreditation language

Translations may be described as machine-assisted and editorially reviewed for completeness, terminology, structure, and formatting when those checks are completed.

Do not describe a translation as independently certified, professionally accredited, or human-reviewed unless a qualified external reviewer has actually provided that approval.

## Publication gate

A guide is complete only after all intended English, Spanish, and Portuguese DOCX and searchable PDF files pass:

- Factual and source verification
- Editorial and natural-readability review
- Funding and compensation coverage
- Licensing and jurisdictional safeguards
- Translation completeness and terminology consistency
- Accessibility review
- Link and filename checks
- Rendered-page visual inspection
- Metadata and version reconciliation
- Publication-candidate review
