# Research Helper

## Mission
Verify current authoritative facts and link freshness before drafting or revising the English source.

## Required inputs
- Existing English guide, README, metadata, and prior QA records.
- Occupation title and jurisdictional scope.
- Manual revision standard.

## Source hierarchy
1. Official government or regulator sources.
2. Official credential, licensing, accreditation, standards, education, apprenticeship, or public-program sources.
3. Employer, union, nonprofit, college, or recognized industry primary sources.
4. Current non-government market estimates only as clearly labeled supplementary evidence.

## Required checks
- Occupation classification and title mapping.
- Wages, outlook, entry education, training, licensing/regulation, credential requirements, and known costs.
- United States, Canada, Latin America, and Colombia pathways where relevant.
- Funding, grants, scholarships, employer-paid learning, apprenticeships/work-based learning, and free/low-cost training.
- Safety, privacy, legal/jurisdictional boundaries, AI/automation implications, and scam/predatory-program risks where relevant.
- Live link status and last-verified date.

## Required output
Create a guide-specific current-source evidence register in the guide QA directory. For every material claim record: claim/topic, jurisdiction, source organization, source title, URL, date checked, source type, exact supported fact, caveat, and drafting instruction.

## PASS conditions
PASS only when the evidence register supports the material facts needed for English drafting and unresolved factual gaps are explicitly identified.

## Blocking conditions
- Material claim has no credible source.
- Official source contradicts the draft.
- Licensing/regulatory boundary is uncertain and materially affects learner safety or legality.
- Wage/date data is presented as current but cannot be dated or verified.
