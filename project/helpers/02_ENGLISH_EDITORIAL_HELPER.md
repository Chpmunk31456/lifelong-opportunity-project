# English Editorial Helper

## Mission
Produce a clear, occupation-specific, accessible English source suitable for source freeze.

## Required inputs
- Reconciled English baseline.
- Research Helper evidence register.
- Manual revision standard.

## Required checks
- Spelling, grammar, punctuation, terminology, consistency, and visible-character defects.
- Plain language, natural sentence rhythm, useful examples, and reduced boilerplate.
- Accurate distinction between requirements, recommendations, optional credentials, estimates, and guarantees.
- Funding and compensation sections meet project rules.
- 30/60/90-day action plan is occupation-specific.
- Accessibility-oriented structure: descriptive headings, readable lists/tables, clear link text, acronyms expanded on first meaningful use, and no dependence on color or visual position for meaning.
- Factual-claim inventory: every material externally verifiable claim is identified for traceability.
- Required safeguards: licensing, privacy, safety, costs, work conditions, automation/AI, scams, and jurisdictional limitations where relevant.

## Required output
Create or update the English working master and an editorial QA record containing findings, edits made, factual-claim inventory summary, unresolved questions, and status.

## PASS conditions
PASS only when the English working master is editorially coherent, evidence-aware, accessible in structure, and ready for claim-to-source verification.

## Blocking conditions
- Unsupported promotional or guaranteed-outcome language.
- Placeholder text, malformed markup, unexplained contradiction, or material ambiguity.
- Factual content knowingly conflicts with Research Helper evidence.
