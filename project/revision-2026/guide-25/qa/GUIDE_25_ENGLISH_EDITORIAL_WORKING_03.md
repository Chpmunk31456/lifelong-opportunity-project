## Editorial corrections established

1. **Legacy status language corrected.** The English README previously described the July 2026 v1.0 package as a technically reviewed publication edition while the controlled Version 2.0 revision is still underway. The README now identifies those DOCX/PDF files as legacy reference inputs and explicitly states that the controlled revision remains incomplete.
2. **Occupation scope must remain bounded.** The revised guide must describe general maintenance and repair work without teaching readers to perform regulated electrical, plumbing, HVAC/R, boiler, pesticide, asbestos/lead, elevator, or similar specialist work outside applicable qualification, licensing, employer-policy, or jurisdictional requirements.
3. **Safety language must be employer-procedure led.** Lockout/tagout and hazardous-energy control must be framed as formal employer-controlled safety processes requiring procedures and training, not as self-taught maintenance skills.
4. **Official and commercial compensation data must remain separate.** U.S. BLS statistics are the official national occupational baseline. Indeed salary figures may appear only as dated, explicitly non-government market estimates and must not be blended into a single wage claim.
5. **Funding language must be verification-first.** WIOA/American Job Center support, registered apprenticeship, employer reimbursement, scholarships, tools/PPE assistance, union/industry training, and other support must be presented as pathways to investigate, never as guaranteed benefits.
6. **Regional portability must not be implied.** U.S., Canadian, and Colombian programs have different eligibility and regulatory systems. The guide must tell readers to verify local rules before relying on another country's pathway.

## Required English Version 2.0 content floor

The reconstructed English master must, at minimum, include:

- plain-language description of general maintenance/building maintenance work;
- realistic entry pathways, including high-school/equivalent and on-the-job learning context where applicable;
- current U.S. BLS occupation, median-pay, employment/outlook, and openings data with national-statistics caveats;
- clearly labeled 2026 Indeed maintenance-market estimates as optional commercial context;
- Apprenticeship.gov and American Job Center/WIOA locators;
- employer-paid education/training verification checklist, including approval, grade/service, repayment, tax, and employment-duration conditions;
- OSHA hazardous-energy/lockout-tagout boundary and referral to employer procedures/training;
- Canada NOC 73201 wage/requirements comparison with provincial/territorial licensing caveat;
- Colombia SENA Betowa, Agencia Pública de Empleo SENA, and Servicio Público de Empleo locators;
- a Latin America locator-first section using official vocational-training, employment-service, labor-ministry, municipal, union, and employer pathways rather than unsupported cross-country assumptions;
- free-first learning and 'verify before paying' guidance;
- accessibility and accommodation prompts for training and employment services;
- ethical AI guidance that permits planning, drafting, and study support but requires verification of legal, safety, licensing, wage, and eligibility claims;
- explicit non-guarantee language for employment, wages, admissions, funding, apprenticeship placement, reimbursement, credentials, and promotions.
