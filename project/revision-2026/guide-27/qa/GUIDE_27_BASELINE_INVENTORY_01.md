## Existing publication state

The English edition README identifies the existing package as **version 1.0**, published July 2026, with DOCX and searchable PDF files present. Its review notice explicitly says exact source equivalence and independent human linguistic review should not be assumed unless separately documented.

These are baseline inputs only and must be reverified for the controlled v2.0 revision.

## Controlled-revision gaps

Before Guide 27 can advance to publication, the controlled revision must independently verify and document:

- current U.S. diesel-service occupation, wage, outlook, education, training, and work-environment evidence;
- clearly separated current non-government compensation estimates;
- diesel-specific worker-safety and regulated-task boundaries, including shop hazards, exhaust exposure, lifting/vehicle-support practices, batteries/electrical systems, and refrigerant-service requirements where applicable;
- free/low-cost training, workforce funding, employer support, and apprenticeship/work-based-learning pathways;
- current Canada occupation classification, wage, apprenticeship/certification, Red Seal or provincial/territorial pathways where applicable, and jurisdiction-specific verification guidance;
- current Colombia SENA and public-employment pathways relevant to diesel, heavy-vehicle, or heavy-equipment maintenance;
- a Latin America locator framework that does not transpose U.S., Canadian, or Colombian licensing, certification, environmental, or worker-safety rules across jurisdictions;
- ethical AI, privacy, accessibility, emissions-system integrity, electronic diagnostics, and verification guidance appropriate to modern diesel service;
- English evidence traceability and source freeze before localization;
- es-419 and pt-BR parity against the frozen English source;
- trilingual structural, terminology, link, encoding, DOCX, PDF, metadata, rendering, checksum, publication, and release-audit controls.
