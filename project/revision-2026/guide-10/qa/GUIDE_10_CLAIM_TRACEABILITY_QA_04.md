## Traceability checks

### U.S. official occupational benchmark

The English master accurately treats BLS Training and Development Specialists, SOC 13-1151, as a broader related benchmark. The master matches the verified BLS values: **$65,850** median, below **$37,510** lowest decile, above **$120,190** highest decile, **452,300** jobs in 2024, **501,000** projected in 2034, **11%** growth, **48,700** numeric increase, and approximately **43,900 openings/year**.

### Current U.S. nongovernment title estimate

The ZipRecruiter Training and Development Coordinator estimate is isolated from official data and reports the reviewed July 20, 2026 values: **$57,194/year**, **$27.50/hour**, and approximately **$47,000–$66,000/year** for the common range shown on the page. The guide explicitly says this is not an official statistic or guarantee.

### Canada

The master matches Job Bank's Staff Training and Development Officer mapping to **NOC 11200** and the national wage table: **C$26.00/hour low, C$40.87/hour median, C$59.77/hour high**, updated November 19, 2025 for reference period 2023–2024. The degree language is qualified as Job Bank's usual requirement for the NOC rather than a universal legal requirement for every coordinator vacancy.

### Colombia

The master accurately identifies SENA `Gestión del talento humano` and the **48-hour** `Gestión del desarrollo del talento humano` complementary course, while warning that availability, modality, location, and cohort dates change. It does not fabricate a Colombia-wide salary figure.

### Funding and verification

The master uses verification-first language for CareerOneStop/WIOA, Federal Student Aid, Apprenticeship.gov, U.S. Department of Education accreditation resources, EEOC disability resources, and IRS Section 127. The Section 127 figure is accurately limited to **$5,250** under a qualifying employer plan for 2025 and 2026.

### Risk boundaries

Privacy, cybersecurity, accessibility, AI, accommodation, training-record integrity, compliance, safety, clinical, labour-relations, and legal issues are framed as general risk-navigation guidance with escalation to responsible authorities or qualified professionals.
