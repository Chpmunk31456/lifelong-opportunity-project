## United States — official BLS benchmark

BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, Training and Development Specialists (SOC 13-1151):

- May 2024 median annual wage: **$65,850**
- lowest 10%: below **$37,510**
- highest 10%: above **$120,190**
- 2024 employment: **452,300**
- 2034 projected employment: **501,000**
- projected growth 2024–2034: **11%**
- projected numeric change: **48,700**
- projected openings: about **43,900/year**
- typical pathway described by BLS: bachelor's degree plus related work experience

Source: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/training-and-development-specialists.htm

The guide treats this as a broader specialist benchmark rather than a one-to-one mapping for every coordinator title.

## Colombia — SENA and public employment

SENA Betowa currently documents relevant pathways:

- `Gestión del talento humano`: titled program covering planning, execution, and evaluation of training and competency-development activities; current catalog page identifies a 24-month program.
- `Gestión del desarrollo del talento humano`: complementary special course, **48 hours**, focused on planning human-talent development. Current offerings are cohort/location dependent.

Sources:
