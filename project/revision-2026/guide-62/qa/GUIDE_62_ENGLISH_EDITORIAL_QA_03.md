- clear distinction between teacher-assistant support work and licensed/regulated professional authority;
- explicit safeguarding and emergency escalation boundaries;
- privacy and FERPA-aware handling of student records;
- disability-inclusive and accessibility-aware language;
- special-needs and personal-care training/authorization boundaries;
- free-first and low-cost training strategy;
- U.S., Canada, Colombia, and Latin America pathway coverage;
- separate official and non-government compensation evidence;
- school-year/part-time compensation caveat;
- responsible AI and cybersecurity controls; and
- assurance language that does not claim independent human certification or accreditation.

## Findings

No blocking editorial defect was identified. The master avoids presenting the worker as the teacher of record or as independently authorized to diagnose, alter IEPs or accommodations, provide regulated clinical services, make safeguarding determinations reserved to designated authorities, or use higher-risk procedures without training and authorization.

The U.S. BLS broad occupational wage/outlook evidence is explicitly distinguished from the detailed O*NET occupation code surfaced by Apprenticeship.gov. Canada is separately mapped to NOC 43100. Colombia is handled conservatively without inventing a one-to-one national wage series.
