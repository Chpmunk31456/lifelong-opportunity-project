## Purpose

This record documents a sentence-level editorial review of the opening occupational-description and entry-requirements sections of the neutral Latin American Spanish working master. It separates completed review evidence from corrections that still require controlled application and verification.

## Findings and controlled replacement requirements

### 1. Avoid an English-derived escalation calque

**Current wording:**

> escalar inquietudes urgentes, clínicas, de protección o privacidad al personal calificado.

**Required replacement:**

> remitir de inmediato al personal calificado las inquietudes urgentes o relacionadas con asuntos clínicos, de protección o de privacidad.

**Reason:** In this context, *escalar inquietudes* is an avoidable calque from English. The replacement is clearer across Latin America, identifies the receiving party, and preserves the duty to refer rather than independently resolve matters outside the worker’s authorized scope.

### 2. Use a more natural expression for conflict de-escalation

**Current wording:**

> desescalamiento de conflictos;

**Required replacement:**

> capacidad para reducir la tensión y manejar conflictos de forma segura;

**Reason:** The replacement uses plain, natural language and communicates the underlying competency more clearly to readers who may not know specialist terminology.

### 3. Replace another escalation calque in the safety paragraph

**Current wording:**

> Los empleadores deben proporcionar supervisión, procedimientos de seguridad, rutas de escalamiento y apoyo para casos difíciles.

**Required replacement:**

> Los empleadores deben proporcionar supervisión, procedimientos de seguridad, vías claras para remitir situaciones que excedan las funciones del trabajador y apoyo para casos difíciles.

**Reason:** The replacement explains the action expected of the worker and reinforces scope-of-practice safeguards.

### 4. Improve natural phrasing for typical entry preparation

**Current wording:**

> la formación de corto plazo en el puesto de trabajo como la preparación habitual.

**Required replacement:**

> la capacitación de corta duración en el puesto de trabajo como preparación habitual.

**Reason:** *Capacitación de corta duración* is more natural in neutral Latin American Spanish and more closely describes the source concept without implying a standardized academic program.

## Accessibility and meaning-preservation check

The required replacements:

- preserve the occupational meaning of the English controlled master;

- maintain the distinction between a community health worker and licensed clinical professionals;
- improve plain-language readability for adult learners;
