## United States — primary official source

**U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook — Community Health Workers**  
URL: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/community-health-workers.htm  
Page last modified: August 28, 2025  
Data period: May 2024 wage data; 2024–2034 projections

### Controlled figures

- Median annual wage: **USD 51,030**
- Median hourly wage: **USD 24.54**
- Employment in 2024: **65,100 jobs**
- Projected employment growth, 2024–2034: **11%**, identified by BLS as much faster than average
- Projected openings: the current BLS narrative states **about 7,800 openings per year**, on average, over the decade
- Typical entry-level education: **high school diploma or equivalent**
- Typical on-the-job training: **short-term on-the-job training**

### Editorial use controls

1. Present USD 51,030 and USD 24.54 as national medians, not entry salaries or guaranteed offers.
2. Preserve the May 2024 data period and the page’s August 28, 2025 modification date.
3. Use 11% only for the 2024–2034 projection period.
4. Use the narrative figure of about 7,800 annual openings. Do not use the inconsistent 7,400 figure shown in some machine-readable quick-facts extracts without explaining the discrepancy.
5. Explain that pay varies by state, metropolitan area, employer, union coverage, schedule, language skills, duties, and certification requirements.
6. Do not infer that the national education profile overrides employer-specific or state certification requirements.

## Canada — primary official source

**Government of Canada Job Bank — Community Health Worker in Canada: Wages**  
URL: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/296075/ca  
Occupation mapping: NOC 42201, Social and community service workers  
Page modified: June 2, 2026  
Wage table updated: November 19, 2025  
Reference period: 2023–2024

### Controlled figures

- National low wage: **CAD 19.00 per hour**
- National median wage: **CAD 26.00 per hour**
- National high wage: **CAD 36.06 per hour**
- Workers receiving at least one non-wage benefit: **83.6%**

### Editorial use controls

1. Label these as national estimates for NOC 42201, which is broader than the U.S. BLS Community Health Workers occupation.
2. Do not present the low-to-high range as a guaranteed salary band.
3. Direct readers to provincial, territorial, and regional Job Bank tables before making education or employment decisions.
4. Keep community health nursing separate because nursing is a regulated occupation with different education, registration, and wage data.
5. Describe the 83.6% figure as receipt of at least one employer-provided non-wage benefit, not a promise of any particular benefit.

## Colombia and Latin America — income control

No single national Colombian occupation series has been verified in this batch as directly equivalent to the U.S. BLS Community Health Workers occupation. The English revision must therefore:

- avoid converting U.S. or Canadian wages into Colombian salary expectations;
- distinguish paid employment from volunteer service, community participation, stipends, project payments, grants, reimbursements, and scholarships;
- identify the actual employer type before discussing income: EPS, IPS, ESE, territorial government, NGO, university project, contractor, or authorized community-based organization;
- use current official Colombian sources or disclosed employer postings when adding a local salary example;
- label any non-government salary estimate by source, access date, geography, sample limitations, and whether it reflects base pay or total compensation.

## Non-government market estimates

No non-government estimate is approved for integration in this register yet. Any later Glassdoor, Indeed, salary-survey, or disclosed-employer figure must be supplementary to official evidence and must pass all of the following controls:

- current and accessible on the review date;
- clearly tied to the correct occupation and geography;
- labeled as an estimate or disclosed posting, not official labor statistics;
- sample size or methodological limitation disclosed when available;
- not averaged together with BLS or Job Bank figures;
- not represented as guaranteed compensation.

## QA findings

- The current BLS page contains a discrepancy between a machine-readable quick-facts value of 7,400 openings and narrative text stating about 7,800 openings per year. The controlled revision will use the narrative figure and document the source date.
- The Canadian Job Bank title maps to NOC 42201 and must not be treated as perfectly equivalent to the U.S. occupation.
- Currency, geography, data period, and source type must remain visible wherever figures are used.
