- Edition stage: English factual-revision research
- Register date: 2026-08-03
- Branch: `revision/guide-00-100-2026`
- Status: authoritative-source register completed; English integrated master not yet approved
- Independent human, medical, legal, accessibility, translation, or accreditation certification: not claimed

## Use rules

1. Treat the occupation title and scope as jurisdiction-specific. Do not imply that a community health worker is a nurse, physician, licensed clinician, or independently regulated practitioner unless the cited jurisdiction expressly says so.
2. Keep official wage figures separate from employer postings and other market estimates. State the source date, currency, geography, unit, and occupational classification.
3. Describe training, certification, licensing, and apprenticeship pathways as examples rather than universal requirements.
4. Recheck time-sensitive wage, outlook, programme, funding, and regulatory claims immediately before publication.
5. Use plain-language explanations and explicitly distinguish navigation/support duties from diagnosis, prescribing, and other regulated clinical functions.

## Authoritative occupation, wage, and outlook sources

### United States

**U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — Occupational Outlook Handbook: Community Health Workers**  
URL: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/community-health-workers.htm  
Page last modified: 2025-08-28  
Accessed: 2026-08-03  
Controlled facts available for revision:

- SOC 21-1094.
- Typical entry-level education: high school diploma or equivalent.
- Typical on-the-job training: short term.
- 2024 median pay reported by BLS: USD 51,030 annually and USD 24.54 hourly.
- Employment projection: 11% growth from 2024 to 2034, with approximately 7,800 openings per year on average.
- Role framing: promoting wellness, helping people adopt healthy behaviours, implementing programmes, advocacy, and connecting people with health and social resources.

Revision caution: national medians do not predict an individual offer; state, employer, funding source, credentials, language ability, schedule, and experience materially affect compensation.

URL: https://www.apprenticeship.gov/apprenticeship-industries/healthcare  
Accessed: 2026-08-03  
Controlled fact: Community Health Worker is listed among high-demand healthcare Registered Apprenticeship occupations.

Revision use:

- Explain that Registered Apprenticeship combines paid work, structured on-the-job learning, related instruction, progressive wage increases, and a nationally recognized credential when the programme meets federal or state registration requirements.
- Direct readers to the official Apprenticeship Job Finder and local workforce system rather than promising a placement.
- State that programme availability varies by state, sponsor, and funding cycle.

## Colombia and Latin America pathway controls

### Colombia

**Ministerio de Salud y Protección Social — Organizaciones de Base Comunitaria under Resolución 2628 de 2025**  
Repository entry accessed through the Ministry’s Biblioteca Digital: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/sites/rid/lists/bibliotecadigital/forms/todos%20los%20items.aspx  
Materials published in the repository: 2026-06-30 to 2026-07-01  
Accessed: 2026-08-03  
Controlled facts available for revision:

- Resolución 2628 de 2025 establishes requirements and processes for authorization and operation of community-based organizations that participate in comprehensive community health care.
- Departmental and district territorial health authorities evaluate applications and issue operating authorizations.
- Ministry manuals describe registration, documentary support, evaluation, correction requests, and approval or denial processes.

Revision caution: an authorized community-based organization is not the same as an individual occupational licence. The final guide must not imply that this resolution creates one national personal credential titled “Community Health Worker.”

**Ministerio de Salud y Protección Social — Salud Mental Comunitaria**  
URL: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/publica/salud-mental/Paginas/salud-mental-comunitaria.aspx  
Accessed: 2026-08-03  
Controlled use: Colombia’s community-health ecosystem includes community mental-health devices, community-based rehabilitation, psychological first-aid guidance, and territorial integrated health networks. Use these as examples of community-facing work settings and learning topics, not as automatic qualifications.

### Latin America and global applicability

Use WHO and PAHO-aligned terminology carefully. “Community health worker,” “promotor/a de salud,” “agente comunitario de salud,” and related titles are not interchangeable across countries. The Spanish and Portuguese editions must retain local examples while clearly stating that hiring standards, recognized credentials, public-service classifications, and permitted duties differ by country and employer.

## Funding and low-cost learning research gate

The integrated English master must include a verification table with, at minimum:

- U.S. workforce funding routes: American Job Centers, WIOA eligibility screening, Registered Apprenticeship sponsors, community colleges, public-health departments, and employer tuition support.
- Canada routes: provincial student aid, public colleges, employer-supported training, Indigenous and newcomer employment services where applicable, and apprenticeship or work-integrated learning programmes.
- Colombia routes: SENA programme search, territorial health secretariats, public employment services, Ministry-authorized community organizations, and employer/NGO training.
- Latin America routes: national public vocational systems, ministries of health, public universities, PAHO/WHO open learning, and verified NGO programmes.

No scholarship, grant, free course, stipend, or employer benefit may be described as currently available until its official page, eligibility rules, geography, and application status have been checked during the English integration stage.

## Non-government income research gate

Current non-government estimates and disclosed employer postings remain pending. They must be collected close to publication and labelled separately as market observations, not official wage statistics. Required controls:

- Record employer, title, location, remote/on-site status, posting date, currency, pay period, and stated range.
- Exclude postings whose duties are substantially clinical, nursing, case-management, or social-work roles unless the distinction is explained.
- Do not calculate a market average from a small or non-representative sample.
- Archive only permissible citation details; do not reproduce copyrighted job descriptions.
