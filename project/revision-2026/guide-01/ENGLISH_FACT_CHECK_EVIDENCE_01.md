## Evidence-handling rules

- Prefer current official government, intergovernmental, public-education, and recognized workforce sources.
- Record the source date or the date shown on the source page whenever available.
- Label commercial salary estimates as estimates based on platform methodology, postings, or user submissions.
- Do not mix Community Health Worker data with registered nursing, social work, or other regulated occupations.
- Do not imply that a course, certificate, apprenticeship, or credential guarantees employment, licensing, immigration status, funding, or income.
- Recheck time-sensitive facts and links before publication.

## United States — occupation, entry pathway, pay, and outlook

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, “Community Health Workers.”

URL: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/community-and-social-service/community-health-workers.htm

Source page last modified: 2025-08-28.

Verified findings for controlled integration:

- Community health workers promote wellness, help people adopt healthy behaviours, implement programs, and advocate for people who may have limited access to health resources and social services.
- Typical entry education is a high school diploma or equivalent, although some employers prefer postsecondary education.
- Short-term on-the-job training is typical, and some states require certification.
- Median pay was USD 51,030 annually, or USD 24.54 hourly, in May 2024.
- Employment is projected to grow 11% from 2024 through 2034.
- BLS projects approximately 7,800 openings per year on average during that period.

Controlled wording requirement:

> These are national occupational statistics, not a promise of local pay, hiring, hours, benefits, or job availability. Readers must check state requirements and current local postings.

### Current non-government U.S. pay estimate

Source: Indeed Career Explorer, “Community health worker salary in United States.”

URL: https://www.indeed.com/career/community-health-worker/salaries

Source update observed: 2026-07-20.

Verified platform estimate:

- Average base salary estimate: USD 24.40 per hour.
- Displayed low-to-high range: USD 12.72 to USD 46.79 per hour.
- Indeed stated that the estimate used approximately 3,500 salaries taken from job postings during the preceding 36 months.

Controlled wording requirement:

> Indeed is a commercial job platform. Its estimate is a current market signal, not an official wage statistic or guaranteed offer. Methodology, sample mix, location, title matching, and posting quality can affect the result.

## Canada — title mapping, requirements, wages, and benefits

### Government of Canada Job Bank

Source: Government of Canada Job Bank, “Community Health Worker in Canada — Labour Market Facts and Figures.”

URL: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/296075/ca

Page date modified: 2026-06-02.

Verified findings for controlled integration:

- Job Bank maps this title to Social and community service workers, NOC 42201.
- The profile describes workers who administer and implement social-assistance programs and community services and assist clients with personal and social problems.
- The national profile states that this occupation usually requires a college diploma, an apprenticeship of two or more years, or relevant supervisory experience.
- Requirements and title usage vary by province, territory, employer, and service setting.

Scope warning:

> The Canadian Job Bank title mapping is broader than the U.S. BLS Community Health Worker occupation. The guide must explain this difference and must not present the two classifications as identical.

### Government of Canada Job Bank — wages

Source: Government of Canada Job Bank, “Community Health Worker in Canada — Wages.”

URL: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/296075/ca

Wage data updated: 2025-11-19. Page date modified: 2026-06-02. Reference period: 2023–2024 Labour Force Survey, Statistics Canada.

Verified national wage findings:

- Low: CAD 19.00 per hour.
- Median: CAD 26.00 per hour.
- High: CAD 36.06 per hour.
- Job Bank reported that 83.6% of workers in NOC 42201 received at least one non-wage benefit.

Controlled wording requirement:

> These data apply to the broader NOC 42201 classification and may include roles whose duties, credentials, and pay differ from a specific community health worker vacancy.

## Latin America — regional framing requirement

The integrated English edition must distinguish among:

- community health worker;
- promotora or promotor de salud;
- agente comunitario de salud;
- auxiliar promotor de salud;
- patient navigator;
- social and community service worker; and
- regulated clinical occupations.

The guide must warn readers that titles, legal scope, employer requirements, training systems, compensation models, and employment status vary substantially across countries and programmes. A role may be salaried employment, contracted programme work, a stipend-supported community function, or volunteer service. Country-specific official sources must be checked before a reader relies on the title.
