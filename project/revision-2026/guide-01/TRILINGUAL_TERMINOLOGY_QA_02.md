## 3. Professional-scope safeguards

All three working masters preserve the following controls:

- a community health worker is not automatically a nurse, physician, psychologist, social worker, therapist, or other licensed clinician;
- duties depend on employer authorization, program rules, training, supervision, and local law;
- urgent, clinical, safeguarding, and privacy concerns must be escalated to qualified personnel;
- the guide does not authorize diagnosis, prescribing, emergency decision-making, or independent clinical practice;
- community-based or organizational authorization does not automatically create an individual professional licence.

**Result:** PASS.

### Editorial consistency note

The Portuguese master sometimes uses the shorter phrase `aprendizagem formal` where the terminology register gives the fuller controlled form `aprendizagem profissional formal remunerada`. The surrounding text explicitly states that a formal apprenticeship is structured paid work with related instruction, so the legal and financial distinction is preserved. During final editorial correction, use the fuller controlled form on first substantive definition and allow the shorter form only after the meaning has been established.

The Spanish master uses `aprendizaje formal` in readable prose while defining it as structured paid work with related instruction. During final editorial correction, retain `aprendizaje formal remunerado` at the first substantive definition to match the terminology register exactly.

**Result:** CONDITIONAL PASS — meaning is preserved; first-definition wording requires final consistency correction.
