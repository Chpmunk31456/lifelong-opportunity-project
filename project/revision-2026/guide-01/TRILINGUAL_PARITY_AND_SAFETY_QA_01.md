## Safety-meaning result

The following safety meanings are preserved across English, es-419, and pt-BR:

- a community health worker is not automatically a licensed clinician;
- workers must remain within authorized duties, supervision, training, and local law;
- urgent, clinical, safeguarding/protection, and privacy concerns must be referred to qualified personnel;
- course completion does not guarantee employment, certification, registration, licensure, promotion, or income;
- wages, stipends, scholarships/grants, loans, reimbursements, apprenticeships, and completion certificates are distinguished;
- private or identifiable patient and community-member information must not be entered into unapproved AI services;
- AI must not be used to diagnose, prescribe, determine eligibility, or make emergency decisions;
- salary figures are reference points rather than promises;
- non-government salary estimates are labeled separately from official statistics.

**Safety-meaning gate result:** PASS for the controlled meanings reviewed.
