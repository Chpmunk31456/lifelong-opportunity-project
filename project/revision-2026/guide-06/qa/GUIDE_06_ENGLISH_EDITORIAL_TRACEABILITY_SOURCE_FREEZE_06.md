## Editorial and terminology controls

PASS:
- Administrative support is separated from executive, legal, HR, financial, medical, technical, and regulated responsibilities.
- Authority boundaries are explicit for contracts, payments, hiring, medical/legal decisions, records, confidential information, and payment-detail changes.
- Privacy/cybersecurity controls require approved systems, channels, devices, storage, and AI services.
- Ethical-AI language permits authorized generic drafting/formatting/practice while prohibiting fabricated approvals, minutes, signatures, expenses, decisions, credentials, and confidential facts.
- Accessibility is treated as an end-to-end training/testing/workplace issue rather than assumed from admission.
- Private credentials are not represented as government licenses or universal hiring requirements.
- U.S., Canada, Colombia, and broader Latin America claims are geographically qualified.
- Funding, scholarship, apprenticeship, employer-support, and repayment language requires eligibility and written-term verification.
- Prose is direct, plain-language oriented, and avoids employment, wage, credential, funding, or accessibility guarantees.
