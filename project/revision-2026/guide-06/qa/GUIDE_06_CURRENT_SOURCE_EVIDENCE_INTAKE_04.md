## United States — official occupational anchor

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

Primary source: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/secretaries-and-administrative-assistants.htm

BLS reports for May 2024:

- Secretaries and administrative assistants, overall median annual wage: **$47,460**.
- Lowest 10 percent: below **$33,840**.
- Highest 10 percent: above **$76,550**.
- Secretaries and administrative assistants, except legal, medical, and executive: **$46,290** median annual wage.
- Executive secretaries and executive administrative assistants: **$74,260** median.
- Legal secretaries and administrative assistants: **$54,140** median.
- Medical secretaries and administrative assistants: **$44,640** median.

BLS projects overall employment of secretaries and administrative assistants to show **little or no change (0%) from 2024 to 2034**. The general category excluding legal, medical, and executive administrative assistants (SOC 43-6014) is projected at **-2%** over the same period.

**Use control:** The Guide 06 title combines Administrative Assistant and Office Coordinator. BLS does not publish one national wage for that combined title. The 43-6014 administrative-assistant category is the closest general official anchor for ordinary administrative work; specialty categories must not be blended into one guaranteed salary.

### Employer educational assistance

IRS source: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-updates-frequently-asked-questions-about-section-127-educational-assistance-programs

IRS guidance dated April 20, 2026 states that under a qualifying Section 127 educational-assistance program, up to **$5,250** of qualifying educational assistance can be excluded from an employee's gross income for calendar years 2025 and 2026. The program must satisfy Section 127 requirements; the IRS materials include a modified sample plan and emphasize program-specific rules.

**Use control:** Do not carry forward the legacy generic claim that an employer service obligation may last up to two years. Readers must obtain the actual written employer plan/agreement and verify coverage, eligibility, repayment, service, tax, payroll-deduction, layoff, disability, transfer, retirement, and separation terms that apply to them.

## Canada — official occupational anchor

Government of Canada Job Bank:

- Administrative Assistant summary, NOC 13110: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/24789/ca
- Administrative assistants wage report: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/295982

Job Bank identifies Administrative assistants as **NOC 13110**. The national wage table reviewed for this revision reports:

- Low: **C$19.23/hour**
- Median: **C$26.44/hour**
- High: **C$36.88/hour**

The wage table was updated **November 19, 2025**, uses a **2023–2024 reference period**, and cites the Statistics Canada Labour Force Survey. Job Bank states that preparation commonly involves post-secondary education, an apprenticeship of less than two years, or more than six months of on-the-job training; exact employer and provincial requirements vary.

**Use control:** Canadian NOC mapping is a national occupational reference, not a universal credential or hiring requirement. Verify province, language, sector, software, experience, and employer expectations.

### Servicio Público de Empleo

Public employment portal: https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/

Current 2026 Servicio Público de Empleo examples show that `auxiliar administrativo` requirements vary substantially by employer and location: some listings accept secondary education and no prior experience, while others request technical education and experience. Duties commonly include records/document management, correspondence, databases, customer service, and office software.

**Use control:** These live postings are examples of title and requirement variation, not a national wage statistic. The guide should instruct readers to compare current postings by city, contract type, education, experience, software, accessibility, schedule, and duties before paying for training.

## Latin America — portability and local verification

Administrative titles, labor rules, benefits, privacy obligations, education pathways, and wages vary substantially across Latin America. Use local public employment services, public technical-training institutions, employer postings, and applicable labor/education authorities. Do not assume U.S., Canadian, or Colombian wages or credentials transfer directly to another country.

## Accessibility, privacy, cybersecurity, and ethical AI controls

The revised guide should preserve and strengthen these occupation-specific controls:

- Administrative staff may handle personnel, customer, patient, student, legal, financial, executive, visitor, scheduling, contract, and operational information.
- Use only employer-approved systems, storage, communication channels, devices, and AI tools for confidential work.
- Do not sign contracts, approve expenses, make regulated decisions, or provide legal/HR/clinical/financial advice without authority.
- Treat unexpected payment, bank-detail, identity, credential, or executive requests as high-risk until verified through approved channels.
- AI may support generic drafting, formatting, fictional practice, and workflow documentation when authorized, but must not fabricate approvals, meeting records, decisions, expenses, credentials, or confidential business records.
- Accessibility should be checked across training, testing, workplace systems, documents, meetings, communications, and work-based learning, not assumed from admission alone.
