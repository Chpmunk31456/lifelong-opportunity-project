## Occupational-risk classification

Guide 23 has elevated housing, financial, fiduciary, privacy, safety, accessibility, and jurisdiction-specific regulatory risk. Property and community-association managers may handle rents, assessments, deposits, vendor payments, owner/tenant records, maintenance, rule enforcement, notices, budgets, insurance coordination, contractor access, and resident communications.

The 2026 revision must fail closed on these distinctions:

- general property-management work versus activities that require a real-estate, strata, condominium, community-association, or other licence in a particular jurisdiction;
- administrative support versus legal advice, eviction practice, conveyancing, trust-account authority, or representation reserved to licensed professionals;
- owner/board policy implementation versus unlawful discrimination or inconsistent enforcement;
- routine maintenance coordination versus regulated electrical, gas, fire-safety, elevator, structural, environmental, or other licensed technical work;
- financial recordkeeping versus authority to hold, transfer, invest, or disburse client or association funds;
- public property information versus permission to collect, disclose, retain, or upload resident, owner, payment, access-control, or security data;
- association governance support versus powers assigned by statute, bylaws, declaration, board resolution, contract, or regulator;
- U.S., Canadian, Colombian, and other Latin American property-management systems are not interchangeable;
- AI-generated lease, enforcement, legal, safety, financial, screening, or compliance conclusions require authoritative verification and appropriately authorized review.
