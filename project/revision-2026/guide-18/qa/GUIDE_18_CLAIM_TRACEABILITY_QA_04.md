## Traceability map

### Occupation definition and titles

English Sections 2–5 trace to O*NET 43-6012.00, updated 2026. The master preserves the O*NET administrative/secretarial framing and does not inflate “may assist with legal research” into authority to provide legal advice.

### U.S. entry requirements, wages, and outlook

Sections 6, 7, and 13 trace to the current BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook profile. Controlled figures are preserved exactly:

- USD 54,140 May 2024 median annual wage for legal secretaries and administrative assistants;
- 156,300 employment in 2024;
- 147,300 projected employment in 2034;
- -6% projected change;
- -9,000 numeric change.

The master correctly distinguishes the legal-specific median from the broader all-secretaries median.

### Canada

Sections 6, 8, and 13 trace to Government of Canada Job Bank NOC 13111. Controlled national wage values are preserved exactly: C$18.75 low, C$27.47 median, C$41.72 high. Preparation language remains qualified as “usually requires” rather than universal licensing language.

### Colombia

Sections 6, 9, 10, and 18 trace to SENA Betowa's Asistencia Administrativa program. Controlled values are preserved: Titulada, 2,208 hours, Técnico, displayed minimum age 14+, and admission subject to available places/procedure. The guide does not misstate this general administrative program as a legal-practice qualification.

### Funding and workforce support

Section 11 traces to Federal Student Aid, Department of Labor American Job Centers/job-seeker resources, and the April 2026 IRS Section 127 update. The USD 5,250 figure is framed as a qualifying tax-exclusion ceiling under an employer plan, not a promised employer benefit.

### Apprenticeship

Section 12 traces to Apprenticeship.gov tools. Because the controlled research did not establish a definitive 43-6012.00 approval state suitable for a blanket claim, the guide uses a verify-the-specific-program approach and distinguishes Registered Apprenticeship from ordinary work-based learning.

### Unauthorized-practice boundary

Sections 2, 6, 9, 16, 17, and 19 reflect ABA nonlawyer-assistance guidance and make the local-jurisdiction boundary explicit. The guide does not purport to define unauthorized practice of law for every jurisdiction.

### AI and confidentiality

Section 16 reflects ABA Formal Opinion 512 guidance on generative AI in legal practice, adapted conservatively for administrative staff: employer-approved systems only, confidentiality protection, no public-tool disclosure of protected information, and authorized human review.

### Accessibility

Section 14 traces to EEOC reasonable-accommodation guidance and is expressly jurisdiction- and fact-dependent.

### Private market estimates

Section 13 labels ZipRecruiter and Indeed as private/non-government sources. ZipRecruiter Legal Administrative Assistant is recorded at USD 53,753/year and about USD 25.84/hour as of July 23, 2026. Indeed's broader Legal Assistant estimate is recorded at USD 22.92/hour, updated July 20, 2026, with an explicit adjacent-title/scope warning.
