## Editorial review

The English working master was reviewed for occupation specificity, plain-language readability, accessibility-oriented structure, scope boundaries, geographic separation, free-first learning strategy, funding cautions, responsible AI, privacy/cybersecurity, and employment-outcome disclaimers.

## Required occupation controls

PASS.

The guide consistently distinguishes legal administrative support from the unauthorized or unsupervised practice of law. It does not grant authority to give individualized legal advice, independently choose legal strategy, establish attorney-client relationships, set legal fees, represent clients, or perform other activities reserved by the applicable jurisdiction to an authorized professional.

## Geographic clarity

PASS.

United States, Canada, Colombia, and broader Latin America pathways are separated rather than blended into a single credential or licensing model.

## Education and funding language

PASS.

The guide prioritizes employer training, public resources, SENA where applicable, workforce support, and recognized low-cost education before private paid credentials. FAFSA, American Job Center/workforce support, scholarships, and Section 127 are framed as eligibility-dependent rather than guaranteed benefits.

## Wage and labor-market language

PASS.

Official BLS and Government of Canada Job Bank figures are labeled as official data. ZipRecruiter and Indeed figures are explicitly labeled private/non-government estimates with a title-scope warning for Indeed's broader “Legal Assistant” category.

## Privacy, cybersecurity, and AI

PASS.

The guide contains explicit controls for client/prospective-client information, privilege/work product, court and matter records, credentials, phishing/social engineering, file transfer, employer-approved systems, and use of AI. It prohibits placing protected matter information into unapproved/public AI tools and requires authorized human review for substantive legal output.

## Accessibility-oriented structure

PASS.

The master uses descriptive numbered headings, short paragraphs, lists, explicit warnings, verification checklists, and country labels. Critical instructions are expressed in text and do not depend on color.

## Style and claims

PASS.

The guide avoids employment, salary, funding, apprenticeship, licensing, accommodation, or credential guarantees. It also avoids claims of independent human certification, professional translation certification, accessibility certification, legal review, accreditation review, or regulator approval.

**PASS.** The English working master may proceed to claim/evidence traceability review.
