## Key occupational risk control

Because this guide concerns work in legal offices, the revision must clearly distinguish administrative support from the unauthorized practice of law. The guide must not imply that an administrative assistant can independently provide legal advice, select legal strategy, establish attorney-client relationships, sign filings reserved to authorized professionals, or perform jurisdiction-restricted legal services without appropriate supervision and authority.
