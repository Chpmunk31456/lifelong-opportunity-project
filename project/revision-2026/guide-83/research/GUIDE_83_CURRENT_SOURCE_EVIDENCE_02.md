## United States — primary benchmark

### O*NET-SOC 15-1231.00 — Computer Network Support Specialists

O*NET OnLine, updated 2026, describes this occupation as analyzing, testing, troubleshooting and evaluating existing network systems including LANs, WANs, cloud networks, servers and other data-communications networks, and performing network maintenance to minimize interruption.

Representative tasks include:

- backing up network data;
- configuring security settings/access permissions;
- analyzing network-security breaches or attempted breaches;
- identifying causes of networking problems using diagnostic tools;
- documenting network-support activity;
- configuring LAN/WAN routers and related equipment;
- installing network/security/firewall software;
- troubleshooting connectivity problems;
- providing network/connectivity support;
- evaluating network performance/availability data and disaster-recovery implications.

### Preparation and apprenticeship

O*NET classifies 15-1231.00 in **Job Zone Four — Considerable Preparation Needed**. It notes that many occupations in this zone require a four-year degree, while some do not, and that considerable related skill/experience and vocational/on-the-job preparation may be needed.

O*NET lists two approved example Registered Apprenticeship titles:

- **Cloud Support Specialist**;
- **Junior Cloud Engineer (Nof)**.

These titles are evidence that approved apprenticeship pathways exist for related work; they do not mean a current opening exists in every location.

## Canada — NOC 22220

### Computer network and web technicians

Canada Job Bank identifies **NOC 22220 — Computer network and web technicians**. The network portion establishes, operates, maintains and coordinates LANs/WANs, mainframe networks, hardware, software and related equipment, and monitors/optimizes connectivity and performance.

Job Bank's current national summary indicates **college or apprenticeship** as the usual broad preparation category.

Current requirements state:

- completion of a college or other program in computer science, network administration, web technology or a related field is usually required;
- software-vendor certification/training may be required by some employers;
- **registration with a regulatory body is required in Saskatchewan**.

The controlled guide must preserve that jurisdiction-specific caveat rather than saying the occupation is uniformly unregulated across Canada.

### Current Canadian wages

Job Bank national wages, updated **November 19, 2025**, reference period 2023–2024:

- low: **C$21.00/hour**;
- median: **C$36.00/hour**;
- high: **C$55.00/hour**.

Job prospects vary by province/territory. The guide must direct readers to the current regional Job Bank view rather than imply one national outlook applies everywhere.

## Colombia — CUOC 35130

### Técnicos en redes y tecnologías de la información

OCUPACOL maps Guide 83 directly to **CUOC 35130 — Técnicos en redes y tecnologías de la información**, competency level 3.

Relevant official occupational denominations include:

- Técnico de apoyo de red;
- Técnico de redes y sistemas informáticos;
- Técnico de sistemas en red;
- **Técnico de soporte de red informática**;
- Técnico en mantenimiento de red informática;
- Técnico en redes de computadores;
- Técnico especialista en infraestructura tecnológica.

Official functions include:

- implementing, operating, administering and troubleshooting data networks;
- installing/configuring computers, network software, operating systems and applications;
- startup/shutdown, backups and disaster-recovery operations;
- handling user requirements under procedures and current rules;
- configuring active interconnection devices;
- using information-security diagnostic tools;
- monitoring data centers;
- maintaining network infrastructure;
- documenting technology solutions and records.

OCUPACOL displays historical/derived market indicators, including a salary range, but explicitly states the data **do not have statistical representativeness** under the applied methodology and references older periods. The controlled guide therefore will **not** present that range as a representative current national Colombian salary benchmark.

## Current SENA pathways

### Instalación de redes de computadores

SENA Betowa currently lists **Instalación de redes de computadores** as:

- **Técnico**;
- **2,208 hours**;
- titulada training;
- competencies including physical data-network implementation, local wireless-network implementation, workplace safety, electrical-installation basics and information-tool use.

The listing contains specific cohort/location/registration windows; availability changes by center and intake. The guide must tell readers to verify live seats and dates.

### Gestión de redes de datos

SENA Betowa also lists **Gestión de redes de datos** as:

- **Tecnólogo**;
- **3,984 hours**;
- titulada training;
- coverage of structured cabling, data centers, wired/wireless networks and network security for IT infrastructure support.

This is a deeper pathway than the Técnico option and should not be presented as mandatory for every entry-level network-support job.

## Network-support technical boundary

The revision must clearly distinguish a network support technician from independent network architect, security architect, penetration tester or enterprise change authority.

Required boundaries:

- do not bypass access controls to troubleshoot;
- do not disable firewall/security controls without approved authority/change process;
- do not scan, probe or test systems outside assigned authorization;
- do not make production routing/switching changes without required approval/change record;
- preserve configuration backups and rollback plans where required;
- verify device/interface/VLAN/routing context before changing configuration;
- use least privilege and named accounts;
- protect credentials, keys, secrets and exported configurations;
- escalate suspected breaches rather than improvising incident response beyond role authority;
- preserve logs/evidence when required;
- never invent test results or claim restoration before validation.

## Core technical content required in the English rebuild

The controlled English master should cover, at minimum:

- OSI/TCP-IP model as a troubleshooting aid;
- IPv4/IPv6 concepts, subnetting and addressing;
- DHCP, DNS, ARP/neighbor discovery;
- switches, VLANs, trunks and spanning-tree concepts;
- routers, routing tables, default gateways and BGP awareness;
- wired/wireless connectivity;
- VPN and remote connectivity;
- firewalls/security controls at a support level;
- packet loss, latency, jitter, bandwidth and availability;
- network monitoring, logs, SNMP/telemetry concepts;
- cloud-network and hybrid-network basics;
- backups, configuration control and disaster recovery;
- ticketing, documentation and escalation;
- user communication and impact assessment;
- change windows/rollback;
- cybersecurity hygiene;
- responsible AI/automation;
- accessibility/inclusive support;
- lab/portfolio design using owned or explicitly authorized environments.

## Responsible AI and automation

AI may help summarize logs, draft documentation, explain protocols or propose troubleshooting hypotheses, but the guide must require:

- use only approved tools/data;
- no credentials/secrets/protected configs in unapproved public AI services;
- human verification of commands, configuration changes and conclusions;
- source/log traceability;
- testing in appropriate non-production or authorized environments;
- no autonomous production network change without approved authority;
- escalation when AI output conflicts with observed evidence.

NIST AI RMF and its Generative AI Profile are guidance, not substitutes for employer change/security governance.

## Accessibility

Network-support workflows and documentation should use accessible electronic-document and support practices: keyboard-accessible tools where possible, readable structure, meaningful labels, non-color-only status cues, accessible runbooks and communication alternatives. Automated accessibility checks do not constitute full legal certification.
