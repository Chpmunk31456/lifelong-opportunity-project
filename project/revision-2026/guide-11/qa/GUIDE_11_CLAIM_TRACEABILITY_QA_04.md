## Controls

- Every material numeric claim is tied to a dated/current source class.
- Official occupational values are not relabeled as coordinator-specific guaranteed pay.
- Private salary estimates are explicitly labeled non-government.
- The source does not claim a uniform Latin American credential or wage.
- Program availability and eligibility are qualified.
- The source list reproduces the material URLs needed for localization parity.
