## Traceability controls

- Private salary data is not blended with government wage statistics.
- Canada figures are identified as a Canadian benchmark and not converted into U.S. equivalents.
- Colombia program availability is not guaranteed.
- No unverified insurance license, certification, accreditation, or legal-authority claim is made.
- The statements about AI, fraud indicators, coverage, underwriting, settlement, and claims authority are framed as workflow/scope controls rather than unsupported legal conclusions.
- Time-sensitive facts carry dates or are placed in the current-source list for pre-release reverification.
