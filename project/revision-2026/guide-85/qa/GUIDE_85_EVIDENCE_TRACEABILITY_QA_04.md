## Benchmark-boundary checks

### United States — PASS

The master does not present Statisticians wages, education or outlook as a universal Data Analyst series. The limitation is stated near the opening framing and repeated in income sections.

### Canada — PASS

The master presents NOC 21223 as a comparison with stronger database/data-management scope than many general analyst jobs. It does not claim a perfect crosswalk.

### Colombia — PASS

The master uses function-based comparisons and does not manufacture a representative national salary from nonrepresentative OCUPACOL indicators.

### Non-government compensation — PASS

Indeed values are labelled as current non-government title-specific market estimates, separate from official wage statistics, with a direction to verify the live page when making compensation decisions.

## Training and funding claim controls — PASS

The master consistently treats CareerOneStop/WIOA, Apprenticeship.gov, Canada training programs, SENA Betowa and ILO/Cinterfor as locators or pathways. It does not promise:

- eligibility;
- admission;
- current seats;
- employer recognition;
- apprenticeship availability;
- funding;
- completion;
- employment outcome.

## Safety / ethics / AI traceability — PASS

The privacy, access-control, analytical-integrity and responsible-AI sections are framed as practical controls and professional boundaries. The master does not claim that NIST guidance is mandatory law or that this guide provides legal, statistical, cybersecurity or compliance certification.

## Link and source presentation — PASS
