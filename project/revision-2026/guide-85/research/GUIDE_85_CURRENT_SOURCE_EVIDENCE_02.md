## Research decision — multi-benchmark model

`Data Analyst` is a cross-cutting title. Current official systems do not provide one exact universal occupation code that covers every commercial, technical, statistical, research and business data-analyst role. Guide 85 therefore uses a transparent **multi-benchmark model**.

### Primary U.S. quantitative benchmark

**O*NET-SOC 15-2041.00 — Statisticians** is used as the primary official quantitative/statistical benchmark because:

- O*NET is updated 2026 and classifies the occupation as Bright Outlook;
- its core tasks include analyzing/interpreting data, evaluating statistical validity, preparing data, identifying trends/relationships, reporting results and preparing graphics;
- its detailed work activities explicitly include determining methods for data analysis, analyzing trends/relationships, preparing analytical reports, evaluating data quality and preparing data for analysis;
- O*NET explicitly lists approved apprenticeship titles **Data Analyst**, **Data Analyst (Nof)** and **Junior Data Analyst**.

However, this benchmark is **more statistically advanced and graduate-heavy than many commercial entry-level Data Analyst jobs**. The guide must preserve that limitation wherever its wages, outlook or preparation profile are presented.

### Adjacent U.S. benchmark

O*NET-SOC **15-2051.01 — Business Intelligence Analysts** is an adjacent benchmark for business/reporting/dashboard-oriented roles and is covered directly in Guide 84. Guide 85 may reference BI as a related pathway, but it must not silently reuse Guide 84's Data Scientists-crosswalked wage series as though it were generic Data Analyst pay.

## Canada — NOC 21223 comparison

Canada Job Bank's current title **Data Analyst - Informatics and Systems** maps to **NOC 21223 — Database analysts and data administrators**.

Job Bank states that this NOC designs, develops and administers data-management solutions and develops data-administration policy, standards and models. This is **more database/data-management oriented than many general analytical/reporting roles**, so the guide must present it as a Canadian comparison rather than a universal one-to-one match.

Current requirements:

- a bachelor's degree or college program, usually in computer science, computer engineering or mathematics, is usually required;
- computer programming and related experience is usually required;
- according to current Job Bank records, the occupation is **not regulated in Canada**.

Current national wages, updated **November 19, 2025**, reference period 2023–2024:

- low: **C$25.00/hour**;
- median: **C$40.87/hour**;
- high: **C$61.03/hour**.

Job prospects vary by province/territory.

## Colombia — function-based multi-CUOC mapping

A generic `Analista de datos` title spans multiple official CUOC contexts. Guide 85 will use function-based comparisons rather than claim one exclusive national code.

### CUOC 25210 — Diseñadores y administradores de bases de datos

This group explicitly includes the title **Analista de datos comerciales** and is appropriate when the role focuses strongly on data architecture, database structure, data administration, warehouses, quality and data-management systems.

Relevant functions include:

- designing/developing database architecture and management systems;
- analyzing, interpreting and systematizing large amounts of data using analytics methods/tools;
- developing processes for cleaning, extracting, processing, structuring, analyzing, visualizing and communicating databases;
- verifying integrity/availability and evaluating security/completeness/consistency of databases;
- designing/documenting data models and dictionaries.

### CUOC 25110 — Analistas de sistemas

This group is appropriate for **business/BI-oriented data analysis** and explicitly includes:

- Analista de analytics;
- Analista de inteligencia de negocios;
- Analista de Power BI;
- Analista de información comercial;
- Analista de procesamiento de información.

Relevant functions include integrating organizational systems/data using visualization and analytical methodologies and systematizing large datasets with analytics tools.

### CUOC 21200 — Matemáticos, actuarios y estadísticos

This group is appropriate when the role is strongly statistical/research-oriented and explicitly includes **Analista estadístico** among its denominations.

Relevant functions include applying statistical methodology, interpreting results, designing analytical methods and mathematical/statistical modeling.

## Data-analysis integrity boundaries

A Data Analyst should not:

- silently alter source records to obtain a desired result;
- remove legitimate outliers only because they weaken the story;
- hide material filters/exclusions;
- choose a denominator after seeing which one produces the preferred conclusion;
- present correlation as causation;
- present a model/forecast/AI narrative as observed fact;
- ignore missing-data or selection-bias limitations;
- bypass access controls to get data;
- publish personally sensitive or confidential data outside authorization;
- claim statistical certainty unsupported by sample/method;
- claim accounting, clinical, legal or regulatory conclusions outside assigned expertise.

Required good practice includes source traceability, reproducible logic, validation, reconciliation, documented assumptions, version/correction history and escalation of unresolved material discrepancies.

## Responsible AI and automation

AI may help draft SQL/Python/R, explain formulas, propose exploratory questions, create synthetic test data or draft non-sensitive summaries. Controls must include:

- approved tools/data only;
- no confidential data, credentials or protected extracts in unapproved public AI;
- human validation of generated code, calculations and narrative;
- testing on appropriate data;
- reconciliation to authoritative sources;
- explicit distinction between observed evidence and AI-generated interpretation;
- review for bias, data leakage, fabricated fields, unsupported causal claims and incorrect calculations;
- no autonomous publication of decision-critical results outside approved governance.

NIST AI RMF and the Generative AI Profile provide voluntary risk-management guidance and do not replace organizational data governance.

## Privacy/cybersecurity boundary

Analysts may handle employee, customer, financial, health, operational or commercially sensitive data. Required controls include least privilege, MFA where required, approved exports/storage/sharing, credential/secret protection, row-level/security-role controls, masking/aggregation where required, incident reporting and approved retention/deletion.

Having query access does not mean every field may be used or redistributed.

## Accessibility

Data reports and dashboards should consider meaningful labels, readable text/contrast, non-color-only encoding, keyboard/navigation support where platforms provide it, accessible tables/alternatives where practical, clear units and understandable descriptions. Automated checks do not prove full legal accessibility compliance.
