## Editorial review
The controlled English master was reviewed against the Guide 72 current-source evidence pack and the expanded opportunity standard.

### Occupation scope — PASS
- Uses O*NET-SOC **41-1011.00 — First-Line Supervisors of Retail Sales Workers** as the primary U.S. benchmark.
- Preserves Canada **NOC 62010 — Retail sales supervisors** and Colombia **CUOC 52220 — Supervisores de tiendas y almacenes** as comparison frameworks.
- Explains why the project title combines first-line retail supervision with merchandising coordination without claiming that every supervisor performs corporate merchandising work.
- Distinguishes team-lead, supervisor, merchandising-coordinator, and store/department-manager authority.

### Natural English and usability — PASS
- Written in direct, plain professional English with short explanatory sections, practical examples, and checklists.
- Avoids academic or legalistic framing where simpler language is sufficient.
- Defines operational concepts such as shift handoff, merchandising execution, escalation, shrink, transaction controls, and authority boundaries in context.
- Uses a realistic progression from cashier/sales/stock/customer-service roles rather than presenting private schooling as a default prerequisite.

### Labour-market claims — PASS
- Preserves current official U.S. 2025 wage benchmark: **$23.33/hour / $48,520/year**.
- Preserves U.S. 2024 employment of about **1,432,600**, projected decline of **1% or lower**, and approximately **125,100 projected openings over the 2024–2034 projection period** without converting that value into an unsupported annual claim.
- Preserves Canada Job Bank wage values **C$16.55 / C$22.00 / C$36.06 per hour** and current detailed entry requirements.
- Does not invent an official Colombia representative wage.
- Labels U.S. Indeed **$48,124/year** and Colombia Indeed **COP 1,923,218/month** as non-government market estimates with limitations.

### Education, funding, and work-based learning — PASS

- Uses SENA Betowa **Operaciones comerciales en retail**, Técnico, **2,208 hours**, as a public training pathway while warning that cohorts and seats change.
- Uses ILO/Cinterfor as a regional vocational-training discovery route rather than proof of a current free course or apprenticeship.
- Treats employer-sponsored learning, team-lead programs, cross-training, and work-based learning as employer/program dependent.
