## Editorial controls applied

The English v2 working master was reconstructed from the verified Guide 21 evidence register rather than treating the legacy publication package as automatically current.

The editorial review confirmed that the working master:

- uses plain, natural English and short, scannable sections;
- distinguishes factual property-record research from legal opinions and regulated professional authority;
- distinguishes occupational title examination from title-insurance licensing and underwriting;
- avoids implying that U.S., Canadian, Colombian, and other Latin American systems are interchangeable;
- includes current United States official wage research and separately labeled private-market estimates;
- includes Canada Job Bank occupational and wage context;
- includes a Colombia-specific SNR pathway and SENA low-cost training example;
- includes broader Latin America transferability guidance without inventing a common regional licensing model;
- includes WIOA/CareerOneStop, Apprenticeship.gov, public training, employer-paid training, reimbursement, and scholarship/funding checks;
- clearly labels training availability and employer support as items to verify rather than guarantees;
- includes privacy, information-security, data-minimization, and conservative AI safeguards;
- avoids employment, salary, licensing, accreditation, legal-review, or independent-human-certification guarantees;
- retains direct source URLs for later link QA and traceability; and
- uses UTF-8-safe punctuation and accessible heading/list structure suitable for downstream DOCX/PDF generation.

## Editorial risk checks

**Unauthorized-practice risk:** PASS. The guide repeatedly separates factual research from legal advice, ownership conclusions, lien-priority conclusions, closing/conveyancing authority, registrar functions, and insurance authority.

**Income-label risk:** PASS. Official O*NET/BLS-derived figures are separated from Indeed and ZipRecruiter estimates and no artificial blended salary is created.

**Jurisdiction-transfer risk:** PASS. Canada and Colombia are described through their own systems and the Latin America section warns against automatic cross-border transfer of authority.

**AI/privacy risk:** PASS. AI is limited to low-risk support uses; authoritative property/legal conclusions require record verification and authorized review; sensitive data use requires organizational authorization and applicable controls.

**Training/funding overclaim risk:** PASS. WIOA, apprenticeships, SENA, scholarships, public programs, and employer support are framed as pathways to investigate and verify, not guaranteed benefits.
