## Traceability model

Every material occupational, compensation, education, safety, sampling-quality, funding, jurisdiction, and pathway claim in the English master is traceable to the controlled research intake or is explicitly framed as general career-planning guidance rather than a factual regulatory claim.

The master carries 26 direct authoritative verification URLs, grouped by jurisdiction and purpose. Source links are retained directly in the reader-facing master so a reader can recheck changing requirements.

## Claim-to-source traceability

### United States occupational evidence

- **SOC 19-4042, typical education, wage, employment, outlook, openings, work setting, sample-tracking duties, and hazardous-waste-training caveat:** U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Environmental Science and Protection Technicians.
- **O*NET 19-4042.00, field/laboratory work under specialist direction, related titles and task context:** O*NET OnLine.
- **Adjacent environmental-engineering-technician duties:** BLS Environmental Engineering Technologists and Technicians; kept separate from the primary SOC.

### United States safety controls

- **Conditional hazardous-waste operations / emergency-response scope:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120.
- **Employer hazard assessment and PPE selection:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132.
- **Worksite-specific respiratory-protection program, medical evaluation, fit testing, selection, training and maintenance:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134.
- **Chemical labels, SDS and training:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1200.
- **Permit-required confined-space evaluation, program, roles, monitoring, communication and rescue framework:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146.

The master uses these sources to define authorization boundaries and escalation—not to teach hazardous procedures.

### Environmental quality and sample integrity

- **Field-sampling quality-system principles:** EPA Field Sampling Quality Control.
- **Field records, unique sample identity, date/time/location and project-specific collection planning:** EPA Field Sample Collection.
- **Sample handling and custody concepts:** EPA Collecting and Handling Samples.

The EPA fish/shellfish pages are explicitly fenced as program-specific examples of quality and custody principles. Their collection techniques are not generalized to unrelated matrices or projects.

### United States funding

- **FAFSA as a free application route that may connect eligible learners/programs to federal grants, work-study and loans:** Federal Student Aid 2026–27 FAFSA guidance.

The master does not claim that a private safety class, short sampling course, employer training, or vendor certificate is automatically federal-aid eligible.

### Canada

- **Environmental Technician search-title mapping and national outlook context:** Government of Canada Job Bank summary.
- **CAD $22.00 / $33.89 / $51.10 hourly wage indicators:** Job Bank wages.
- **NOC 22300 education, supervised-experience and provincial-title/certification context:** Job Bank requirements.
- **Canada Student Grants and Loans plus designated-program/institution verification:** Canada.ca student-aid pages.

The Canadian mapping is explicitly described as broader than U.S. SOC 19-4042 and not an exact crosswalk.

## Assurance and fail-closed controls

The master clearly separates:

- occupational task descriptions from authorization;
- sample collection from compliance authority;
- education from legal qualification;
- safety training from site authorization;
- public program profiles from live availability;
- AI assistance from controlled environmental data; and
- national labor-market statistics from individual outcomes.

A claim that cannot be traced to the research intake or current official source is not accepted for English Source Freeze.
