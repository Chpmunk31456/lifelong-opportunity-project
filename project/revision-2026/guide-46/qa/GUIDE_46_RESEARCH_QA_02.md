## Safety and scope verification

The intake correctly treats ordinary field work as potentially involving travel/driving, outdoor weather, prolonged standing, lifting/carrying equipment, industrial or contaminated locations, sample handling, monitoring equipment, and PPE. It also establishes conditional controls for hazardous-waste operations, chemical hazards, respiratory protection, and permit-required confined spaces.

HAZWOPER is not presented as universally required. Respirator selection is not delegated to the worker or guide. Confined-space entry and rescue are not taught. Employer/site hazard assessment, authorization, training, approved procedures, and qualified roles remain controlling.

The intake does not provide hazardous step-by-step instructions for spill response, hazardous-material handling, respirator selection, decontamination chemistry, confined-space entry, excavation entry, intrusive sampling, energized work, or emergency rescue.

## Environmental sampling and data-integrity verification

The intake establishes that approved project plans, methods, SOPs, laboratory/client instructions, and applicable regulation control actual sampling. EPA resources are used to support defensible field-record, sample-identification, chain-of-custody, and quality-control principles without turning a program-specific procedure into a universal field method.

Controlled Version 2 must fail closed on fabricated, backfilled, silently normalized, or altered environmental data. AI may not invent observations, measurements, sample IDs, times, locations, custody transfers, calibration records, photographs, laboratory results, hazard classifications, or compliance conclusions.
