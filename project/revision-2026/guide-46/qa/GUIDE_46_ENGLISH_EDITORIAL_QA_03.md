## Safety and authorization review

The master uses career-level safety guidance rather than hazardous procedures. It:

- makes HAZWOPER conditional rather than universal;
- places PPE selection under employer hazard assessment;
- places required respirator use under a written, worksite-specific respiratory-protection program;
- prohibits self-selection of respirators, cartridges, filters, protection factors, or change schedules;
- treats confined spaces, excavations, work at height, water exposure, traffic control, energized equipment, intrusive work, hazardous-material disturbance, and emergency response as specialized controlled work;
- never teaches confined-space entry, rescue, atmospheric authorization, hazardous-material response, decontamination chemistry, or disposal;
- requires stop-and-escalate action for uncertain substances, atmospheres, procedures, sample identity, equipment state, PPE, weather, traffic, access, communications, or authorization; and
- preserves employer/project/site/regulatory control over actual field work.

## Sampling and environmental-data-integrity review

The master correctly treats sampling as project-controlled work and requires verification of the approved plan/SOP/method, matrix, location, laboratory instructions, containers, preservation, holding time, quality-control requirements, instrument controls, sample IDs, custody process, packaging, shipping, and deviation authority before field collection.

It does not convert EPA program-specific collection pages into universal sampling methods.

The master explicitly protects:

- contemporaneous observations and measurements;
- sample IDs, seals, timestamps, locations, custody records, and field forms;
- instrument identification and required calibration/verification records;
- raw anomalous observations;
- documented correction processes; and
- separation of data collection from legal/regulatory compliance authority.

It prohibits fabrication, backfilling, concealment, silent normalization, substitution of plausible values, invented calibration, and unauthorized release or certification of results.

## Responsible-AI, privacy, and cybersecurity review

The AI section permits only bounded low-risk uses such as study support, organization, clearly fictional practice, résumé drafting from supplied facts, and public-source research organization.

AI is explicitly prohibited from inventing or changing real field observations, measurements, sample identifiers, timestamps, locations, custody events, calibration results, photographs, laboratory results, safety determinations, compliance conclusions, sampling methods, preservatives, holding times, analytical methods, decontamination chemistry, PPE, respirators, confined-space classifications, or emergency responses.

Sensitive site coordinates, photographs, access details, chain-of-custody forms, laboratory results, client data, investigation data, credentials, critical-infrastructure information, and regulated records are fenced from unauthorized AI/cloud use.
