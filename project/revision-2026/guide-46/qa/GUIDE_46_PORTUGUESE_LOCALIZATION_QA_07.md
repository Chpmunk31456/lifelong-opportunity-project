## Safety and authorization parity

The Portuguese edition does not convert occupational tasks into authorization. It preserves that:

- HAZWOPER is conditional, not universal;
- PPE selection belongs to the employer's hazard assessment;
- required respirator use belongs to a written worksite-specific respiratory-protection program;
- confined-space entry/rescue, hazardous-material response, excavation entry, energized work, work at height, traffic control, work near/on water and emergency response are specialized controlled work;
- actual sampling follows the approved project plan/SOP/method, laboratory and client requirements, and applicable rules; and
- unknown hazards, sample conflicts, equipment/calibration uncertainty, anomalous results, missing communications or work outside authorization require stop-and-escalate action.

No hazardous procedural instruction was added in localization.

## Environmental-data and AI parity

The Portuguese edition preserves the requirement to record only actual observations and measurements, protect sample identity and chain of custody, use approved correction procedures, preserve anomalous raw observations, and never invent calibration or compliance conclusions.

Responsible-AI restrictions remain intact. AI may not fabricate, backfill, normalize or alter real environmental field data; select real sampling methods, preservation, analytical methods, respirators or PPE; determine safety/compliance; replace approved project/safety/laboratory systems; or expose sensitive client, site, laboratory, geolocation or investigation information to an unauthorized service.
