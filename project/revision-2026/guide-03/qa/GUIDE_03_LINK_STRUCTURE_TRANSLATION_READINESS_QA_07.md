## Translation-readiness controls

**PASS — source roles are explicit.** Official government/occupational sources, private credential bodies, and non-government market evidence are labeled according to role.

**PASS — no ambiguous universal credential claim.** Translation can preserve the distinction between employer preference, credential-body rules, and legal requirements.

**PASS — country boundaries are explicit.** U.S. coding/privacy/credential terminology is not presented as automatically transferable to Canada, Colombia, or the rest of Latin America.

**PASS — protected terms can remain intact where appropriate.** Acronyms and formal names such as BLS, CMS, HIPAA, ICD-10-CM, ICD-10-PCS, CPT, HCPCS, AHIMA, AAPC, WIOA, NOC, SENA and Registered Apprenticeship can be retained with explanatory surrounding text in translation.

**PASS — URLs are language-neutral controls.** The same source URLs can be carried into translations unless an authoritative localized source is intentionally substituted and documented.

**PASS — anti-guarantee language is explicit.** Translation must preserve the guide’s statements that wages, employment, funding, credential acceptance, apprenticeship availability, remote work, and international equivalence are not guaranteed.
