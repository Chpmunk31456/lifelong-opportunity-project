### CMS HCPCS — PASS

Source: https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/healthcare-common-procedure-system

Revalidated distinctions:

- HCPCS has Level I and Level II subsystems.

Control: preserve the guide’s beginner-level distinction among ICD-10-CM/PCS, CPT and HCPCS; do not reproduce proprietary codebook content.
