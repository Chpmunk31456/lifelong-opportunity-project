## Gate purpose

Review the current English working master for natural language, terminology consistency, scope boundaries, reader clarity, translation readiness, and avoidable ambiguity after current-source revalidation. This gate does not represent independent human certification, legal review, coding certification, accessibility certification, professional translation certification, or final publication approval.

## Editorial review performed

The full working master was reviewed section by section against the legacy-source reconciliation and current-source controls. The review focused on:

- consistent separation of medical billing, medical coding, health-information, records, claims, denial and revenue-cycle work;
- avoidance of language that could imply a universal license, credential, wage, apprenticeship, remote-work right, or country-to-country equivalence;
- distinction among ICD-10-CM, ICD-10-PCS, CPT and HCPCS Level II;
- accurate use of HIPAA minimum-necessary and role-based-access concepts without implying universal HIPAA applicability;
- separation of official occupational evidence from non-government exact-title market evidence;
- natural, direct English suitable for later neutral Latin American Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese translation;
- clear anti-fraud, documentation-integrity, privacy, security and responsible-AI boundaries; and
- consistent use of verification language before readers spend money, accept repayment obligations, or rely on credentials.

## Corrections applied

Minor terminology and readability corrections were applied directly to the working master without changing verified facts or numerical values:

1. Replaced an awkward `coding-compliance` construction with `coding and compliance` in the general-advice disclaimer.
2. Changed `Do not diagnose a patient` to `Do not diagnose patients` for natural general-scope wording while retaining the qualification that separately qualified and authorized professionals may have different authority.
3. Replaced the nonstandard phrase `least-necessary-access rules` with `minimum-necessary access rules`, consistent with the HHS terminology already used elsewhere in the guide.
4. Removed an unnecessary hyphen in one introductory use of `health information` while retaining compound-modifier hyphenation where it improves readability.

## Terminology controls

**PASS — occupational scope.** The master does not collapse billing, coding, health-information management, records work, denial management and revenue-cycle work into one universal occupation.

**PASS — credentials.** AHIMA and AAPC credentials remain clearly identified as private professional credentials rather than government licenses, and the guide directs readers to verify employer demand and current exam rules before paying.

**PASS — coding systems.** ICD-10-CM/PCS, CPT and HCPCS Level II remain distinct and the master explicitly requires effective-date verification.

**PASS — privacy/security.** HIPAA language remains scoped to covered entities/certain business associates and appropriate payment/health-care-operations contexts; state law, contracts and other privacy obligations are acknowledged.

**PASS — automation/AI.** AI-assisted coding or billing output is framed as decision support requiring verification and accountable human judgment, not autonomous authority.

**PASS — international terminology.** Canada is presented through role-dependent NOC comparators, while Colombia/LATAM material avoids importing U.S. HIPAA, Medicare/Medicaid, CPT/HCPCS or private-credential assumptions into local employment.

## Natural-language result

The current English master reads as a practical career-navigation guide rather than a regulatory manual, sales document, certification advertisement, or guaranteed-career promise. Lists and tables are used where they improve scanability; warnings are specific enough to be actionable without becoming alarmist or repetitive.

No remaining wording issue identified in this review is material enough to block the next gate.

## Controlled decision

**PASS — English editorial, terminology and natural-language QA.** The English working master is suitable to proceed to complete link/structure/translation-readiness checks.

The master is not yet frozen for translation or publication. Link reachability, structural consistency, source-list completeness and translation-readiness controls remain required before the English freeze.
