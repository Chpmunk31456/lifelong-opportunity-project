## Identity, access, security, and privacy parity — PASS

All three editions preserve that support staff must not:

- reset accounts without required identity verification;
- bypass MFA;
- grant privilege without approval;
- disable security controls merely to close a ticket;
- store prohibited credentials/secrets in tickets;
- use unapproved remote-support tools;
- browse unrelated user information;
- make independent firewall/network/security-policy changes outside assigned authority;
- hide suspected compromise or data exposure.

All editions preserve escalation for phishing, malware, suspected compromise, data exposure, privileged-access requests, and other security-relevant conditions.

## Accessibility parity — PASS

All editions protect user accessibility settings and assistive technologies from being disabled merely because they are unfamiliar. Section508.gov/WCAG are framed as educational references, not proof of legal accessibility certification.

## Responsible-AI parity — PASS

All editions preserve:

- approved low-risk AI assistance only where policy allows;
- mandatory human verification before consequential commands or system changes;
- no protected credentials, tokens, logs, screenshots, internal architecture, incident evidence, or private user data in unapproved AI services;
- no AI-generated bypass of change/security/escalation controls;
- NIST AI RMF/Generative AI Profile as voluntary references rather than certification.
