## Traceability controls

### U.S. occupation and compensation

- BLS occupational family remains Welders, Cutters, Solderers, and Brazers.
- $51,000 annual and $24.52 hourly May 2024 medians are attributed to BLS and identified as national combined-occupation statistics.
- 2% projected growth for 2024–2034 and about 45,600 annual openings are preserved with replacement-needs context.
- The guide does not convert these figures into a promised local, apprentice, fabrication-technician, union, or project wage.
- Salary.com's July 1, 2026 market estimate is explicitly labeled non-government and kept methodologically separate from BLS.

### Safety and work boundaries

- OSHA 29 CFR 1910 Subpart Q is cited as U.S. general-industry welding/cutting/brazing authority, not international law.
- 1910.252 fire prevention, ventilation, PPE, and confined-space boundaries are reflected.
- 1910.253 fuel-gas/oxygen explosion risk is reflected without procedural improvisation.
- 1910.254 instruction/qualification language is reflected for arc-welding equipment.
- The candidate does not provide unsupervised beginner instructions for live arcs, fuel gas, pressure cylinders, confined spaces, hazardous coatings/metals, elevated work, energized systems, or other high-risk work.
- AI is explicitly barred from substituting for a WPS/PQR/WPQ, engineering drawing, code, acceptance criterion, manufacturer instruction, respiratory-protection program, permit, or site safety procedure.

### Apprenticeship and U.S. public funding

- Apprenticeship.gov is described as a locator/verification starting point, not proof that every “apprentice” vacancy is a Registered Apprenticeship Program.
- WIOA/American Job Center support is described as conditional on local eligibility, provider/program status, approvals, funding, and covered-cost rules.
- Employer-paid training, paid trainee/helper routes, public technical/community-college options, scholarships, and reimbursement are presented as routes to investigate rather than guaranteed benefits.

### AWS credential

- AWS Certified Welder is described as a performance-based industry certification using AWS Accredited Testing Facilities.
- The candidate preserves scope dependence by procedure, process, material, position, and applicable code/standard.
- No universal government-license, universal process qualification, or automatic employer acceptance claim is made.

### Canada

- Welder is associated with Red Seal and NOC 72106.
- Provincial/territorial apprenticeship and certification administration is explicit.
- Job Bank's C$22.00 low / C$30.00 median / C$47.00 high national wage references are identified as national reference values, not guaranteed offers.
- Canada Apprentice Loan is represented as up to C$4,000 in interest-free loans per period of technical training for eligible apprentices, with Quebec alternate support noted.
- Closed former federal apprenticeship grants are not presented as current open cash grants.

### Colombia and Latin America

- SENA Betowa is used as a dynamic training locator; no cohort is promised to remain open.
- SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo is used as a dynamic public vacancy locator; individual postings are not treated as national wage benchmarks.
- Cross-border use of AWS, OSHA, Red Seal, or SENA is explicitly bounded by local law, project, employer, code, and qualification requirements.
- Latin America guidance requires country-specific public vocational, labor, safety, technical, and employment verification.
