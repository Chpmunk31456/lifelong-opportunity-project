## Editorial controls checked

- English v2.0 is a full controlled reconstruction rather than a cosmetic legacy edit.
- Occupational scope distinguishes welder, fabrication technician, fitter-welder, helper/trainee, and welding-operator roles rather than treating them as universally equivalent.
- Safety-critical boundaries are explicit for hot work, fire/explosion, welding fumes and gases, unknown coatings/base metals, compressed cylinders, electrical shock, arc radiation, confined spaces, elevated/energized work, pressure equipment, and stop/escalate conditions.
- OSHA references are bounded to U.S. general-industry welding/cutting/brazing requirements and are not presented as universal international law.
- BLS official pay, growth, and openings figures are labeled national combined-occupation statistics rather than guaranteed welder-only, fabrication-technician, local, union, apprentice, or project wages.

- WIOA/American Job Center language is conditional and requires verification of local eligibility, provider status, approvals, and covered costs before enrollment.

- AWS Certified Welder is correctly presented as a performance-based industry credential administered through AWS Accredited Testing Facilities, with procedure/process/material/position/code scope limits; it is not represented as a universal government license.
- Canada coverage distinguishes NOC 72106, Job Bank national wage references, Red Seal Welder, province/territory apprenticeship administration, Canada Apprentice Loan, and current-support verification.
- Colombia coverage uses SENA Betowa and Agencia Pública de Empleo as dynamic public training/employment locators and does not represent a particular cohort or vacancy as permanently available.
- Latin America guidance requires country-specific legal, safety, technical-education, qualification, and public-employment verification and does not transplant U.S., Canadian, Colombian, or AWS rules across jurisdictions.

- Resume/portfolio guidance prohibits fabricated credentials, false qualification claims, unsafe demonstrations, and disclosure of employer/customer-sensitive information.
- The 90-day plan, school-evaluation checklist, decision checklist, and stop/escalate rules are actionable, free-first, and do not promise employment or credential attainment.
- Language was checked for spelling, grammar, terminology consistency, natural human readability, scannable structure, plain-language safety warnings, and encoding-safe punctuation.
- Markdown structure is coherent and important safety guidance is text-based rather than dependent on color, graphics, or inaccessible visual cues.
