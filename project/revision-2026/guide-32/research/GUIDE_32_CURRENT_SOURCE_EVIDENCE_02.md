## United States — occupation, pay, outlook, and entry routes

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — Occupational Outlook Handbook

Source: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/welders-cutters-solderers-and-brazers.htm

Current controlled facts:

- BLS groups welders with cutters, solderers, and brazers for this federal occupational profile.
- Median annual wage: **$51,000 in May 2024**.
- Median hourly wage displayed by BLS: **$24.52**.
- Employment outlook: **2% growth from 2024 to 2034**.
- BLS projects about **45,600 openings per year**, on average, over the decade, primarily from replacement needs.
- Typical entry education is a **high school diploma or equivalent**, combined with technical and on-the-job training.
- BLS explicitly identifies vocational-technical institutes, community colleges, private welding schools, military training, and employer-based apprenticeship as possible pathways.

Controlled use: These are national occupational statistics, not a promise of local wages, employment, or eligibility. “Fabrication technician” can overlap other production or metalworking occupations and should not be represented as identical to the BLS welding group in every job.

## United States — safety boundaries

### OSHA 29 CFR 1910 Subpart Q — Welding, Cutting and Brazing

Sources:

- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910SubpartQ
- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.252
- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.253
- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.254/

Controlled facts and drafting requirements:

- General-industry welding/cutting/brazing is addressed in OSHA Subpart Q; additional standards can apply by industry and hazard.
- OSHA 1910.252 addresses fire prevention, personal protective equipment, ventilation, and confined-space precautions.
- OSHA 1910.253 addresses oxygen-fuel gas welding and cutting and explicitly identifies explosion risk from unsafe fuel-gas/oxygen mixtures.
- OSHA 1910.254 requires workers designated to operate arc-welding equipment to be properly instructed and qualified for that equipment.

Editorial boundary: the guide must not teach unsupervised hot work as a beginner exercise. Practice involving live arcs, fuel gases, pressure cylinders, confined spaces, hazardous coatings/metals, elevated work, or energized equipment belongs under competent instruction, employer/site procedures, hazard assessment, and applicable law. AI output must not substitute for a welding procedure specification (WPS), engineering drawing, code, permit, manufacturer instruction, respiratory-protection program, or site safety procedure.

## Industry credential — American Welding Society

### AWS Certified Welder

Source: https://www.aws.org/Certification-and-Education/Professional-Certification/Certified-Welder-Program/

Controlled facts:

- AWS describes its Certified Welder credential as a performance-based industry certification administered through AWS Accredited Testing Facilities.
- The program has no universal prerequisite course or minimum experience requirement; the candidate must successfully complete the applicable performance qualification test.
- Testing is procedure-, process-, material-, position-, and code/standard-dependent.
- Certification maintenance/continuity requirements apply.

Drafting boundary: AWS certification is an **industry credential**, not a universal government license to perform any welding work. Employers, contracts, codes, owners, projects, jurisdictions, and specific welding procedures may impose additional or different qualification requirements.

## Canada — occupation, trade standard, wages, and apprenticeship

### Red Seal — Welder

Sources:

- https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/welder.shtml
- https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/weld/overview.shtml

Controlled facts:

- Welder is a Red Seal trade associated with **NOC 72106**.
- The Red Seal Occupational Standard describes the national trade standard and supports Red Seal examination content.
- The trade page lists participating provinces and territories, but legal apprenticeship/certification rules remain provincial/territorial.

Drafting rule: direct readers to the appropriate provincial or territorial apprenticeship/certification authority for legal entry, apprenticeship registration, qualification, exam, and certification requirements.

### Government of Canada Job Bank — wages

Source: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/23308

Controlled facts from the current national wage page:

- Occupation shown: welder operator / welders and related machine operators, NOC 72106.
- Canada hourly wage range shown: **$22.00 low / $30.00 median / $47.00 high**.
- Wage page says data were updated November 19, 2025, with reference periods identified on the page.

Controlled use: present as Job Bank national wage information, not a guaranteed wage or a substitute for regional/collective-agreement/employer data.

### Canada — apprentice support

Sources:

- https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/training/support-skilled-trades-apprentices/become-apprentice.html
- https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/training/support-skilled-trades-apprentices/loan.html
- https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/training/support-skilled-trades-apprentices/funding-opportunities.html

Controlled facts:

- Apprentices register their training agreement with the applicable provincial/territorial authority.
- The Canada Apprentice Loan currently offers up to **$4,000 in interest-free loans per period of technical training** for eligible apprentices in designated Red Seal trades; Quebec has alternate support.
- Other current federal pages direct apprentices to Employment Insurance during eligible technical training and additional skilled-trades supports.
- Former Apprenticeship Incentive and Completion Grants closed; they must not be presented as currently open cash grants.

## Colombia — public training and employment pathways

Source example: https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/aplicacion-de-soldadura-smaw-en-aceros-al-carbono?modality=P&offertype=company

Controlled facts:

### SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo (APE)

Source: https://agenciapublicadeempleo.sena.edu.co/spe-web/spe/public/buscadorVacante?solicitudId=soldador

Controlled facts:

- APE publishes welding-related vacancies in Colombia with employer-defined titles, experience requirements, locations, contract types, and salary bands when supplied.
- Examples visible in 2026 include general welder, TIG welder, welding helper, fitter-welder/pailero-soldador, pipe welder, and specialized welding titles.

Controlled use: use APE as a free public employment-service locator and as evidence that welding titles/requirements vary. Individual vacancy pay is not a national salary benchmark and may expire quickly.
