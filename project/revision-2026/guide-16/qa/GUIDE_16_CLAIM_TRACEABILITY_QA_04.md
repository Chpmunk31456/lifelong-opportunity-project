## Traceability controls

- Mortgage licensing statements are limited to current CFPB Regulation H / S.A.F.E.-Act minimum standards and examples; the guide directs readers to verify current state/NMLS/employer rules for their actual role.
- Private salary data is not blended with BLS official statistics.
- Canada NOC-level regulatory information is not generalized to every mortgage/credit activity.
- The SENA program is presented as a broad financial-operations pathway, not a loan-processing or MLO license.
- Automation, privacy, fraud, accessibility and AI statements are role controls rather than unsupported predictions or legal determinations.
- No unsupported guarantee of credit authority, licensing, accreditation, employment or earnings is present.
