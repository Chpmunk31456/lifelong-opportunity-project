## Controlled occupation mapping

`Case Management Assistant and Resource Navigator` is treated as a support-level/navigation role, not as a universal regulated occupation with one global credential. The closest current U.S. federal occupational family is **Social and Human Service Assistants, O*NET-SOC 21-1093.00**.

O*NET (updated 2026) describes this occupation as assisting social and human-service providers, helping clients identify and obtain benefits and community services, preparing routine information, making referrals, maintaining records, and working with supervisors and other professionals. This supports the guide's resource-navigation and case-assistance framing while requiring an explicit boundary against independent licensed, clinical, legal, benefits-adjudication, or emergency authority.

## Scope and professional-boundary control

The controlled guide must not imply that an assistant/resource navigator may independently:

- diagnose or treat mental-health, substance-use, developmental, or medical conditions;
- provide psychotherapy or clinical counseling without the required credential and authorization;
- make legal determinations or provide legal advice;
- adjudicate government-benefit eligibility when that authority belongs to an agency or authorized official;
- make independent safeguarding, mandatory-reporting, or emergency-command decisions outside applicable law and employer protocol;
- represent themselves as a licensed social worker, psychologist, counselor, attorney, nurse, physician, or other protected professional title without that credential; or
- override supervisors, care teams, or agency procedures because a client situation appears urgent.

The guide should frame the role around information gathering, service navigation, routine documentation, referral follow-through, logistical coordination, and escalation under supervision.

## Canada — occupational comparison

Government of Canada Job Bank maps the broad comparison to **Social and community service workers, NOC 42201**.

Current Job Bank requirements state that completion of a college or university program in social work, child and youth care, psychology, or another social-science or health-related discipline is usually required, while previous social-service experience as a volunteer or support worker may replace formal education for some occupations in the group.

This is a broad comparison rather than proof that every `resource navigator` or `case management assistant` position carries the same provincial credential requirements. The guide must instruct readers to verify the exact title, province, employer, and any protected-title or registration rules before paying for training.

## Colombia — occupational comparison

SENA's Clasificación Nacional de Ocupaciones identifies **CNO 4211 — Asistentes en servicios social y comunitario**, under social sciences, education, government services, and religion. This is the closest controlled occupational comparison for support-level community/social-service navigation work.

The guide must distinguish this occupational family from regulated professional practice and should direct readers to verify actual employer duties, local requirements, and current SENA/Agencia Pública de Empleo pathways before enrolling.

## Privacy, safeguarding, and AI controls

Case-assistance and navigation work may involve highly sensitive client information. The controlled English master must require:

- minimum-necessary collection and access;
- approved case-management systems and channels;
- identity verification before disclosure;
- role-based access and multifactor authentication where required;
- objective, factual, proportionate documentation;
- escalation of abuse, neglect, exploitation, self-harm, violence, trafficking, medical danger, or other urgent concerns according to current law and employer protocol; and
- no entry of identifiable client records or protected information into public AI tools unless explicitly authorized with required privacy/security controls.

AI may assist with generic study, fictional practice, or non-sensitive organization, but must not replace professional judgment, crisis assessment, legal advice, benefits adjudication, clinical diagnosis, safeguarding decisions, or mandated-reporting decisions.
