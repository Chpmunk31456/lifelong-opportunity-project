## Controlled occupation mapping

`Case Management Assistant and Resource Navigator` is treated as a support-level/navigation role, not as a universal regulated occupation with one global credential. Titles may include case management assistant, case aide, resource navigator, community navigator, benefits/resource navigator, social-services assistant, community-support worker, outreach assistant, or related support titles. Actual duties and credential rules vary by employer and jurisdiction.

The closest current U.S. federal occupational family is **Social and Human Service Assistants, O*NET-SOC 21-1093.00**. O*NET describes this occupation as assisting social and human-service providers, helping clients identify and obtain benefits and community services, preparing routine information, making referrals, maintaining records, and working with supervisors and other professionals. Current reported job titles include Social Services Aide, Social Services Assistant, Social Work Assistant, Advocate, Clinical Assistant, and Residential Care Assistant.

This mapping supports the guide's resource-navigation and case-assistance framing while requiring an explicit boundary against independent licensed, clinical, legal, benefits-adjudication, safeguarding, or emergency authority.

### Free-first training and workforce funding

There is no single nationally mandatory private `case management assistant` or `resource navigator` certificate for the mapped federal occupation. A lower-risk strategy is to compare current local postings first, then use employer onboarding, supervised support experience, community-college/public-provider coursework, and workforce-funded training where appropriate.

CareerOneStop, sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor, provides:

- an **American Job Center** network for employment and training assistance;
- a **WIOA-Eligible Training Program Finder** for people who qualify for Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act training services; and
- training, scholarship, certification, license, and apprenticeship search tools.

WIOA eligibility, approved providers, local priorities, and available funds vary by area. WIOA support must be described as **conditional and not guaranteed**.

Registered Apprenticeship opportunities may exist in selected human-service or adjacent occupations, but availability is employer- and location-specific. Users should search Apprenticeship.gov for current openings rather than assume a program exists locally.

## Canada

### Occupational comparison and requirements

The closest broad Canadian comparison is **Social and community service workers, NOC 42201**. Job Bank states that these workers administer and implement social-assistance and community-service programs and assist clients with personal and social problems. This is a broad comparison, not proof that every `resource navigator` or `case management assistant` role has identical requirements.

Current Job Bank requirements state that completion of a college or university program in social work, child and youth care, psychology, or another social-science or health-related discipline is usually required. Previous work experience in a social-service environment as a volunteer or support worker may replace formal education requirements for some occupations in the unit group. Some social-service-worker titles may require membership or registration with a provincial regulatory body.

The guide must instruct readers to verify the exact title, province/territory, employer requirements, and any protected-title or registration rules before paying for training.

### Official compensation

Government of Canada Job Bank reports national NOC 42201 wages, updated **November 19, 2025**, of approximately:

- **CAD $19.00/hour low**
- **CAD $26.00/hour median**
- **CAD $36.06/hour high**

The underlying national wage reference period is 2023–2024 Labour Force Survey data. Provincial and regional wages vary materially.

- Job Bank — NOC 42201 requirements: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/5112/ca

### Training and funding control

Canada does not have one national credential or funding program that applies to every NOC 42201 role. Candidates should compare public college/university programs, provincial/territorial employment services, student-aid options where applicable, employer-supported training, and any title-specific regulatory requirements. Eligibility for public funding must be verified with the responsible government program before financial commitments are made.

A current SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo posting for **Gestor comunitario** includes activities directly relevant to Guide 60: interviewing people and families to identify needs, helping users identify options and action plans, connecting them with legal, medical, financial, housing, employment, transport, and other referral resources, and conducting social-service activities under professional supervision. ## Scope, safeguarding, and ethical controls

The controlled English master must state that a Case Management Assistant/Resource Navigator may support information gathering, service navigation, routine documentation, referral coordination, appointment/logistics support, follow-up, and escalation under employer procedures, but must not independently:

- diagnose or treat mental-health, substance-use, developmental, or medical conditions;
- provide psychotherapy or clinical counseling without the required credential and authorization;
- make legal determinations or provide legal advice;
- adjudicate government-benefit eligibility when that authority belongs to an agency or authorized official;
- promise housing, financial assistance, immigration outcomes, disability benefits, treatment outcomes, or emergency aid that the worker does not control;
- make safeguarding, mandatory-reporting, or emergency-command decisions outside applicable law and employer protocol;
- represent themselves as a licensed social worker, psychologist, counselor, attorney, nurse, physician, certified case manager, or another protected/credentialed professional without actually holding that credential; or
- override supervisors, care teams, agency protocols, or legally designated authorities because a client situation appears urgent.

Workers should use approved crisis/escalation pathways for abuse, neglect, exploitation, self-harm risk, violence risk, trafficking, homelessness emergencies, medical danger, or other urgent concerns.

## Privacy, documentation, and cybersecurity controls

Case-assistance and navigation work may involve highly sensitive client information. Version 2 must require:

- collection and access limited to the authorized purpose and minimum necessary information;
- approved case-management systems, devices, and communication channels;
- identity verification before disclosure;
- role-based access and multifactor authentication where required;
- factual, relevant, respectful, proportionate documentation that separates observation, client report, and professional conclusion;
- secure handling of names, addresses, dates of birth, health/mental-health data, disability information, immigration details, benefits records, housing information, family data, credentials, and crisis information; and
- prompt incident reporting under employer policy.

## AI-use boundary

AI may assist with generic study questions, fictional role-play, plain-language explanations of non-sensitive terminology, or drafts of generic checklists. It must **not** replace professional judgment, crisis assessment, benefits-eligibility decisions, legal advice, clinical diagnosis, safeguarding decisions, mandated-reporting decisions, or employer-approved case-management procedures.

Workers must not paste identifiable client records, case histories, medical/mental-health information, immigration details, addresses, benefits information, credentials, case notes, or other protected data into public AI tools without explicit organizational authorization and the required privacy/security controls.

AI-generated resource information, policy summaries, contact information, eligibility rules, and legal statements must be verified before use because services and rules can change.

## Research decision

**Research: PASS.** The evidence is sufficient for controlled English Version 2 reconstruction provided the guide:

- preserves the support-level/resource-navigation boundary;
- distinguishes the broad occupational mappings from protected or regulated professional titles;
- keeps official wages separate from private title-specific estimates;
- avoids unsupported national Colombian wage claims;
- treats WIOA, apprenticeship, training, and public-program availability as conditional rather than guaranteed;
- preserves crisis/safeguarding, privacy, cybersecurity, and AI-use controls; and
- directs readers to recheck current local rules, employers, providers, and official sources before acting.

This is internal source-controlled work. It does not claim independent human certification, professional accreditation, certified translation, legal review, clinical review, licensed social-work review, financial advice, benefits-program approval, or guaranteed employment outcomes.
