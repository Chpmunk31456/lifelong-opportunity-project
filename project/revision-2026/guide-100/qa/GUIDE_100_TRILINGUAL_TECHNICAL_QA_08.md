**PASS — U.S. labour-market values.** All editions preserve the 2025 O*NET/BLS-linked combined-series median of US$30.26/hour and US$62,930/year, approximately 351,200 employment in 2024, approximately 1%–2% projected 2024–2034 growth, approximately 22,600 annual openings and about 83% employment in health care/social assistance. All editions explain title sensitivity/noise.

**PASS — CLIA parity.** CLIA applicability, waived/moderate/high-complexity concepts, nonwaived quality requirements, personnel-qualification caveats and the instruction to verify current CMS/CDC/eCFR plus state/employer requirements remain materially aligned. **PASS — quality-system parity.** Quality control, calibration, quality assurance, proficiency-testing integrity, corrective action, competency, audit trail and data-integrity controls remain aligned across languages. **PASS — specimen identity parity.** All editions preserve pre-analytical controls for patient/specimen identity, containers, volume, labeling, transport, temperature, integrity, aliquots, rejection/recollection and traceability. Guessing or relabeling from memory remains prohibited.

**PASS — safety parity.** Bloodborne-pathogen, universal-precaution, sharps, PPE, exposure-reporting and chemical-safety boundaries remain materially aligned with the OSHA source framework. No edition introduces improvised exposure treatment, culture handling or chemical recipes.

**PASS — laboratory-discipline parity.** Chemistry, hematology/hemostasis, urinalysis/body fluids, immunology/serology, microbiology, immunohematology/transfusion services, histology and molecular-testing descriptions remain scoped by training, competency, authorization and validated methods.

**PASS — privacy/cybersecurity parity.** LIS/EHR, unique account, access-control, workstation, personal-email/cloud, USB, phishing, downtime and reconciliation controls are preserved. U.S. HIPAA is not incorrectly generalized as a global legal framework.

**PASS — responsible-AI parity.** All editions prohibit unapproved AI use for patient interpretation, diagnosis, critical-value decisions, QC acceptance, proficiency testing, transfusion selection, microorganism identification as final authority, calibration/method changes, validation, result release and required qualified review.
