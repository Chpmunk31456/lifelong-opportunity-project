## High-consequence boundary checks

### Scope of practice
PASS. The master does not state that a title or short course independently authorizes diagnosis, patient-result interpretation, test-result release, high-complexity testing, transfusion decisions, pathology conclusions or laboratory direction.

### CLIA personnel qualification
PASS. The master explains that test complexity, laboratory certificate, education/experience, role assignment, state law and other requirements matter. It directs readers to current CDC/CMS/eCFR sources rather than supplying a universal eligibility shortcut.

### Quality control and proficiency testing
PASS. The master requires failed QC to be handled through approved procedures and prohibits manipulating repeat testing or proficiency-testing results.

### Safety
PASS. Bloodborne-pathogen, sharps and chemical-safety instructions are sourced to OSHA and framed through employer controls/SOPs. No unsafe improvised reagent, culture or exposure procedure is provided.

### Patient privacy and cybersecurity
PASS. The master gives conservative controls for identifiers, systems, accounts, downtime records and unapproved storage/AI use without claiming one privacy law applies globally.

### AI
PASS. NIST is used only as a general governance reference. Patient-specific interpretation, diagnosis, QC acceptance, validation, result release and other consequential clinical decisions remain with authorized humans and validated systems.

## International equivalence check

PASS. The master explicitly avoids false one-to-one equivalence:

- U.S.: O*NET 29-2012.00 technician benchmark.
- Canada: NOC 32120 MLT and NOC 33101 MLA/technical roles are separate comparators.
- Colombia: CUOC 32120 technician and CUOC 53294 auxiliary roles are separate classifications.

Credential transferability, legal scope and recognition are left to the applicable current regulator/authority.

## Market-data check

PASS. Official national wage data are the primary benchmark. Private salary estimates are separately labelled as noisy, title-sensitive market context. No private estimate is presented as a government statistic or guaranteed wage.
