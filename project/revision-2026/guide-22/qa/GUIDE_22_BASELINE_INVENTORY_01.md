## Occupational-risk classification

Guide 22 has elevated legal, financial, advertising, housing-discrimination, privacy, and licensing risk. Real-estate sales and leasing duties may involve regulated brokerage activity, property advertising, client representation, offers, negotiations, deposits, rental applications, identity and financial records, housing eligibility, commissions, and jurisdiction-specific disclosure obligations.

The 2026 revision must fail closed on these distinctions:

- real-estate sales-agent or broker licensing versus unlicensed administrative, showing, leasing, or customer-service duties;
- brokerage authority versus property-owner or property-manager duties;
- sales and leasing support versus legal, tax, mortgage, appraisal, inspection, escrow, notarial, registry, or title opinions;
- U.S. state licensing rules versus Canadian provincial/territorial rules and Latin American country/local rules;
- lawful marketing and tenant/buyer screening versus discriminatory steering, advertising, eligibility, or availability practices;
- public property information versus authorization to collect, retain, disclose, or upload personal, financial, identity, or application data;
- gross commissions or self-employment revenue versus employee wages or net income after business expenses;
- AI assistance versus authoritative legal, licensing, valuation, eligibility, disclosure, contract, or fair-housing decisions.
