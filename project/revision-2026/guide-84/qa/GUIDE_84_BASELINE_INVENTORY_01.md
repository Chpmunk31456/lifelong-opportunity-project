- translating business questions into measurable analytical requirements;
- data-source identification, data lineage and source-of-truth discipline;
- SQL/querying and relational-data concepts;
- data cleaning, transformation and validation;
- dimensional/star-schema and semantic-model concepts;
- KPI definitions and metric governance;
- dashboards, reports and data visualization;
- Power BI/Tableau and transferable BI concepts;
- statistics and trend interpretation;
- requirements, stakeholder communication and decision support;
- data quality, reproducibility and documentation;
- privacy, access control and confidential-data handling;
- responsible AI/automation and human verification;
- accessibility/inclusive dashboards and documents;
- U.S., Canada, Colombia and broader LATAM pathways;
- current official wage/outlook evidence plus clearly labelled market estimates;
- ethical portfolio, resume/interview and career-progression planning.

## Critical evidence boundary

O*NET-SOC **15-2051.01 — Business Intelligence Analysts** is a current, specific BI occupation. However, O*NET explicitly states that its national wage and employment data for this occupation are **collected from Data Scientists**. The controlled revision must disclose this crosswalk wherever those figures are presented; it must not imply that the BLS/O*NET wage and projection series represent a BI-only sampled population.

## Baseline controls

- No legacy numeric claim is accepted without current-source verification.
- Country mappings may differ in scope and must not be described as exact legal equivalence.
- BI analysis must distinguish observed data from assumptions, estimates and recommendations.
- Analysts must not silently change source data, KPI definitions or filters to obtain a preferred result.
- Sensitive data, production access and row-level/security permissions remain governed by employer authority and applicable policy.
- AI-generated queries, summaries, calculations and interpretations require human validation.
- Publication remains fail-closed until trilingual parity and DOCX/PDF package QA pass.
