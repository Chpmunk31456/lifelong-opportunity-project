## BI semantic anchors

All localizations must preserve:

- business question before visualization;
- source-of-truth and lineage discipline;
- row grain/key/join risk;
- SQL query validation;
- documented transformations and reproducibility;
- star-schema/fact/dimension concepts;
- semantic-model and reusable metric logic;
- KPI definition/governance;
- explicit filters/date logic;
- data-quality dimensions;
- reconciliation before decision-critical publication;
- descriptive statistics and correlation-versus-causation boundary;
- non-misleading visualization principles;
- stakeholder requirements and separation of fact, assumption, interpretation and recommendation.

## Integrity/security/privacy anchors

All localizations must preserve:

- no silent source-data manipulation;
- no unapproved KPI redefinition;
- no hidden material filters/exclusions;
- no deliberate deletion of legitimate outliers merely to improve presentation;
- least privilege;
- approved exports/storage/sharing;
- row-level access/security concepts;
- credential/secret protection;
- incident reporting;
- no claim of accounting/legal/regulatory/security authority solely from BI access.

## Responsible AI anchors

- approved systems/data only
- no protected/confidential extracts or credentials in unapproved public AI
- human verification of generated SQL, DAX/formulas, calculations and narratives
- reconciliation to authoritative sources
- generated interpretation distinguished from observed fact
- review for systematic error/bias/unsupported causation
- no autonomous publication of decision-critical output outside approved governance
