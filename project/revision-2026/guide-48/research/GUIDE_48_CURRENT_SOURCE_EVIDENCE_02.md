## Research boundary

This evidence set treats **Medical Assistant** as the U.S. occupation defined by O*NET/BLS and does not assume that the same title, clinical scope, credential, or legal authority transfers directly to Canada, Colombia, or another Latin American jurisdiction. Cross-border sections must describe analogous pathways carefully and preserve local scope-of-practice distinctions.

## United States — occupation identity and duties

### O*NET OnLine — Medical Assistants, 31-9092.00

Current O*NET classifies Medical Assistants as **31-9092.00** and describes the occupation as performing administrative and certain clinical duties under the direction of a physician. Administrative duties can include scheduling, records, billing, and coding; clinical duties can include vital signs, histories, examination preparation, blood collection, and medication administration as directed.

O*NET's current task/activity material also includes instrument cleaning, contaminated-supply disposal, specimen collection, routine testing, treatment-room preparation, assisting practitioners, appointment scheduling, inventory, and clerical work. These are occupational examples, not a universal authorization to perform every task in every state or workplace.

Source: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/31-9092.00  
Current page state observed: Bright Outlook, updated 2026.

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — education, pay, and outlook

BLS states that medical assistants typically need postsecondary education such as a certificate, while some enter with a high-school diploma and learn through on-the-job training. Requirements and permitted clinical functions can vary by state and employer; the guide must tell readers to verify local law, credential expectations, supervision requirements, and employer policy before performing clinical tasks.

BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook reports for Medical Assistants:

- **May 2024 median annual wage:** USD **$44,200**;
- lowest 10 percent: below **$35,020**;
- highest 10 percent: above **$57,830**;
- 2024 employment: approximately **811,000**;
- projected 2034 employment: approximately **912,200**;
- projected growth, 2024–2034: **12%**;
- projected average annual openings: approximately **112,300**.

Source: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/medical-assistants.htm  
BLS page last modified August 28, 2025; accessed/rechecked for this revision August 19, 2026.

## United States — apprenticeship, low-cost training, and funding

### Registered Apprenticeship

Apprenticeship.gov identifies **Medical Assistant** as a high-demand healthcare Registered Apprenticeship occupation and lists a competency-based occupational framework for the occupation. Availability is employer/location dependent; listing the occupation does not guarantee an open apprenticeship in every region.

Source: https://www.apprenticeship.gov/apprenticeship-industries/healthcare

### WIOA / American Job Center pathway

CareerOneStop's WIOA-Eligible Training Program Finder directs eligible users to state directories of WIOA-approved training and advises people who are unsure of eligibility to contact their local American Job Center. Medical-assistant candidates should check whether a specific local program is approved before assuming WIOA will pay for it.

Source: https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/EmploymentAndTraining/find-WIOA-training-programs.aspx

### Employer support

The guide should explicitly recommend asking healthcare employers about paid onboarding, tuition assistance, apprenticeship/cohort programs, exam or certification reimbursement, scrubs/equipment support, cross-training, and advancement pathways. Any repayment or minimum-service obligation should be obtained in writing before accepting employer-funded education.

## Canada — do not force a one-to-one U.S. title mapping

Canada's Job Bank does not provide a single national occupation that is an exact equivalent of the U.S. Medical Assistant blend of administrative and delegated clinical work. The Version 2 guide must say this directly.

Depending on the actual duties, related Canadian pathways may include:

### Medical administrative assistant — NOC 13112

Job Bank describes Medical administrative assistants as performing secretarial and administrative duties in doctors' offices, hospitals, clinics, and other medical settings. Current national summary information reports:

- typical requirement: secondary school plus a one- or two-year college program or other specialized secretarial/medical-secretarial courses;
- national median wage: approximately **CAD $25.00/hour**;
- Job Bank records this occupation as not regulated nationally, while employer requirements still vary.

Sources:  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/25791/ca  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/25791/ca

### Medical office assistant / receptionist — NOC 14101

Job Bank maps the title Medical Office Assistant to Receptionists (NOC 14101), focused on reception, scheduling, call handling, and clerical work rather than the full U.S. clinical Medical Assistant scope. Current national summary information reports a median wage of approximately **CAD $21.00/hour** and typically secondary-school completion with possible on-the-job training.

Sources:  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/24389/ca  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/24389/ca

### Patient/basic-care support — NOC 33102

For roles involving direct basic patient care, Job Bank's Nurse aides, orderlies and patient service associates (NOC 33102) may be a closer comparison for some duties, but it is a distinct occupation and must not be presented as a direct Medical Assistant equivalent. Current national summary information reports a median wage of approximately **CAD $24.00/hour** and generally post-secondary, supervised practical, or substantial on-the-job preparation.

Sources:  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/15798/ca  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/15798/ca

### Canada student aid

For an eligible designated post-secondary program, Canada Student Grants and Loans may help with education costs. For the 2026–27 school year, the Canada Student Grant for Full-Time Students can provide up to **CAD $4,200 per year / $525 per month of study**, subject to financial need, qualified-program/designated-school rules, province/territory participation, and other eligibility requirements. Students apply through their province or territory.

Sources:  
https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/student-aid/grants-loans/full-time.html  
https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/student-aid/grants-loans/province-apply.html

Do **not** describe Red Seal apprenticeship grants or Canada Apprentice Loans as a normal Medical Assistant funding route; those programs are aimed at designated skilled trades and do not create a general medical-assistant entitlement.

## Colombia — distinct auxiliary-health and administrative pathways

The U.S. Medical Assistant occupation should not be translated into a claim that Colombia authorizes the same blended clinical scope.

### OCUPACOL — Auxiliares administrativos en salud, CUOC 42291

Colombia's Ministry of Labour OCUPACOL profile **42291 — Auxiliares administrativos en salud** describes workers who receive and orient users, support physicians administratively, process affiliation/admission and medical-record forms, and review/generate billing in health-service settings. It lists occupational titles such as auxiliar administrativo en salud, auxiliar de admisiones hospitalarias, auxiliar de apoyo administrativo en salud, auxiliar de registro y admisiones, and related user-service/billing roles.

Source: https://ocupacol.mintrabajo.gov.co/Profile/OccupationalProfile/42291

### Colombian auxiliary-health training/legal context

SENA's legal compilation of the Colombian rules for auxiliary health areas identifies **Auxiliar Administrativo en Salud** among auxiliary-health personnel and states that recognized auxiliary-health occupational profiles use a competency-based occupational aptitude certificate with the `Técnico Laboral en...` denomination under the applicable framework. Because Colombian health-training and practice requirements are legal/regulatory matters, readers must verify the current program authorization and any current Ministry of Health/Education requirements before enrolling or performing clinical work.

Source: https://normograma.sena.edu.co/compilacion/docs/decreto_4904_2009.htm  
The compilation notes later incorporation into national regulatory decrees; the Version 2 guide must treat this as regulatory context, not individualized legal advice.

### Current Colombian labour-market evidence

SENA's Agencia Pública de Empleo has current 2026 postings under the title **Auxiliar administrativo en salud**, confirming active employer use of the occupation in Colombia. Recent listings in the live search included salary bands such as **COP $1,500,001–$2,000,000** and, in another posting, **COP $2,000,001–$2,500,000**, with experience requirements varying by employer. These are vacancy examples, not official national wage statistics and must not be presented as a guaranteed market rate.

Locator/search source: https://agenciapublicadeempleo.sena.edu.co/

### Colombia free/public training first

Readers in Colombia should search current SENA/Betowa offerings and verify the exact program title, training centre, modality, registration status, and whether a program is formally authorized for an auxiliary-health credential before paying a private provider.

SENA Betowa training locator: https://betowa.sena.edu.co/

## Latin America and Caribbean pathway

Health-support occupation names, training regulation, credentialing, delegated clinical tasks, and employer expectations vary substantially across Latin America. OIT/Cinterfor should be used as a regional locator to identify national vocational-training institutions, after which the learner should verify the current national health/education regulator and employer requirements.

Regional vocational-training locator: https://www.oitcinterfor.org/

The guide must not imply that a U.S. Medical Assistant certificate automatically authorizes practice elsewhere in the region.

## Clinical safety, privacy, and AI controls required in the English master

The controlled English Version 2 must include explicit boundaries that:

- clinical tasks are performed only when allowed by applicable law, training, credential status, delegation/supervision, and employer policy;
- learners must not independently diagnose, prescribe, alter treatment, interpret results beyond authorized duties, or present themselves as licensed clinicians when they are not;
- injection, medication, specimen collection, wound care, electrocardiography, or other hands-on procedures require the exact training and authorization applicable to the jurisdiction and workplace;
- infection prevention, sharps safety, exposure response, PPE, biomedical-waste handling, and emergency procedures follow employer and jurisdictional requirements;
- protected health information and patient identifiers must not be entered into public AI tools or unauthorized systems;
- AI may assist with non-sensitive study, terminology practice, or administrative drafting only when permitted, but must not substitute for clinical judgment, diagnosis, treatment decisions, medication instructions, or approved records; and
- patient-facing information generated by AI must be checked against approved clinical/employer sources before use.

## Source hierarchy and evidence decision

The controlled revision will use official government/public sources as the factual backbone: U.S. DOL O*NET/BLS, Apprenticeship.gov/CareerOneStop, Government of Canada Job Bank/Canada Student Aid, Colombia Ministry of Labour OCUPACOL, SENA legal/training/employment systems, and OIT/Cinterfor. Salary.com is retained only as a clearly labeled current non-government U.S. market estimate.

**Research gate: PASS.** The evidence is sufficient to reconstruct an occupation-specific English Version 2 while preserving jurisdictional distinctions and clinical-safety boundaries. The next permitted gate is **English Editorial / controlled Version 2 reconstruction**.
