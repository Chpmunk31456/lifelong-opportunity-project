## Traceability map

### United States occupation and scope

- O*NET Medical Assistants `31-9092.00` supports the occupation identity and the administrative/clinical task examples.
- BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook supports typical entry preparation, the 2024 employment/outlook series, and the warning that requirements vary.
- O*NET national wages supports the newer 2025 BLS wage figures used as the primary current official compensation figures.

### U.S. training and funding

- Apprenticeship.gov healthcare resources support Medical Assistant as a high-demand Registered Apprenticeship occupation and the existence of a competency-based framework.
- CareerOneStop supports the WIOA eligible-training and American Job Center pathway, with eligibility kept conditional.

### Canada

- Job Bank NOC 13112 supports the medical-administrative comparison, requirements, and national median.
- Job Bank NOC 14101 supports the medical-office/reception comparison, requirements, and national median.
- Job Bank NOC 33102 supports the distinct basic patient-care support comparison, requirements, and national median.
- Canada Student Aid sources support the 2026–27 full-time grant maximum and province/territory application route.
- The English master explicitly refuses to treat these occupations as one direct U.S. Medical Assistant equivalent.

### Colombia

- Ministry of Labour OCUPACOL CUOC 42291 supports the `Auxiliares administrativos en salud` occupation identity, administrative health-service functions, and related job titles.
- SENA regulatory compilation supports the distinct Colombian auxiliary-health training framework and the need to verify authorized programs/current regulation.
- SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo supports current employer use of the `Auxiliar administrativo en salud` title and the dated vacancy examples.
- SENA Betowa is used only as a current public training locator; no unverified specific course availability is guaranteed.

### Latin America and Caribbean

- OIT/Cinterfor is used as a regional vocational-training institution locator, not as evidence that one U.S. credential transfers across borders.

### Non-government compensation

- Salary.com is cited only for its August 1, 2026 U.S. Medical Assistant estimate and is explicitly separated from BLS/O*NET government wage statistics.

## Claim-control review

No evidence-supported statement in the master was converted into a stronger guarantee. In particular:

- apprenticeship availability remains employer/location dependent;
- WIOA and student aid remain eligibility dependent;
- clinical tasks remain subject to law, training, delegation, supervision, and employer policy;
- Canadian and Colombian pathways remain non-equivalent to the U.S. occupation;
- Colombian vacancy salary bands remain examples, not national wage statistics;
- private salary data remains labeled non-government; and
- no source is used to claim independent certification, accreditation, legal review, medical review, or guaranteed employment.
