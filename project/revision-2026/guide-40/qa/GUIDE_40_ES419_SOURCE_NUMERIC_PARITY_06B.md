## Required es-419 terminology boundaries

- construction laborer: `obrero de construcción` or `trabajador de construcción`, by context.
- trade helper: `ayudante de oficio` / `ayudante de oficio de construcción`.
- skilled trade: `oficio especializado`.
- PPE: `equipo de protección personal (EPP)`.
- on-the-job training: `capacitación en el trabajo`.
- work-based learning: `formación basada en el trabajo`.
- Keep official names such as `Registered Apprenticeship`, `Construction Craft Worker`, `Red Seal`, `NOC 75110`, OSHA and O*NET where source fidelity requires them.

## Assurance boundaries

The localized master must not imply that helper experience independently authorizes regulated skilled-trade work, heavy-equipment operation, hazardous-material removal, blasting, specialized scaffolding/rigging, electrical/plumbing practice, or other separately controlled work.

OSHA 10/30 Outreach remains voluntary awareness training, not a certification or license and not a substitute for task-, standard-, site-, employer-, competent-person-, or qualified-person requirements.

The localized master must not introduce claims of independent human certification, accreditation, certified translation, legal review, financial advice, guaranteed admission, guaranteed funding, guaranteed apprenticeship placement, or guaranteed employment.
