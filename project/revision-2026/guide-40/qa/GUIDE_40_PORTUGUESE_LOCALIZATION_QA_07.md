## Localization controls

- **Occupation scope:** PASS. The pt-BR edition preserves the distinction between general construction labor/helper work and independently regulated or specialist skilled-trade work.
- **Safety boundary:** PASS. OSHA Outreach is described as safety-awareness training, not a professional certification or license, and the text preserves the requirement for employer/site/task-specific training and qualified supervision.
- **U.S. numeric parity:** PASS. The pt-BR edition preserves BLS May 2024 figures of $46,050, $22.14/hour, $46,730, $40,430, 1,649,100 jobs, 7% projected growth, and 149,400 average annual openings; it separately identifies the O*NET/BLS 2025 median of $47,120/$22.66 per hour.
- **Private U.S. estimate labeling:** PASS. Salary.com is clearly labeled supplementary/private and retains the July 1, 2026 values of $43,578/year, $21/hour, and the stated $40,504–$49,004 percentile range.
- **Canada parity:** PASS. NOC 75110 and the national Job Bank wage figures CAD $18.25/$25.00/$40.00 per hour are preserved, together with the November 19, 2025 update and 2023–2024 reference period.
- **Canada progression/funding:** PASS. Construction Craft Worker/Red Seal is presented as a possible progression route rather than a universal requirement; Canada Apprentice Loan is limited to eligible apprentices and preserves the up-to-CAD $4,000 amount.
- **Colombia pathway:** PASS. SENA remains the free-first public training anchor; the Mampostería example retains approximately 1,296 hours and is not presented as guaranteeing a current seat. High-risk work-at-height training is treated separately.
- **Latin America coverage:** PASS. OIT/Cinterfor is retained as an institutional locator, with country-specific verification required.
- **Funding and employer support:** PASS. WIOA, Canadian apprenticeship support, SENA/public training, employer, union, and contractor support are described as conditional rather than guaranteed.
- **Accessibility:** PASS. The text avoids assuming one physical profile for all construction jobs and points readers toward role-specific essential functions and applicable accommodation or rehabilitation resources.
- **AI/privacy/cybersecurity:** PASS. AI is limited to low-risk learning/administrative use and is explicitly excluded as a substitute for site procedures, manufacturer instructions, task-specific controls, professional decisions, or qualified supervision. Confidential project information is protected.
- **Scam/consumer safeguards:** PASS. The translation preserves warnings about guaranteed jobs/salaries, generic credentials, misleading OSHA claims, unclear practical training, hidden costs, and requests for payment for job offers.
- **Source URL coverage:** PASS. The pt-BR edition retains the principal official BLS, O*NET, OSHA, Apprenticeship.gov, CareerOneStop, Job Bank, Red Seal, Canada, SENA, and OIT/Cinterfor locators plus the clearly separated Salary.com supplementary source.
- **Language quality:** PASS. The edition uses natural Brazilian Portuguese, Brazilian punctuation and number formatting in prose, and consistent terminology without machine-literal constructions that change meaning.
- **Encoding/accessibility:** PASS. UTF-8 text, Markdown heading hierarchy, simple lists, descriptive institution names, and readable link presentation are preserved.
- **Assurance boundary:** PASS. The edition does not claim independent human certification, certified translation, accreditation, legal advice, medical fitness determination, or guaranteed employment.
