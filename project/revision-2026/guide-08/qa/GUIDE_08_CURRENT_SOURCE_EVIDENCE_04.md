## United States — official occupation evidence

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

BLS identifies **Human Resources Assistants, Except Payroll and Timekeeping (SOC 43-4161)** as a distinct occupation.

Current May 2025 OEWS national data report:

- employment: **90,220**
- mean hourly wage: **$25.12**
- mean annual wage: **$52,250**
- median hourly wage: **$24.33**

BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook data for May 2024 report a median annual wage of **$49,440** for this occupation. Employment projections for 2024–2034 show **95,200** jobs in 2024 and **88,400** in 2034, a projected decline of about **7%**. These figures must be labeled by reference year rather than blended into a single statistic.

Official sources:
- https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ocwage.t01.htm
- https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/information-clerks.htm
- https://www.bls.gov/emp/tables/occupational-projections-and-characteristics.htm

## United States — current non-government market estimate

Indeed's Human Resources Assistant salary page, updated **July 20, 2026**, reports an average base salary of **$21.76/hour** in the United States, with a displayed low of **$16.09/hour** and high of **$29.42/hour**, based on 7.8k salaries from job postings over the prior 36 months.

This is a non-government market estimate and must not be presented as an official wage statistic or guaranteed pay.

Source:

## Canada — official occupation evidence

Government of Canada Job Bank maps **Human Resources Assistant** to **Personnel clerks, NOC 14102**. The national profile states that the occupation usually requires a secondary-school diploma or several weeks of on-the-job training.

National wages updated **November 19, 2025**:

- low: **C$21.75/hour**
- median: **C$27.88/hour**
- high: **C$38.46/hour**
- reference period: **2023–2024**
- source reported by Job Bank: Labour Force Survey, Statistics Canada

Official sources:
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/26969/ca
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/26972/ca

## Colombia — official public training and employment pathways

SENA's Betowa catalog currently lists the **Técnico en Talento Humano** program with **2,208 hours** of training. Listed competencies include supporting human-talent activities, preparing documents, customer service, digital tools, workplace rights, and an applied practical stage. Availability and schedules are cohort- and location-dependent and must be checked at enrollment time.

SENA also lists a **48-hour virtual complementary course, Herramientas para la Gestión del Talento Humano**, with current 2026 enrollment availability shown for at least one Bogotá offering.

The SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo is a free public employment-intermediation service. Current vacancy records for titles such as `auxiliar en talento humano` and `auxiliar recursos humanos` show duties including recruitment support, personnel records, selection/entry/training documentation, reports, social-security affiliations, payroll changes, document management, and employee service. Vacancy-level pay and requirements should be researched locally rather than converted into a national salary claim.

Official sources:
- https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/talento-humano?programId=224441
- https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/herramientas-para-la-gestion-del-talento-humano?programId=148534

## Employer-supported education — United States control

IRS guidance updated in April 2026 states that qualifying benefits under a Section 127 educational assistance program may be excluded from an employee's gross income up to **$5,250 per calendar year for 2025 and 2026**. The amount is subject to cost-of-living adjustment for taxable years beginning after 2026. The guide must not imply that every employer offers such a plan or that every educational expense qualifies.

Official source:
- https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-updates-frequently-asked-questions-about-section-127-educational-assistance-programs

## Apprenticeships and free/low-cost pathways

For the United States, readers should search current Registered Apprenticeship opportunities by occupation and geography rather than assume a dedicated HR-assistant apprenticeship exists locally:
- https://www.apprenticeship.gov/

For Canada, readers should check provincial/territorial training and employment systems in addition to Job Bank because availability, funding, credential expectations, and work-based learning differ by jurisdiction.

For Latin America, avoid generalizing a single credential or wage structure across countries. Use the country's public employment service, labor ministry, public technical-education system, and current employer postings. In Colombia, SENA and its Agencia Pública de Empleo provide the principal public pathway examples used in this guide.

## Reconstruction controls

The English v2 master must:

- use the BLS 43-4161 occupation rather than higher-paid Human Resources Specialist data as the primary U.S. official comparison;
- state reference dates beside wage and projection data;
- keep Indeed's July 20, 2026 estimate clearly labeled as non-government market evidence;
- map Canada to NOC 14102 and retain Job Bank's update/reference-period labels;
- describe SENA offerings as current pathways whose cohort availability changes;
- distinguish scholarships, grants, tax benefits, employer tuition assistance, apprenticeships, and free training instead of calling all assistance "free";
- avoid implying legal, tax, labor-relations, accommodation, benefits, immigration, or payroll authority beyond an assistant's assigned role;
- retain privacy, discrimination, accessibility, records-integrity, cybersecurity, and human-review controls for AI-assisted HR work.
