# Guide 73 — Current Source Evidence

## Occupation
Sales Support and Account Coordinator

## Research date
2026-08-21

## Controlled purpose
This evidence pack supports the English revision of Guide 73. The guide title spans support and account-coordination work that does not map perfectly to one occupational code, so the controlled approach uses transparent benchmark families rather than claiming a universal one-to-one crosswalk. Compensation values are descriptive and do not guarantee pay, employment, commission, promotion, funding, or credential outcomes.

## United States — official benchmark

### O*NET OnLine — Sales Representatives of Services
Source: https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/41-3091.00

Controlled facts:
- O*NET-SOC **41-3091.00 — Sales Representatives of Services, Except Advertising, Insurance, Financial Services, and Travel** is an appropriate broad benchmark for service-oriented account and sales-support work when duties include customer questions, quotes, ongoing account support, proposals, customer records, and service terms.
- The O*NET profile was updated in **2026** and is designated Bright Outlook.
- O*NET describes the occupation as selling services to individuals or businesses and notes that workers may explain options or resolve client problems.
- Relevant tasks include answering customer questions about prices, availability, and credit terms; consulting with clients after sales or contract signing; maintaining customer records in automated systems; preparing forms or agreements; developing proposals; quoting prices or fulfillment dates; and negotiating sales/service terms.
- O*NET lists CRM, ERP, office-suite, spreadsheet, presentation, project-management, and sales/marketing software among relevant technology families.
- O*NET reports **2025 median wages of $33.65/hour and $69,990/year** for this broader sales-representative benchmark.
- Employment: approximately **1,226,700 workers in 2024**.
- Projected growth: **3% to 4% from 2024 to 2034**.
- Projected job openings: approximately **123,000** over the projection period as reported on O*NET.
- O*NET classifies the occupation in **Job Zone 1-2**, commonly associated with a high-school diploma or GED and on-the-job training, although actual employer requirements vary materially by industry and account complexity.

### Benchmark limitation
Guide 73 must not imply that every Sales Support or Account Coordinator is a quota-carrying salesperson. Many employers separate lead generation, pricing authority, negotiation, contracting, commission ownership, and closing authority from support/coordination roles. The English master should distinguish administrative/operational account support from direct-sales accountability.

## Canada — official benchmark

### Job Bank — Sales and account representatives, wholesale trade (non-technical)
Sources:
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/23200/ca
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/23197
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/22311/ca

Controlled facts:
- Canadian benchmark: **NOC 64101 — Sales and account representatives - wholesale trade (non-technical)**.
- Job Bank describes the occupation as selling non-technical goods and services to retail, wholesale, commercial, industrial, professional, and other clients in Canada or internationally.
- The occupation is used across goods- and service-producing employers, including business-services firms and transportation companies.
- Canada-wide wages, updated **November 19, 2025**: **C$19.23 low, C$31.50 median, C$56.23 high per hour**; reference period 2023-2024, Statistics Canada Labour Force Survey.
- Job Bank notes that job prospects vary by province and territory.
- The current summary states that a secondary-school diploma or several weeks of on-the-job training is generally associated with the occupation; the detailed requirements page also notes that sales or product/service-related experience is usually required for some roles.
- Voluntary professional sales certification exists, but the guide must not present voluntary certification as a universal legal requirement.

### Benchmark limitation
NOC 64101 includes active selling and may be broader or more sales-intensive than a coordinator who primarily supports account managers, maintains CRM data, prepares approved quotes, coordinates orders, tracks renewals, or handles post-sale administration. The English guide should state this difference explicitly.

## Colombia — official occupation benchmark

### OCUPACOL — Ministerio del Trabajo
Source: https://ocupacol.mintrabajo.gov.co/Profile/OccupationalProfile/33220

Controlled facts:
- Colombian classification: **CUOC 33220 — Representantes comerciales**, competence level 3.
- Listed titles include **Ejecutivo de cuenta de ventas no técnicas**, Ejecutivo de ventas no técnicas, Representante comercial, Representante de ventas, Asesor comercial, Vendedor interno de ventas no técnicas, and post-sale service titles.
- Relevant functions include identifying customer needs, following up after a sale, taking customer orders, maintaining market information, supplying product/service information, preparing quotes and sales terms, promoting sales, identifying prospects, and managing customer-account collection responsibilities where assigned.
- Knowledge areas include administration, mathematics, marketing/advertising, customer service, office/administrative services, economics, and foreign languages.
- The occupational scope is broader and more sales-intensive than some account-coordinator jobs, so the controlled guide must distinguish direct selling from support/coordination authority.
- OCUPACOL displays occupation-level market indicators, but its displayed wage figures must not be treated as a definitive current salary benchmark without methodological qualification. The guide should prefer clearly labelled current market estimates for Colombia compensation and use OCUPACOL primarily for occupation scope and titles.

## Current non-government market estimates — clearly labelled

These figures are market estimates from Indeed, not government wage statistics. They are title-specific and should not be blended with official occupational benchmarks as though they measure identical work.

### United States — Sales Support Representative
Source: https://www.indeed.com/career/sales-support-representative/salaries

- Indeed reported an average base salary of approximately **$21.10/hour** for Sales Support Representatives in the United States.
- The estimate was based on approximately **4.2k salaries from job postings over the prior 36 months**, updated **August 3, 2026**.
- Displayed range: approximately **$14.98 to $29.71/hour**.

### Controlled interpretation
The gap between these market-title estimates illustrates why Guide 73 must explain title ambiguity. Industry, location, commission eligibility, customer segment, account complexity, technical knowledge, language requirements, seniority, and whether the job owns revenue or merely supports it can materially change compensation.

## Privacy, cybersecurity, fraud, and responsible-AI controls

### Practical control areas
The English revision should explicitly cover:
- customer and prospect contact data;
- CRM records and access permissions;
- quotes, discounts, credit terms, purchase orders, contracts, and renewal information;
- payment-change and bank-detail fraud;
- phishing and business-email-compromise risk;
- approval authority for pricing, discounts, credits, refunds, contract terms, and commitments;
- secure sharing of customer lists and exports;
- avoiding personal email or unapproved cloud storage for business records;
- human verification of unusual requests and consequential AI output;
- prohibition on placing credentials, payment data, confidential customer information, contract text, or restricted business information into unapproved AI tools.

Reference frameworks/guidance:
- CISA Secure Our World: https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

The guide should present these as practical risk-management boundaries rather than claiming cybersecurity, legal, privacy, or AI-governance certification.

## Editorial controls for the English revision
The revised English master should:
1. distinguish sales-support/account-coordination work from full account-executive, account-manager, and quota-carrying sales responsibility;
2. explain how titles vary by employer and industry;
3. use O*NET 41-3091.00, Canada NOC 64101, and Colombia CUOC 33220 as transparent benchmarks while stating that the scopes are not identical;
4. teach CRM hygiene, customer-record accuracy, quote/order coordination, follow-up, meeting support, pipeline updates, handoffs, account documentation, and post-sale issue escalation;
5. explain authority limits around discounts, contracts, pricing, credit, refunds, payment changes, and customer promises;
6. include current official wage data and separately labelled non-government title estimates;
7. cover WIOA/American Job Center, Canada training supports, SENA/Betowa, OIT/Cinterfor, scholarships, internships, employer training, and work-based learning without promising eligibility or availability;
8. include plain-language and accessibility-aware customer communication;
9. include phishing, BEC/payment-change verification, CRM access, customer-data handling, and approved-system controls;
10. permit AI for low-risk drafting, summaries, meeting preparation, or CRM-note cleanup only with employer approval, human verification, and privacy controls;
11. avoid presenting voluntary sales certifications as universal requirements;
12. avoid promising commissions, remote work, advancement, funding, or employment;
13. preserve dysgraphia-friendly structure through short sections, reusable checklists, examples, and clear escalation steps.
