## Freeze controls

1. **Occupation scope fixed.** U.S. Pharmacy Technicians remain anchored to O*NET/SOC 29-2052.00. Canada remains NOC 32124. Colombia remains separately mapped to CNO 3315 / the SENA Servicios Farmacéuticos pathway.
2. **Jurisdiction boundaries fixed.** The English source does not treat one credential, registration rule, supervision model, or permitted task as universal.
3. **Pharmacist/authorized-professional boundary fixed.** Diagnosis, prescribing, therapeutic substitution, final clinical verification, pharmacist-only counseling, and other regulated clinical judgments are not assigned to technicians generally.
4. **Specialized-work boundary fixed.** Compounding, sterile/hazardous preparation, vaccine administration, controlled-substance functions, and advanced technical roles remain conditional on current legal scope, employer procedures, supervision, training, and documented competency.
5. **Medication-data integrity fixed.** The source prohibits fabrication, backfilling, silent normalization, concealment, and unauthorized changes to patient, prescription, inventory, controlled-substance, compounding, insurance, storage, recall, expiration, and quality records.
6. **AI/privacy boundary fixed.** Public AI is not an operational source of truth and may not receive protected pharmacy data unless the employer has explicitly approved the system and data use.
7. **Official numeric controls fixed.** U.S. 2025 median $22.00/hour and $45,750/year; 490,400 workers in 2024; 6% growth; 49,000 annual openings; Canada CAD $17.50 / $24.83 / $34.20; Colombia CNO 3315; SENA 2,640 hours and 16+.
8. **Private wage separation fixed.** Indeed's approximately $21.13/hour July 27, 2026 estimate remains clearly labeled non-government and is not merged with official wage data.
9. **Funding boundaries fixed.** WIOA, FAFSA, Canada student aid, SENA/public training, and employer support are conditional routes rather than guarantees.
10. **Assurance boundary fixed.** The guide does not claim independent human certification, professional pharmacy or medical review, legal/regulatory review, professional translation certification, accessibility certification, accreditation, guaranteed licensure/registration, guaranteed funding, guaranteed employment, or guaranteed earnings.
