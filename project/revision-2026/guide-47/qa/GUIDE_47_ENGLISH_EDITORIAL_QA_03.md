## Editorial controls completed

The controlled English Version 2 master was reviewed against the current-source evidence and Guide 47 baseline requirements.

### Occupation and jurisdiction framing

PASS. The guide anchors the U.S. occupation to O*NET/SOC 29-2052.00, distinguishes Canadian NOC 32124, and separately maps the Colombian SENA/CNO 3315 auxiliary-services pathway. It does not represent these systems as one universal credential or legal scope.

PASS. State/provincial/country regulation, registration, certification, supervision, and employer authorization are repeatedly presented as variable and subject to current verification.

### Pharmacy-scope boundary

PASS. The guide does not teach diagnosis, prescribing, therapeutic substitution, final clinical verification, pharmacist-only counseling, unauthorized refill decisions, controlled-substance bypass, or concealment of medication errors.

PASS. Compounding, sterile/hazardous preparation, vaccine administration, controlled-substance work, and other specialized tasks are treated as conditional functions requiring actual jurisdictional authorization, employer procedure, training, supervision, and competency.

### Medication safety and data integrity

PASS. The guide includes stop-and-escalate conditions for patient identity, prescription completeness, product/strength/form/quantity mismatches, storage or recall concerns, controlled-substance discrepancies, and tasks outside authorization.

PASS. It explicitly prohibits inventing, backfilling, copying forward, silently normalizing, or altering live prescription, patient, inventory, controlled-substance, compounding, insurance, temperature, recall, expiration, or quality records.

### Privacy, cybersecurity, and AI

PASS. Patient, prescription, insurance, prescriber, controlled-substance, inventory, and pharmacy-system information is treated as sensitive. The guide requires approved devices, systems, accounts, and data-sharing methods.

PASS. AI is limited to non-sensitive learning support. The guide prohibits using AI as the source of truth for live medication selection, prescription data, dosage calculations, compounding data, controlled-substance records, claims, inventory corrections, storage/expiration data, final verification, or counseling.

### Training, funding, and career pathways

PASS. U.S. on-the-job training, postsecondary training, state licensing/certification verification, WIOA, FAFSA, Apprenticeship.gov, Canadian supervised practical training and student aid, SENA Técnico en Servicios Farmacéuticos, and OIT/Cinterfor regional vocational locators are represented with conditional eligibility/availability language.

PASS. The SENA Técnico/Auxiliar pathway is not represented as equivalent to Tecnólogo en Regencia de Farmacia or Químico Farmacéutico.

### Wage and outlook controls

PASS. The guide uses the current O*NET/BLS 2025 U.S. wage series as the official controlled wage anchor: USD $22.00/hour and $45,750/year. It separately identifies 490,400 workers in 2024, 6 percent projected growth for 2024–2034, and about 49,000 annual openings.

PASS. Indeed's approximately USD $21.13/hour July 27, 2026 figure is clearly labeled as a non-government market estimate and is not averaged with official data.

PASS. Canada Job Bank national low/median/high wages are separately labeled CAD $17.50 / $24.83 / $34.20 per hour.

### Accessibility, readability, and encoding

PASS. The English master uses descriptive headings, short paragraphs, plain-language warnings, ordered sequences only where sequence is meaningful, and lists for scan-friendly controls. Acronyms and jurisdictional distinctions are introduced in context.

PASS. Text is valid UTF-8 and does not rely on corrupted underscore substitutions found in legacy localized filenames.

PASS. No unsupported claim of independent human review, professional pharmacy/medical review, legal/regulatory review, professional translation certification, accessibility certification, accreditation, guaranteed licensure/registration, funding, employment, or earnings is made.
