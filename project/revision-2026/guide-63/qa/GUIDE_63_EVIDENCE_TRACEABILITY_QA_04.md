## Controlled claim traceability

### United States

PASS — The master preserves the official distinction between:

- Library Technicians — SOC 25-4031
- Library Assistants, Clerical — SOC 43-4121

PASS — Education language remains differentiated: technicians typically require a postsecondary certificate/nondegree award, while assistants typically require a high-school diploma or equivalent plus short-term on-the-job training.

PASS — May 2024 BLS wage figures remain unchanged:

- Library technicians: $19.22/hour
- Library assistants, clerical: $17.31/hour
- Combined profile: $18.05/hour / $37,540 per year

PASS — Outlook remains stated as a 7% decline from 2024 to 2034 with about 25,800 average annual openings from replacement needs. The master explicitly states that labor-market figures are not guarantees.

PASS — U.S. Copyright Office Section 108 is described as conditional and not as blanket reproduction, digitization, distribution, or publication permission.

### Canada

PASS — The master preserves the national distinction between:

- NOC 52100 — Library and public archive technicians
- NOC 14300 — Library assistants and clerks

PASS — Education language remains source-aligned: two- to three-year college program for technician roles and secondary school normally required for assistant/clerk roles.

PASS — The master preserves Job Bank's current statement that these occupations are not regulated according to its records and does not generalize this into professional librarian status.

PASS — Wage figures remain unchanged:

- NOC 52100 national median: C$28.00/hour
- NOC 14300 national median: C$23.17/hour
- NOC 14300 range: C$15.80 low / C$23.17 median / C$34.00 high

### Colombia

PASS — The master preserves SENA CNO 1351 — Auxiliares de biblioteca as an assistant-level classification and does not equate it to a professional bibliotecólogo credential.

PASS — Colombia wage language remains conservative: no directly comparable current official national wage series was established for the combined Guide 63 title, so the master does not invent one.

PASS — Ministry of Education and Biblioteca Nacional resources are presented as skills-development/public-policy resources, not universal hiring credentials.

## Role, safety, privacy, copyright, accessibility, AI, and cybersecurity boundaries

PASS — Assistant/clerical work is not collapsed into technician work.

PASS — Technician work is not presented as equivalent to professional librarian, archivist, copyright specialist, privacy officer, records officer, or other regulated/professional authority.

PASS — Copyright language does not provide individualized legal advice or blanket permission.

PASS — Patron records, borrowing history, account information, credentials, licensed content, and internal records are protected by conservative employer-policy and authorization language.

PASS — Accessibility language supports inclusive service without claiming independent legal-compliance certification authority.

PASS — Physical duties remain non-procedural and subject to employer training and safety rules.

PASS — Responsible-AI language prohibits placing confidential patron data, credentials, licensed content, unpublished/internal records, or other protected material into unapproved AI systems.

PASS — Cybersecurity language is limited to approved account hygiene, phishing/social-engineering awareness, reporting, and escalation; it does not authorize out-of-scope forensic or administrative actions.
