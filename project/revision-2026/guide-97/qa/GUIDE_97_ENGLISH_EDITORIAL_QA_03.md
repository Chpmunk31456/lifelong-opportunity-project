## Safety and hazardous-energy controls
**PASS**

The master contains conservative, explicit controls for:
- lockout/tagout and hazardous-energy isolation;
- mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, gravitational, pressure, spring, and rotational stored energy;
- machine guarding and rotating equipment;
- pressure systems;
- lifting and rigging;
- welding and hot work;

## Engineering-authority boundaries
**PASS**

The master prohibits unsupported assumptions of authority to:
- sign or seal regulated engineering documents;
- approve safety-critical design changes;
- substitute critical materials without review;
- alter pressure/load/speed/temperature/torque/safety limits without authorization;
- certify legal compliance without qualification;
- bypass quality hold points or change control;
- modify safety systems, firmware, controller logic, or production configurations without authorization.

The guide correctly directs uncertain scope to an engineer, supervisor, safety lead, quality representative, or other authorized professional.

## Cybersecurity

## Responsible AI
**PASS**

The master permits low-risk AI support for public-source learning, drafting, organization, and fictional practice while requiring human verification for consequential technical facts and decisions.

It explicitly prohibits AI-only reliance for dimensions, tolerances, loads, stresses, materials, pressure/temperature limits, torque, safety factors, lifting limits, maintenance intervals, test acceptance, guarding, LOTO, engineering changes, regulatory requirements, failure conclusions, and production-release decisions.

Confidential/proprietary/export-controlled/credential/production-system information is restricted from unapproved AI tools.

No claim of AI certification or autonomous engineering authority is made.

## Legacy action-plan defect
**PASS — repaired**

The baseline recorded the legacy defect **`Action Plan 1 To 6: False`**.

The controlled English v2 master now contains a complete six-step action plan:
1. Confirm fit and target role.
2. Build the technical foundation.
3. Learn one practical tool chain.
4. Build safety and controlled-work habits.
5. Gain supervised experience and evidence.
6. Apply, interview, and improve.

Each step includes a concrete, checkable milestone. The legacy defect is therefore corrected at the controlled-source level.
