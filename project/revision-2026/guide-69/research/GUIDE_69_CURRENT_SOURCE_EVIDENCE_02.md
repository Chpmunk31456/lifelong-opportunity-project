## Editorial controls derived from research

The revised guide should preserve the distinction between front-desk/guest-service work and duties requiring separate management, security, legal, law-enforcement, medical, technical-maintenance, payment-card, or other specialized authority. It should explicitly address handling of identification documents, payment information, room keys/access credentials, reservation and loyalty data, guest locations/room numbers, incident records, and staff information using least-privilege and employer procedures.

Cybersecurity treatment should cover phishing/social engineering, fraudulent payment or bank-change requests, password/account recovery, QR codes, attachments/links, remote-access requests, credential sharing, and verification before disclosing guest or room information. Responsible-AI language should prohibit entering guest payment-card data, identity documents, credentials, room/access details, confidential incident records, employee records, or other restricted data into unauthorized public AI systems, and should require human review of AI-assisted guest messages, translations, itineraries/recommendations, schedules, training materials, and operational decisions.

Accessibility guidance should focus on respectful communication, accessible formats and channels, escalation, and employer procedures without claiming legal certification or guaranteeing a specific accommodation. Night-audit/bookkeeping references must remain within the employer-assigned front-desk role and must not imply professional accounting authority.
