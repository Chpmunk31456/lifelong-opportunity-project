## Traceability map

### United States occupation and scope

- O*NET occupation **31-9091.00**, limited clinical duties under dentist direction, occupational task framing, and Dental Assistant Registered Apprenticeship title trace to O*NET and Apprenticeship.gov evidence.
- State-dependent entry, education, scope, registration/certification, radiography, and expanded-function cautions trace to BLS and the DANB state-requirements locator, with state dental law/board rules explicitly identified as controlling.
- The master does not convert an O*NET task example or national credential into universal legal authorization.

### United States official wages/outlook

The English master preserves the controlled official values without blending different reference periods:

- O*NET/BLS 2025 median: **$23.11/hour** and **$48,070/year**;
- BLS OOH May 2024 median: **$22.74/hour / $47,300/year**;
- BLS OOH low/high: **$36,190 / $61,780**;
- 2024 employment **381,900**;
- projected 2034 employment **406,300**;
- projected growth **6%**;
- projected numeric increase **24,400**; and
- approximately **52,900** average annual openings.

These values trace to the O*NET/BLS evidence and remain clearly dated rather than averaged together.

### United States private market estimate

The Salary.com August 1, 2026 estimate remains explicitly non-government and separate from official evidence: **$41,739/year / $20/hour**, 25th–75th percentile **$37,596–$46,260**, 10th percentile **$33,824**, and 90th percentile **$50,376**. No guarantee or official-status claim is made.

### Infection prevention and workplace safety

CDC Standard Precautions, PPE, sharps, instrument reprocessing, sterilization, environmental controls, manufacturer instructions, monitoring, and failed-cycle/escalation content trace to the CDC dental infection-prevention sources recorded in Research.

OSHA bloodborne-pathogen, dentistry, exposure-control, sharps, post-exposure, and saliva-as-potentially-infectious context traces to the OSHA sources recorded in Research.

### Privacy, cybersecurity, and AI

HIPAA Privacy/Security references trace to HHS. The master converts those principles into conservative operational controls: authorized/minimum-necessary access, secure systems, incident reporting, protection of images/radiographs and identifiers, and prohibition on public/unauthorized AI use with patient data.

AI limitations trace to the Research safety boundary and remain conservative: no diagnosis, treatment planning, radiographic interpretation for clinical decisions, medication instructions, emergency substitution, infection-control substitution, or replacement of dentist judgment.

### Canada

- NOC **33100** and the need to distinguish dental assisting from dental laboratory assisting trace to the Government of Canada NOC evidence.
- OaSIS **33100.01 — Dental assistants** is used as the specific dental-assistant identity rather than importing laboratory duties.
- Job Bank education and province-specific registration/intra-oral requirements are retained with the exact exceptions recorded in Research.
- Job Bank wage values remain **CAD $21.00 / $27.00 / $35.00 per hour**, with **72.3%** receiving at least one non-wage benefit.
- Canada Student Grant maximum remains **CAD $4,200/year / $525 per month of study** for 2026–27, with eligibility/designated-school/province controls.

No U.S. credential-transfer claim is made.

### Colombia

- The Colombia pathway is anchored to Ministry of Labour OCUPACOL **CUOC 53292 — Auxiliares de salud oral**.
- Occupational examples such as assisting the dentist, instrument sterilization, materials, records, oral-hygiene information, impressions, and radiography under technical procedure/dentist instructions trace to the OCUPACOL evidence.
- The separate higher-scope `Higienistas y asistentes odontológicos` profile is expressly excluded as a source of Dental Assistant authority.
- Decree 1409/2024, auxiliary-health personnel status, certificate distinctions, and **ReTHUS** registration trace to the regulatory evidence.
- SENA Resolution 2057/2025 and the updated **Técnico Laboral en Salud Oral** pathway trace to the SENA evidence.
- Betowa is used only as a current offering/cohort locator.
- Resolution 914/2025 supports regulated reusable-device reprocessing context without creating independent practice authority.

### Colombia duration discrepancy

The Research evidence identifies an unresolved public-source reconciliation problem between current program/cohort metadata and the amended regulatory framework. The English master preserves this limitation and **does not publish one definitive Colombia/SENA program duration**. It directs readers to verify the exact current approved cohort/program record. No unsupported numeric duration is introduced.

### Latin America and Caribbean

OIT/Cinterfor is used only as a regional vocational-training institution locator. The master requires current national health, education, radiation-safety, and dental-profession verification and does not claim cross-border credential transfer.

## URL and source-set control

Every substantive source category in the Research evidence has a corresponding direct URL in the English master's `Current sources` section or in the relevant body section. The master retains official/public sources as the factual backbone, DANB only as a specialized state-requirements locator, and Salary.com only as a supplementary private estimate.

No source in the English master is presented as independent human certification, professional translation certification, accreditation, clinical authorization, legal review, or guaranteed outcome evidence.

## Unsupported-claim review

No unsupported national dental-assistant license, universal radiography permission, guaranteed funding, guaranteed apprenticeship, guaranteed salary, automatic Canadian/U.S./Colombian credential equivalence, or definitive Colombian SENA duration was found in the controlled English master.

**Evidence / Traceability: PASS.** Material occupation, numeric, regulatory, funding, infection-control, privacy, AI, and jurisdictional claims in the English Version 2 master are traceable to the controlled Research evidence and preserve its limitations.
