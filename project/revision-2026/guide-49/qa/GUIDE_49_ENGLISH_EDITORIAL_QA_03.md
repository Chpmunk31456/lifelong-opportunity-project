## Occupation and jurisdiction controls

The Version 2 master is occupation-specific to **Dental Assistant** and preserves the U.S. O*NET identity **31-9091.00**. It does not treat dental assistant, dental hygienist, dental therapist, dental laboratory worker, and dentist as interchangeable occupations.

The draft correctly makes dental-assisting scope jurisdiction-dependent. U.S. radiography and expanded-function statements are framed as functions that require verification of current state law, dental-board rules, training, credential status, delegation/supervision, and employer policy. DANB is used as a specialized locator rather than as a substitute for legally controlling state rules.

Canada is kept separate under NOC **33100**, with the more specific dental-assistant OaSIS identity **33100.01** and explicit province-specific registration/intra-oral controls. The text does not claim that a U.S. credential transfers Canadian authority.

Colombia is anchored to **CUOC 53292 — Auxiliares de salud oral**. The draft explicitly prohibits importing higher-scope functions from the separate `Higienistas y asistentes odontológicos` profile. It preserves the Decree 1409/2024, ReTHUS, SENA Resolution 2057/2025, Betowa, and Resolution 914/2025 boundaries.

## Colombia duration discrepancy control

The English master does **not** publish one definitive national SENA Salud Oral duration. It explains that current public regulatory/program metadata reviewed for this revision is not fully reconciled and directs the reader to verify the exact current official cohort/program record before using duration for an enrollment decision. This is the required fail-closed treatment of the evidence discrepancy.

## Clinical, radiography, infection-prevention, and safety controls

The master clearly states that a Dental Assistant title or certificate does not independently authorize diagnosis, treatment planning, prescribing, radiographic diagnosis, irreversible procedures, anesthesia, or another profession's scope.

Radiography is treated as a regulated function requiring current jurisdiction-specific verification. Infection-prevention content is substantive rather than generic: it covers Standard Precautions, PPE, sharps/exposure controls, contaminated instrument handling, cleaning, packaging, sterilization/reprocessing, monitoring, manufacturer instructions, environmental controls, and escalation after failures or exposures.

CDC and OSHA sources are directly linked. Colombia's regulated reprocessing context is also included without converting it into a claim of independent practice authority.

## Privacy, cybersecurity, and AI controls

The master includes patient-record, HIPAA, ePHI, imaging/radiograph, device, phishing, credential, access-control, and incident-reporting safeguards. It expressly prohibits putting protected/confidential patient data, images, radiographs, histories, insurance identifiers, appointment details, or payment data into public or unauthorized AI systems.

AI is limited to low-risk study and generic administrative practice. It is explicitly prohibited from replacing dentist judgment, radiographic interpretation, diagnosis, treatment planning, emergency decisions, medication instructions, infection-control procedures, sterilizer manufacturer instructions, or approved patient records.

## Education, funding, wage, and employment controls

The draft preserves free-first and employer-support pathways and does not promise funding or an open apprenticeship. Registered Apprenticeship and WIOA are described as conditional/local pathways.

Official U.S. compensation is kept separate from the current private market estimate. The master preserves the controlled official values:

- 2025 BLS/O*NET median: **$23.11/hour / $48,070/year**;
- BLS OOH May 2024 median: **$22.74/hour / $47,300/year**;
- BLS OOH low/high: **$36,190 / $61,780**;
- 2024 employment **381,900**; projected 2034 employment **406,300**;
- projected growth **6%**, numeric increase **24,400**, and approximately **52,900** annual openings.

The Salary.com August 1, 2026 estimate remains separately labeled non-government: **$41,739/year / $20/hour**, with stated percentile values **$37,596–$46,260**, **$33,824**, and **$50,376**.

Canada's Job Bank values remain separately labeled in CAD: **$21.00 / $27.00 / $35.00 per hour**, with **72.3%** benefit prevalence. Canada Student Grant information remains conditional and records the 2026–27 maximum of **$4,200/year / $525 per month of study**.

## Readability, structure, and assurance boundary

The master uses a practical learner-facing structure with more than eighteen substantive H2 sections, short paragraphs, lists, regional headings, a 12-week action plan, accessibility guidance, scam controls, spending pause points, and a consolidated current-source section.

The text avoids placeholders, unsupported guarantees, universal credential claims, cross-border scope transfer, and assertions of independent human or professional certification. The source-and-review note explicitly states the AI-assisted and internal-review boundary.
