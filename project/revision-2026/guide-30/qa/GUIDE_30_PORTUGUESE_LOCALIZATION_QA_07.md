PASS. The localization retains all 18 numbered sections and the controlled sequence of the frozen English source.

## Safety and legal-authority parity

PASS. The Portuguese edition preserves deenergization, qualified-person, LOTO, PPE, permit, inspection, supervision, and stop/escalate boundaries. It does not imply that theory, an online course, an AI answer, an employer title, or training alone authorizes regulated electrical work.

No single U.S. national electrician license is claimed. The distinction between electrician work and broader “técnico em elétrica” titles remains explicit.

## Apprenticeship parity

PASS. Both controlled official-source facts remain intact:

- BLS identifies apprenticeship as the typical on-the-job training route; and
- the current Apprenticeship.gov Occupation Finder for O*NET 47-2111.00 states that the occupation is not currently approved for use in a Registered Apprenticeship Program.

The pt-BR edition therefore requires specific sponsor/program verification before using the registered-apprenticeship label.

## Cybersecurity and AI parity

PASS. The pt-BR edition preserves digital-access controls and prohibits sole reliance on AI for energized work, absence-of-voltage verification, LOTO, conductor/raceway sizing, protective-device selection, grounding/bonding, arc-flash/shock boundaries, hazardous locations, code compliance, permits, inspections, licensing, or professional-scope determinations.

It does not claim professional translation certification, independent human review, accessibility certification, legal review, licensing approval, or accreditation.
