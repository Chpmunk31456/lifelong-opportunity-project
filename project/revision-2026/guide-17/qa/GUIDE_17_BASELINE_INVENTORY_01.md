## Initial control observations

`Bank Teller`, `Member Services Representative`, `Universal Banker`, `Personal Banker`, and similar titles are not interchangeable. Duties can range from routine deposits, withdrawals and account service to account opening, lending referrals, product sales or other regulated activity. The v2 guide must tell readers to verify the exact job duties, employer controls and jurisdiction before assuming what the title authorizes or before paying for training or credentials.

The legacy README already states that employment, income, admission, funding, licensing and certification outcomes are not guaranteed. That safeguard should be preserved and strengthened where needed; no independent human certification, accreditation, regulator approval or professional translation certification may be implied without evidence.
