## High-risk wording controls

- Official wage statistics are not presented as entry-level offers or guarantees.
- The Indeed figure is separated from official BLS data and explicitly labeled non-government.
- U.S., Canadian, and Colombian rules are not transposed across jurisdictions.
- Red Seal is not described as a universal legal license.
- SENA cohort availability and APE vacancies are described as variable/current rather than permanent.
- Section 127 is described as conditional on an employer plan and tax requirements, not as a universal employee entitlement.
- Lockout/tagout material is framed as a safety boundary and does not provide bypass or defeat instructions.
- AI guidance requires human verification and prohibits treating AI output as authoritative hazardous-work instruction.
- No independent human certification, professional translation certification, accessibility certification, legal review, accreditation, or trade-licensing approval is claimed.
