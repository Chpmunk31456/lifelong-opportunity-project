## Controlled-revision gaps

Before Guide 28 can advance to publication, the controlled revision must independently verify and document:

- current U.S. industrial machinery mechanic, machinery maintenance worker, and millwright occupation, wage, outlook, education, training, and work-environment evidence;
- clearly separated current non-government compensation estimates;
- occupation-specific worker-safety and regulated-task boundaries, including lockout/tagout, machine guarding, hazardous energy, electrical hazards, lifting/rigging, confined spaces where applicable, and employer/site procedures;
- free/low-cost training, workforce funding, employer support, apprenticeship, and work-based-learning pathways;
- current Canada occupation classification, wage, apprenticeship/certification, Industrial Mechanic (Millwright) Red Seal or provincial/territorial pathways where applicable, and jurisdiction-specific verification guidance;
- current Colombia SENA and public-employment pathways relevant to industrial maintenance, electromechanics, machinery maintenance, or related occupations;
- a Latin America locator framework that does not transpose U.S., Canadian, or Colombian licensing, certification, worker-safety, or employment rules across jurisdictions;
- ethical AI, privacy, accessibility, computerized maintenance management systems, sensor/condition-monitoring data, and verification guidance appropriate to modern industrial maintenance;
- English evidence traceability and source freeze before localization;
- es-419 and pt-BR parity against the frozen English source;
- trilingual structural, terminology, link, encoding, DOCX, PDF, metadata, rendering, checksum, publication, and release-audit controls.
