3. Existing English QC is primarily publication-layout/rendering evidence and does not establish current factual, source, link, training, funding, apprenticeship, wage/outlook, jurisdictional, translation-parity, metadata, checksum, or current live-link compliance under the present standard.
4. The Spanish and Portuguese packages do not contain standalone QC records, so their current structural, numeric, URL, terminology, safety, regulatory, and assurance parity cannot be presumed.
