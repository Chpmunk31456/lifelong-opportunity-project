## Editorial controls

- Occupation title and U.S. O*NET/SOC identifier 29-2055.00 are explicit.
- The role is consistently described as supervised operating-room work rather than independent medical practice.
- Clinical-scope language prohibits independent diagnosis, prescribing, anesthesia decisions, and unauthorized expansion of duties.
- Sterile field, counts, specimens, sharps, infection prevention, equipment, exposure control, privacy, and incident escalation are presented with employer/facility/jurisdiction boundaries.
- U.S. education language uses the BLS certificate-or-associate's-degree baseline and avoids claiming a universal national license.
- Accreditation and CST certification are presented as credential-path considerations rather than universal guarantees.
- Registered Apprenticeship and WIOA are described as availability/eligibility-dependent pathways.
- Canada is deliberately separated from the U.S. occupation; Operating Room Technician is mapped to Licensed practical nurses, NOC 32101, with regulatory registration and additional OR training.
- Colombia is deliberately separated from the U.S. occupation; OCUPACOL CUOC 22402 Instrumentadores quirúrgicos is treated as a broader professional pathway with no automatic foreign-credential equivalence.
- Latin America coverage directs readers to country-specific education, health, labor, and professional authorities.
- Official BLS compensation is separated from the August 2026 non-government Salary.com estimate.
- AI guidance is restricted to low-risk learning/career uses and explicitly cannot replace clinical instruction, competency validation, approved protocols, manufacturer instructions, or authorized clinical judgment.
- Patient/privacy and cybersecurity controls prohibit unapproved disclosure of PHI, credentials, internal clinical information, and confidential material.
- Scam, total-cost, clinical-placement, accreditation, credential-transfer, and debt cautions are included.
- Human-readable headings, short paragraphs, lists, and plain-language warnings support accessibility and scanability.
- No TODOs, translation placeholders, unsupported guarantees, or claims of independent human certification/accreditation were introduced.
- UTF-8 content is normalized in the Version 2 master; the legacy README BOM is not carried forward.
