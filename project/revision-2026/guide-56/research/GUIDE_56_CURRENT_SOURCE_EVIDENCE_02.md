# Guide 56 — Current-Source Evidence 02

**Guide:** 56 — Nursing Assistant and Patient Care Technician  
**Branch:** `revision/guide-00-100-2026`  
**Research date:** August 19, 2026  
**Gate recommendation:** Research — **PASS**

## Research decision

Guide 56 should use **Nursing Assistant** as the principal cross-source occupation anchor. **Patient Care Technician (PCT)** should be treated as an employer-defined adjacent title rather than represented as a single nationally or internationally uniform regulated occupation. Scope, training, delegated duties, credentialing, and permitted clinical tasks must be verified by jurisdiction and employer.

## United States — official occupation, training, pay, and outlook

### Bureau of Labor Statistics

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Occupational Outlook Handbook profile **Nursing Assistants and Orderlies** states that nursing assistants provide basic care and help patients with activities of daily living. Nursing assistants often need to complete a **state-approved education program** and pass their state's **competency exam** to become licensed or certified; requirements vary by state.

Current BLS figures in the profile:

- Nursing assistant median annual wage, May 2024: **USD $39,530**.
- Orderly median annual wage, May 2024: **USD $37,700**.
- Combined nursing assistant/orderly employment growth projected for 2024–2034: **2%**.
- About **211,800 openings per year** are projected on average over the decade, primarily from replacement needs.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook — Nursing Assistants and Orderlies  
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/nursing-assistants.htm

### Federal nursing-home nurse-aide training context

CMS posted current clarification on April 8, 2026 concerning **Nurse Aide Training Competency Evaluation Program (NATCEP)** and **Nurse Aide Competency Evaluation Program (CEP)** requirements and allowable flexibilities. This is relevant to federally regulated nursing-home nurse-aide pathways, but it should not be misrepresented as a universal credentialing rule for every nursing assistant or PCT job in every setting.

Source: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services — QSO-26-08-NH  
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-safety-standards/quality-safety-oversight-general-information/policy-memos/policy-memos-states-cms-locations/clarification-regarding-nurse-aide-training-competency-evaluation-program-natcep-nurse-aide

WIOA support is **not guaranteed**; eligibility depends on the individual, local workforce area, provider, and program.

## United States — current non-government market estimate

Salary.com reports a **Certified Nursing Assistant** average of approximately **USD $34,701 per year / $17 per hour** as of August 1, 2026, with a stated 25th–75th percentile range of approximately **$31,120–$36,431**.

This is a **non-government market estimate** and uses a different methodology and title definition from BLS. It must remain clearly separated from the official BLS median and must not be averaged together with official wage data.

Source: Salary.com — Certified Nursing Assistant Salary, updated August 1, 2026  
https://www.salary.com/research/salary/benchmark/certified-nursing-assistant-salary

## Canada — official occupation, requirements, and wages

Government of Canada Job Bank classifies nurse aides, orderlies, and patient service associates under **NOC 33102**. Current Job Bank summaries describe these workers as assisting nurses, hospital staff, and physicians in the basic care of patients in hospitals, nursing homes, assisted-care facilities, and other healthcare establishments.

The current Job Bank summary states that this occupation usually requires a college diploma, an apprenticeship of less than two years, or more than six months of on-the-job training. Exact requirements and recognized credentials vary by province, employer, and job title.

Current national wage data, updated November 19, 2025 with a 2023–2024 reference period:

- Low: **CAD $19.00/hour**
- Median: **CAD $24.00/hour**
- High: **CAD $28.84/hour**

Sources:

- Government of Canada Job Bank — Nurse Aide summary, NOC 33102  
  https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/18350/ca
- Government of Canada Job Bank — Wage report, NOC 33102  
  https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/15783

Guide language must avoid implying that a U.S. CNA credential automatically transfers to Canada. Readers should verify provincial requirements, employer expectations, program recognition, language requirements, and any registry or credential rules for the location where they intend to work.

## Colombia — occupation, training, and authorization context

### SENA occupational classification

SENA's Observatorio Laboral y Ocupacional classifies **Auxiliares en enfermería** as CNO **3311**. The current description states that these workers perform well-being and comfort activities and support health promotion, prevention, diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and palliative care **under delegation, supervision, and control of a nursing professional**, within current policy and regulation.

The listed functions include supporting diagnostic processes through observation, preparation, sample collection/remission when delegated; assisting people across the life course; supporting basic comfort; following safe-work practices and biosecurity; and orienting patients/families regarding access to care.

Source: SENA Observatorio Laboral y Ocupacional — Auxiliares en enfermería, CNO 3311  
https://observatorio.sena.edu.co/Clasificacion/CnoDetalle?tags=3311

SENA also notes that mandatory occupational competency standards for health-assistant training programs are defined by applicable health-sector rules, reinforcing that this is not an unregulated generic aide role.

Source: SENA Observatorio — funciones/normas CNO 3311  
https://observatorio.sena.edu.co/Clasificacion/CnoDetalleFunciones?tags=3311

### Current SENA training pathway

SENA Betowa currently lists a titled, in-person **Enfermería** technical program of **2,640 hours**, minimum age 16, subject to center selection and seat availability. The competency list repeatedly conditions clinical activities such as medication support and diagnostic procedures on **delegation and health regulation**.

Source: SENA Betowa — Enfermería, program 113535  
https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/enfermeria?level=2&modality=P&programId=113535

The guide should present Betowa as a current public-training locator and instruct readers to verify the live cohort, city, dates, admission process, and availability before planning around a specific offering.

### ReTHUS

Colombia's Ministry of Health defines ReTHUS as the national health-human-talent registry for people who meet the requirements established for authorized practice of a health profession or occupation. Guide 56 must tell readers pursuing a Colombian health occupation to verify whether and how ReTHUS applies to their exact qualification before employment.

Source: Ministerio de Salud y Protección Social — Registro Único Nacional del Talento Humano en Salud (ReTHUS)  
https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/Paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx

## Latin America and the Caribbean — training/certification locator

OIT/Cinterfor's **Red Saber Cuidar** is a regional network focused on professionalizing care work through vocational education, certification of competencies, recognition of qualifications, and quality standards across Latin America and the Caribbean. In 2026 the network launched work toward a regional qualifications framework for care.

This is useful as a **regional institutional locator and policy context**, not as a license or credential. Readers must identify and verify the national training institution, health authority, competency-certification body, and employer rules in their own country.

## Controlled safety and scope boundaries for Version 2

The English Version 2 master must state clearly that a nursing assistant/PCT must not independently:

- diagnose or determine a medical condition;
- prescribe, select, change, or recommend medication treatment;
- perform an invasive or clinical procedure unless the person is trained, authorized, delegated where required, and acting under the applicable employer/jurisdiction protocol;
- alter, invent, omit, or falsify vital signs, intake/output, observations, specimen information, care records, or safety events;
- use unsafe lifting, transfer, mobility, restraint, infection-control, sharps, or body-fluid practices;
- exceed role boundaries merely because a task appears in an online description or AI answer; or
- use generative AI as a substitute for licensed clinical judgment, patient-specific orders, emergency escalation, privacy requirements, or employer policy.

The guide should emphasize immediate escalation of material changes in condition, abnormal observations, falls, suspected abuse/neglect, medication discrepancies, infection-control breaches, equipment problems, and other safety concerns according to training and employer protocol.

## Privacy and AI boundary

Patient information, photographs, clinical records, identifiers, access credentials, internal schedules, and other protected or confidential health information must not be pasted into public AI systems or unapproved tools. AI may support non-sensitive study practice, terminology review, interview preparation, or generic communication exercises, but any clinical or regulatory information generated by AI must be verified against approved training, employer policy, and current authoritative sources.

## Source-quality notes

- BLS, CMS, CareerOneStop, Canada Job Bank, SENA, Colombia Ministry of Health, and OIT/Cinterfor are treated as official/public authoritative sources for the claims attributed to them.
- Salary.com is retained only as a clearly labeled non-government market estimate.
- Wage figures are historical/current-source observations, not guarantees of starting pay or future earnings.
- The title **Patient Care Technician** is not treated as a single standardized international credential.
- Requirements, registration rules, available training cohorts, funding, and delegated clinical functions can change; the guide must tell readers to recheck authoritative local sources before enrollment or practice.

## Research Helper decision

**PASS.** The evidence is sufficient to reconstruct the controlled English Version 2 master with current U.S., Canada, Colombia, and Latin America coverage; public training/funding locators; official compensation; a separately labeled current market estimate; and explicit clinical, privacy, AI, and assurance boundaries.
