# Guide 55 — Current-Source Evidence 02

**Guide:** 55 — Home Health and Personal Care Aide  
**Stage:** Current-source Research — evidence package  
**Research date:** 2026-08-19  
**Branch:** `revision/guide-00-100-2026`

## Research purpose

This evidence package refreshes the occupational, wage, outlook, training, funding, and jurisdiction-boundary claims needed for the 2026 controlled revision of Guide 55. It distinguishes general personal/home-support work from regulated or agency-specific health-service duties and does not treat one jurisdiction's credential as automatically portable to another.

## United States

### Occupational profile, pay, and outlook — U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, **Home Health and Personal Care Aides**.  
URL: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/home-health-aides-and-personal-care-aides.htm

Controlled facts:
- Median annual wage: **USD $34,900 in May 2024**.
- Equivalent published median hourly wage: approximately **$16.78/hour**.
- Lowest 10 percent: below **$25,600**; highest 10 percent: above **$44,190**.
- Employment: **4,347,700 in 2024**.
- Projected employment: **5,087,500 in 2034**.
- Projected growth: **17% from 2024 to 2034**, much faster than the all-occupations average.
- Numeric employment increase: **739,800**.
- About **765,800 openings per year** are projected on average over the decade, including replacement openings.
- Typical entry-level education is a high school diploma or equivalent, although some positions may not require it; short-term on-the-job training is common.
- Work may be full-time or part-time and may include evenings/weekends depending on client needs.
- Demand is supported by population aging and the movement of long-term care toward home- and community-based settings.

### Medicare-certified home health aide qualification and competency boundary

Primary legal source: Electronic Code of Federal Regulations, **42 CFR § 484.80 — Condition of participation: Home health aide services**.  
Official URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-484/subpart-B/section-484.80

Controlled facts for Medicare-participating home health agencies:
- A qualified home health aide must meet one of the qualification pathways in §484.80(a), including an approved training/competency pathway, qualifying competency evaluation, qualifying state-approved nurse-aide program, or qualifying state licensure program.
- Classroom plus supervised practical training under §484.80(b) must total **at least 75 hours**.
- At least **16 hours of classroom training must precede at least 16 hours of supervised practical training** as part of those 75 hours.
- The training content includes communication, observation/reporting/documentation, vital signs, infection prevention, safe environment, emergencies, personal hygiene/grooming, transfers/ambulation, range of motion/positioning, nutrition/hydration, and skin-condition reporting.
- A competency evaluation is required before an individual furnishes home health services on behalf of an HHA through the competency pathway described in the rule.
- Home health aides must receive **at least 12 hours of in-service training during each 12-month period**.
- Assigned services must be ordered/in the plan of care, permitted under state law, and consistent with the aide's training.
- State law can impose additional requirements; the federal rule is not a universal license for every task or setting.

## Canada

### Occupational mapping — Job Bank / NOC 44101

Source: Government of Canada Job Bank, **Home Support Worker in Canada**.  
URL: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/20667/ca

Controlled facts:
- Closest national occupational grouping: **NOC 44101 — Home support workers, caregivers and related occupations**.
- Work includes personal care and companionship for seniors, persons with disabilities, and convalescent clients, generally in the client's residence.
- Workers may be employed by home-care/support agencies or private households, or may be self-employed.
- Job Bank summarizes entry requirements as usually a secondary school diploma or several weeks of on-the-job training; employer and provincial requirements vary.
- Canada-wide prospects are explicitly reported as varying by province/territory.

- Canada median: **CAD $20.50/hour**.

### Canada outlook

## Colombia

### Distinguish caregiving from regulated health-auxiliary practice

Source: Ministerio de Salud y Protección Social, **Formación Talento Humano en Salud**.  
URL: https://www2.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/Paginas/formacion-talento-humano-salud.aspx

Controlled facts:
- Colombia's Educación para el Trabajo y el Desarrollo Humano includes technical labor training and may lead to a **Certificado de Aptitud Ocupacional**.
- In the health area, training and practice are subject to the rules governing **Talento Humano en Salud**.
- Occupational profiles for **Auxiliares en Salud** support and complement comprehensive health care, including activities linked to promotion, prevention, diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and palliation.
- Therefore, Guide 55 must not imply that general caregiver/personal-care experience automatically authorizes regulated clinical or health-auxiliary duties in Colombia.

Additional official context: Ministerio de Salud, **Profesiones y ocupaciones / Talento Humano en Salud**.  
URL: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/Paginas/talento-humano-salud.aspx

### Colombian caregiver framework and competency recognition

Legal source: **Ley 2297 de 2023**, concerning caregivers or personal assistants of persons with disabilities.  
SENA Normograma: https://normograma.sena.edu.co/compilacion/docs/ley_2297_2023.htm

Controlled guidance:
- The law establishes a national orientation/training framework for caregivers or personal assistants of persons with disabilities and provides for evaluation/certification of relevant competencies within Colombia's National Qualifications System.
- The law's training framework is not itself a blanket authorization for regulated clinical tasks.

SENA current evidence: **Certificatón para cuidadores “Manos que Cuidan”**, March 20, 2026.  
URL: https://www.sena.edu.co/es-co/Noticias/Paginas/noticia.aspx?IdNoticia=9127

Current evidence:
- SENA used its Evaluation and Certification of Labor Competencies process to recognize caregiver experience.
- The March 2026 campaign offered certification against competency standards including care according to activities of daily living/autonomy and accompaniment according to spiritual/emotional preferences.
- SENA stated a minimum of six months of experience for that specific campaign.
- The campaign's enrollment window has passed, so the guide must treat it as evidence of a pathway, not as an open current enrollment offer.

SENA later evidence: July 28, 2026 certification activity.  
URL: https://www.sena.edu.co/es-co/Noticias/Paginas/noticia.aspx?IdNoticia=9692

Controlled guidance:
- SENA continued caregiver competency certification activity in 2026, supporting use of SENA/ECCL as a free-first verification pathway.
- Readers should check SENA's current offering rather than assuming a specific Certificatón is open.

## Safety, privacy, accessibility, and AI controls for the English master

Guide 55 should explicitly cover:
- safe body mechanics, transfers, fall prevention, infection prevention, emergency escalation, and employer/client-specific care plans;
- reporting rather than diagnosing changes in condition unless the worker separately holds a credential that authorizes a clinical act;
- medication assistance only within applicable law, employer policy, care plan, and credential scope;
- confidentiality and minimum-necessary handling of health/personal information;
- no entry of identifiable client/patient data into public generative-AI tools unless the employer has expressly authorized a compliant system and use case;
- AI may assist with study, plain-language practice, résumé drafting, scheduling templates, or non-identifiable learning scenarios, but must not replace clinical judgment, emergency protocols, supervision, or care-plan instructions;
- accessibility planning for lifting/mobility requirements, sensory load, communication, reading/writing demands, dysgraphia, neurodiversity, and assistive technology, without promising that every role can accommodate every limitation.

## Research gate conclusion

**Research finding: PASS.**

The occupation has a defensible 2026 evidence base for the United States, Canada, and Colombia. The controlled revision must preserve jurisdiction distinctions, especially between general personal/home support, Medicare-certified U.S. home health aide requirements, Canada's NOC 44101 framework, and Colombia's caregiver versus regulated health-auxiliary boundaries. Wage figures must remain dated and currency-labelled; funding/training pathways must be presented as locators subject to eligibility, not guarantees.
