## Link and claim controls

- Government and accreditor links are presented as direct verification sources rather than third-party summaries.

- Dynamic facts are date-labeled where material.
- Cross-jurisdiction statements avoid automatic credential equivalence.
- No source is used to imply a broader clinical scope than it supports.
- Funding resources are described as eligibility pathways, not guaranteed awards.
