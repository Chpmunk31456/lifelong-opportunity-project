## Baseline defects and controlled-revision needs

1. **No auditable Markdown source master is present in the legacy language trees.** The controlled revision therefore requires reconstruction into a traceable English working master before localization.
2. **Legacy QA does not meet the current 2026 controlled-revision standard.** The existing README/QC records do not establish current-source evidence, jurisdiction-specific credential controls, trilingual structural/source parity, controlled wage/funding evidence, all-page publication QA, metadata, or SHA-256 sealing.
3. **Clinical scope must be fail-closed.** A U.S. occupational therapy assistant is not an occupational therapist. The revised guide must distinguish the assistant role from evaluation, intervention-plan design, discharge decisions, and other responsibilities reserved to the occupational therapist or otherwise controlled by law and supervision requirements.
4. **Credential pathway must be current.** U.S. education must be checked against ACOTE accreditation and NBCOT COTA eligibility/certification requirements, with state licensure requirements treated separately.
5. **Cross-jurisdiction title equivalence must not be assumed.** Canada groups occupational therapy assistants within NOC 32109, while Colombia regulates `Terapia Ocupacional` as a university-level profession under Ley 949 de 2005. The U.S. OTA title must not be translated or represented as if it conferred Colombian `Terapeuta Ocupacional` professional status.
6. **Current labor-market and funding evidence must be refreshed.** U.S. BLS data, Canada Job Bank data, current non-government market estimates, education funding, workforce funding, and Latin America/Colombia pathways require current verification before English editorial reconstruction.
