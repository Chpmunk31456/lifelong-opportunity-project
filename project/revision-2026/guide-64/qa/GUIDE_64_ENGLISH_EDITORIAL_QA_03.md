## Scope reviewed

The controlled English Version 2 master was reviewed for occupation framing, grammar, spelling, readability, accessibility, jurisdictional distinctions, compensation labeling, scope boundaries, funding language, cybersecurity, privacy, safeguarding, program governance, financial controls, responsible AI, and assurance language.

## Findings

- The title is explicitly treated as non-standardized and is not falsely equated with a protected profession or universal management classification.
- U.S. O*NET-SOC 11-9151.00 and Canadian NOC 40030 are labeled as broad management benchmarks, not guaranteed coordinator classifications.
- U.S. official BLS/O*NET figures remain separate from current non-government Salary.com and ZipRecruiter estimates.
- No national Colombian wage figure was invented; SENA pathways and duty evidence are presented as pathways/evidence rather than universal credential requirements.
- Funding language for WIOA and employer support is conditional and does not imply guaranteed funding.
- Apprenticeship guidance directs readers to live searches rather than claiming a universal pathway.
- Regulated social work, clinical, legal, benefits-adjudication, procurement, grant, fiduciary, safeguarding, privacy, cybersecurity, and management authority boundaries are explicit.
- Accessibility and disability-inclusion guidance is practical without claiming legal certification.
- Responsible-AI guidance prohibits unapproved use of sensitive data and requires human review.
- The assurance boundary avoids independent human certification, professional translation certification, accreditation, legal review, accessibility certification, or employment/wage guarantees.
- No unresolved placeholders, encoding defects, or obvious contradictory claims were found in the reviewed master.

## Editorial decision

The English Version 2 master is suitable to advance to Evidence/Traceability QA.
