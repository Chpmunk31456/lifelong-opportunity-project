# Guide 64 — Current-Source Research Evidence

**Occupation:** Community Service Program Coordinator  
**Guide:** 64  
**Branch:** `revision/guide-00-100-2026`  
**Review date:** 2026-08-20  
**Gate:** Current-source Research

## Research decision

The title **Community Service Program Coordinator** is not a single protected or uniformly classified occupation across jurisdictions. The controlled revision therefore uses a bounded comparison approach rather than pretending that every coordinator role is equivalent to a manager, licensed social worker, case manager, grant officer, or government official.

For United States labor-market anchoring, the closest broad official management-level occupation is **O*NET-SOC 11-9151.00 — Social and Community Service Managers**, because O*NET defines that occupation as planning, directing, or coordinating social-service programs or community-outreach organizations. However, many real-world *program coordinator* jobs are below full manager level. The Version 2 guide must explain this distinction and tell readers to compare actual duties, authority, education, budget responsibility, and supervision before using manager-level wage data as a personal expectation.

For Canada, the closest broad management comparison is **NOC 40030 — Managers in social, community and correctional services**. Again, a coordinator title is not automatically a NOC 40030 management role.

For Colombia and broader Latin America, the evidence supports public training and community-program pathways, but no single national occupation/wage mapping was found that cleanly makes every Community Service Program Coordinator equivalent to the U.S. or Canadian management classifications. The guide must therefore avoid inventing a Colombia occupational code or national salary figure.

## United States — official occupational evidence

### O*NET / BLS occupational scope

O*NET OnLine classifies **Social and Community Service Managers as 11-9151.00**. O*NET describes the occupation as planning, directing, or coordinating activities of a social-service program or community-outreach organization and may include budget, policy, staffing, program-quality, and external-relations responsibilities.

Relevant O*NET tasks include:

- establishing and overseeing administrative procedures;
- directing activities of professional, technical, and volunteer staff;
- evaluating program quality and resource use;
- planning and administering program budgets;
- coordinating special events or programs; and
- representing organizations externally.

O*NET places the occupation in **Job Zone Four — Considerable Preparation Needed**. It states that many occupations in this zone require a four-year bachelor's degree, though some do not, and that considerable related experience is typically needed.

**Official source:** O*NET OnLine, 11-9151.00, updated 2026.  
https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/11-9151.00

### Official U.S. compensation and outlook

O*NET reports Bureau of Labor Statistics data for 11-9151.00:

- **2025 median hourly wage: USD $38.65**
- **2025 median annual wage: USD $80,390**
- **2024 employment: approximately 219,800 workers**
- **Projected growth, 2024–2034: 5% to 6%**
- **Projected annual openings over the decade: approximately 18,600**

These figures apply to the broader **Social and Community Service Managers** occupation, not automatically to every coordinator-level role. The guide must label them as an upper/broader occupational benchmark when the actual job has management-level duties.

**Official source:** O*NET OnLine using BLS wage and projections data.  
https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-9151.00

## United States — current non-government coordinator estimates

Current private-market estimates help illustrate why a coordinator title should not simply inherit manager-level BLS wages.

### Salary.com

Salary.com reported that, as of **August 1, 2026**, the average U.S. salary for the narrower title **Community Program Coordinator** was approximately:

- **USD $66,947 per year**
- approximately **USD $32 per hour**
- reported 25th–75th percentile range: approximately **$59,855–$75,965**

This is a **non-government compensation estimate**, not BLS/O*NET data, and its methodology and title matching differ from official occupational statistics.

**Non-government source:** Salary.com, Community Program Coordinator Salary, updated August 1, 2026.  
https://www.salary.com/research/salary/recruiting/community-program-coordinator-salary

### ZipRecruiter

ZipRecruiter reported, as of **July 20, 2026**, an average U.S. pay estimate for **Community Program Coordinator** of approximately:

- **USD $54,966 per year**
- approximately **USD $26.43 per hour**

This is also a **non-government job-market estimate** and must remain clearly separated from official BLS/O*NET statistics.

**Non-government source:** ZipRecruiter, Community Program Coordinator Salary, July 2026.  
https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Community-Program-Coordinator-Salary

### Current posting evidence

Current Indeed postings show coordinator jobs spanning nonprofit services, healthcare, volunteer programs, community engagement, and local government. Duties commonly include program implementation, outreach, volunteer coordination, documentation, metrics, events, partner relationships, and grant-funded administrative work. Examples also show wide compensation variation by employer and location.

This posting evidence supports a guide recommendation to review **15–20 local jobs** before paying for training or relying on a national salary estimate.

**Non-government source:** Indeed, Community Programs Coordinator jobs, current 2026 listings.

## Canada — official occupational evidence

### NOC 40030 comparison

Government of Canada Job Bank classifies **Managers in social, community and correctional services as NOC 40030**. The group plans, organizes, directs, controls, and evaluates programs and activities of social-service and community agencies, correctional institutions, counselling departments, labour organizations, professional associations, political parties, and non-governmental organizations.

Job Bank states that these roles typically involve management responsibility.

**Official source:** Government of Canada Job Bank, NOC 40030 summary/description.  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/1813/ca

### Canadian requirements

Job Bank states that managers in NOC 40030 usually require a **master's degree in a social-science or administrative discipline** and several years of experience in a related occupation, while managers of associations and membership organizations require extensive related experience.

This requirement is important because a coordinator posting with narrower administrative duties may not be equivalent to NOC 40030. The guide must tell readers to verify the actual job requirements rather than assuming a master's degree is universally required for every coordinator title.

**Official source:** Government of Canada Job Bank, requirements for NOC 40030.  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/1797/ca

### Canadian official wages

Job Bank reports national wages for NOC 40030, updated **November 19, 2025**, using a 2023–2024 Labour Force Survey reference period:

- **CAD $27.00/hour low**
- **CAD $43.96/hour median**
- **CAD $71.43/hour high**

These are management-level NOC 40030 wages and should not be presented as guaranteed coordinator pay.

**Official source:** Government of Canada Job Bank wage report.  
https://www.on.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/1813

## Colombia — current public pathways

### SENA community-program and community-management evidence

Current SENA evidence shows active public pathways related to community program delivery, coordination, outreach, participation, and local development.

SENA Betowa currently lists **Gestión comunitaria del riesgo de desastres**, a technical program of **1,920 hours**, with competencies including coordinating campaigns, diagnosing community risk, promoting prevention actions, working under protocols, and using information tools.

**Official source:** SENA Betowa — Gestión comunitaria del riesgo de desastres.  
https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/gestion-comunitaria-del-riesgo-de-desastres?level=2&modality=P&offertype=company&programId=177814

SENA Betowa also lists **Organización comunitaria sostenible**, focused on generating education, participation, and environmental-management processes according to territorial needs and policies.

**Official source:** SENA Betowa — Organización comunitaria sostenible.  
https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/organizacion-comunitaria-sostenible?offertype=company&programId=98528

A current SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo posting documents community-management duties that include programming workshops and outreach activities, preparing reports, supporting local authorities and communities, and coordinating participation among producers, associations, and local actors. This is useful as real-world Colombia duty evidence, but it is **not** a national classification or universal qualification standard.

**Official source:** SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo, current community-management vacancy evidence.  
https://agenciapublicadeempleo.sena.edu.co/spe-web/spe/demanda/solicitud-sintesis/4052296

### SENA 2026 community-work contracting evidence

SENA's 2025/2026 public guidance for CampeSENA/Full Popular documents community-work roles involving organization strengthening, alliances, community accompaniment, action-plan execution, and access to SENA services. It allows varying education levels depending on experience and specifies related community-work experience for certain service contracts.

The compensation ceilings in that circular relate to specific SENA service-contract guidance and **must not be treated as a national salary benchmark** for Community Service Program Coordinators.

**Official source:** SENA Circular 287 of 2025, applicable controlled evidence for 2026 community-work contracting.  
https://normograma.sena.edu.co/compilacion/docs/circular_sena_0287_2025.htm

### Colombia compensation boundary

No directly comparable current national official wage series was identified that cleanly maps a generic **Community Service Program Coordinator** title to one Colombian occupation. The Version 2 guide must therefore avoid inventing a national Colombian salary. Readers should compare current SENA APE, public-sector, nonprofit, foundation, university, healthcare, and municipal postings in the city or department where they intend to work.

## Latin America and the Caribbean — public training locator

OIT/Cinterfor maintains a regional network and observatory of vocational-training institutions across Latin America and the Caribbean. Its statistics and member-institution resources can be used to identify national public vocational-training systems, after which readers should verify the exact local program, cost, recognition, and job relevance directly with the national institution.

This regional source is a **training-system locator**, not a universal credential or salary authority for Community Service Program Coordinators.

**Official/regional source:** OIT/Cinterfor vocational-training observatory.  
https://www.cinterfor.org/statsfp

### Apprenticeships and work-based learning

There is no universal Registered Apprenticeship requirement for Community Service Program Coordinators. Apprenticeship.gov provides a live finder for registered and employer-sponsored opportunities. Readers should search by relevant keywords and location rather than assuming that a coordinator apprenticeship exists locally.

**Official source:** Apprenticeship.gov Finder.  
https://www.apprenticeship.gov/finder/listings

### Employer-supported development

Because coordinator roles often depend on organization-specific systems and grant/program rules, the guide should advise readers to ask employers about:

- paid onboarding and program-specific training;
- safeguarding and mandatory-reporting training where relevant;
- grant-compliance and reporting training;
- budgeting, procurement, and expense-control procedures;
- volunteer-management training;
- accessibility and disability-inclusion training;
- privacy, records-retention, and cybersecurity training;
- project-management tools;
- tuition assistance or course reimbursement; and
- internal pathways from assistant/coordinator to senior coordinator or program manager.

Repayment conditions for employer-funded education should be obtained in writing.

## Scope, safeguarding, privacy, finance, and governance controls for Version 2

The controlled English master must explicitly distinguish coordination from authority. A Community Service Program Coordinator should not be presented as automatically authorized to:

- practice licensed social work, psychotherapy, medicine, nursing, law, or another regulated profession;
- make government-benefit eligibility decisions unless that authority is formally assigned;
- promise funding, housing, treatment, immigration outcomes, grants, or public benefits;
- sign contracts, approve purchases, move restricted funds, alter grant budgets, or bind the organization unless formally delegated;
- make safeguarding or emergency decisions outside approved policy and law;
- access client, donor, volunteer, employee, or beneficiary records without a legitimate role-based need; or
- use public AI tools with confidential, personal, health, legal, safeguarding, donor, applicant, or restricted program information.

The guide should include practical controls for:

- least-privilege access and multifactor authentication;
- approved case/program databases;
- factual, proportionate recordkeeping;
- retention and secure disposal;
- segregation of duties for payments and purchasing;
- grant and restricted-fund documentation;
- conflict-of-interest disclosure;
- vendor and procurement controls;
- accessibility of events, forms, communications, and digital services;
- incident escalation; and
- human review of AI-assisted drafts or summaries.

## Research QA conclusion

The evidence set is sufficient to reconstruct a controlled English Version 2 master while preserving important jurisdictional differences:

- U.S. management benchmark: O*NET-SOC **11-9151.00**;
- U.S. official median: **$38.65/hour, $80,390/year** for the broader manager occupation;
- current private coordinator estimates kept separate from official data;
- Canada broad management comparison: **NOC 40030**;
- Canada national NOC 40030 wages: **CAD $27.00 / $43.96 / $71.43 per hour**;
- Colombia: active SENA community-program pathways and current duty evidence, with **no invented national wage mapping**;
- Latin America: OIT/Cinterfor as a public vocational-training-system locator;
- U.S. funding: WIOA/American Job Center locators with no funding guarantee;
- apprenticeship: search live opportunities rather than claiming a universal pathway;
- explicit program-governance, privacy, safeguarding, finance, accessibility, cybersecurity, and responsible-AI boundaries.

**Result: PASS.** Current-source Research is complete. The next controlled gate is English Editorial / Version 2 reconstruction.
