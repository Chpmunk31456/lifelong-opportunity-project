## Existing publication state

The live revision branch contains a legacy Guide 26 package under `26-automotive-service-technician-and-mechanic/` with English, neutral-Latin-American-Spanish, and Brazilian-Portuguese edition directories, plus a reference directory and guide README.

The English edition README identifies the existing package as **version 1.0**, published July 2026, with DOCX and searchable PDF files present. Its review notice explicitly says exact source equivalence and independent human linguistic review should not be assumed unless separately documented.

The existing reference package includes `sources.md` and `quality.md`. The v1.0 source record already points to BLS automotive-service occupational data, Apprenticeship.gov, IRS education-assistance resources, the Department of Education accreditation database, CareerOneStop license information, and OSHA worker-safety resources. These are baseline inputs only and must be reverified for the controlled v2.0 revision.

## Controlled-revision gaps

Before Guide 26 can advance to publication, the controlled revision must independently verify and document:

- current U.S. occupation, wage, outlook, education, and training evidence;
- clearly separated current non-government compensation estimates;
- automotive-specific safety and regulated-task boundaries, including refrigerant-service requirements where applicable;
- free/low-cost training, workforce funding, employer support, and apprenticeship/work-based-learning pathways;
- current Canada occupation classification, wage, certification/apprenticeship, and provincial/territorial verification guidance;
- current Colombia SENA and public-employment pathways;
- a Latin America locator framework that does not transpose U.S., Canadian, or Colombian rules across jurisdictions;
- ethical AI, privacy, accessibility, high-voltage/EV awareness, and verification guidance appropriate to automotive service;
- English evidence traceability and source freeze before localization;
- es-419 and pt-BR parity against the frozen English source;
- trilingual technical, link, encoding, DOCX, PDF, metadata, rendering, checksum, publication, and release-audit controls.

**PASS.** The legacy package is suitable as an input for controlled reconstruction but is not itself sufficient evidence of completion under the 2026 expanded opportunity standard. Sequential work may advance to current-source research.
