# Guide 26 — Current Source Evidence

**Occupation:** Automotive Service Technician and Mechanic  
**Controlled branch:** `revision/guide-00-100-2026`  
**Evidence review date:** 2026-08-10  
**Gate:** Research Helper

## Purpose

This record freezes the current-source research inputs required before the English v2.0 editorial reconstruction of Guide 26. It distinguishes official labor-market and training evidence from non-government market estimates, records jurisdiction-specific licensing/certification boundaries, and identifies free or subsidized training pathways without implying that any credential guarantees employment or income.

## United States — official occupational evidence

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — Occupational Outlook Handbook

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, *Automotive Service Technicians and Mechanics*.  
URL: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm

Current facts captured for the revised guide:

- 2024 median pay: **$49,670 per year / $23.88 per hour**.
- Employment in 2024: **805,600 jobs**.
- Projected employment change, 2024–2034: **4%**, about as fast as average.
- Projected numerical change, 2024–2034: **33,600 jobs**.
- Approximately **70,000 openings per year** are projected on average over the decade, largely from replacement needs.
- Typical entry-level education is a **postsecondary nondegree award**, although BLS notes that some workers enter with a high school diploma and learn on the job.
- BLS specifically notes increasing need to work with electronic systems, sensors, computerized diagnostics, and electric-vehicle systems.
- BLS identifies safety and environmental requirements around high-voltage EV systems and hazardous-material disposal.

Editorial control: these are national U.S. statistics, not promises of individual earnings. State, metro, employer, specialization, flat-rate practices, certifications, experience, and overtime can materially change pay.

## United States — current non-government market estimate

### Indeed Career Explorer

Source: Indeed, *Automotive technician salary in United States, 2026*.  
URL: https://www.indeed.com/career/automotive-technician/salaries

Snapshot available during this evidence review:

- Average base salary: **$28.61/hour**.
- Reported low/high range: **$17.01–$48.11/hour**.
- Indeed states the estimate was based on approximately **53,500 salaries from job postings over the prior 36 months**, updated **July 20, 2026**.
- Indeed separately reported entry-level automotive technician average base pay around **$24.09/hour** (updated July 11, 2026) and senior automotive technician average base pay around **$35.59/hour** (updated July 12, 2026).

Editorial control: Indeed is a **non-government market estimate**, not an official wage series. It must be labeled separately from BLS data and should not be averaged together with BLS figures.

## United States — apprenticeship, funding, and low-cost pathways

### Apprenticeship.gov

Source: U.S. Department of Labor, Apprenticeship.gov.  
URL: https://www.apprenticeship.gov/

BLS directs jobseekers to Apprenticeship.gov for apprenticeship opportunities. Apprenticeship.gov also listed 2026 automotive-service pre-apprenticeship activity, including a SUNY Canton no-cost automotive service technician pre-apprenticeship supported by a SUNY apprenticeship grant and a registered-apprenticeship employer partner.

Editorial control: availability is local and time-limited. The guide should direct readers to search current Registered Apprenticeship listings rather than imply that a specific 2026 cohort remains open.

### Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA)

Source: U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, TEGL 08-19 and WIOA Eligible Training Provider guidance.  
URL: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/advisories/training-and-employment-guidance-letter-no-08-19

Relevant funding mechanisms include occupational-skills training, on-the-job training, incumbent-worker training, programs combining workplace training with related instruction, skill upgrading/retraining, customized training, and Registered Apprenticeship. Eligible individuals may receive training support through local workforce systems and Eligible Training Provider Lists / Individual Training Accounts, subject to local eligibility and funding.

Editorial control: describe WIOA as **potential assistance**, not guaranteed funding. Readers should verify eligibility with their local American Job Center before paying tuition.

## United States — regulatory and safety boundary

### EPA Clean Air Act Section 609 — motor vehicle air conditioning

Source: U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, *Section 609 Technician Training and Certification Programs*.  
URL: https://www.epa.gov/mvac/section-609-technician-training-and-certification-programs

EPA states that a person who repairs or services a motor-vehicle air-conditioning system for compensation must be properly trained and certified under Clean Air Act Section 609 by an EPA-approved program. EPA also requires compliant refrigerant-servicing equipment and practices.

Editorial control: the guide must not frame general automotive training as permission to service regulated refrigerant systems. Certification/licensing requirements must be checked for the specific jurisdiction and task.

## Canada — occupation, wage, and credential pathway

### Government of Canada Job Bank

Source: Government of Canada Job Bank, Automotive Service Technician, NOC **72410**.  
Representative national wage page: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/14799/ca

Current national wage evidence available in the 2026 Job Bank pages reviewed:

- Canada low: **C$19.00/hour**.
- Canada median: **C$29.89/hour**.
- Canada high: **C$43.27/hour**.
- Wage data were updated **November 19, 2025** and generally reference 2023–2024 Labour Force Survey data.
- Job Bank reports that **85%** of workers nationally in this occupation receive at least one non-wage benefit.

Editorial control: provincial and regional figures differ materially. The guide should direct readers to the current Job Bank page for their province/territory and not present the national range as a local guarantee.

### Red Seal Program

Source: Government of Canada / Red Seal Program, *Automotive Service Technician*.  
URL: https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/auto-serv-tech.shtml

- Automotive Service Technician is a designated Red Seal trade associated with **NOC 72410**.
- The current Red Seal description includes conventional mechanical systems plus hybrid and electric vehicle systems, ADAS, electronics, HVAC, restraints, steering, braking, drivetrains, and diagnostics.
- A Red Seal endorsement is obtained through the provincial/territorial trade-certification system and successful completion of the Red Seal examination where applicable.

Editorial control: apprenticeship, compulsory-trade rules, certification, and exam eligibility vary by province/territory. Readers must verify requirements with the relevant provincial or territorial apprenticeship/trades authority.

## Colombia — training and employment pathways

### SENA training

Source: Servicio Nacional de Aprendizaje (SENA), Betowa training catalogue.  
Representative 2026 program: *Gestión del mantenimiento de automotores*.  
URL: https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/gestion-del-mantenimiento-de-automotores

A 2026 SENA offering reviewed for this evidence record showed an in-person automotive-maintenance program with a July 2026 start and October 2028 end date. SENA states that it serves learners through regional training centers nationwide.

Editorial control: cohorts, locations, modalities, admission windows, and program names change. The guide should direct readers to search the current Betowa/SENA catalogue before making plans or payments. SENA public training should be prioritized as a free-first pathway where an appropriate cohort is available.

### Agencia Pública de Empleo SENA — current vacancy examples

Source: SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo.  
URL: https://agenciapublicadeempleo.sena.edu.co/

Current 2026 search results reviewed included automotive technician/service-technician vacancies in multiple Colombian cities with advertised monthly salary bands commonly around **COP 1.5M–2.5M**, alongside some higher-skill examples around **COP 4.0M–4.5M**. Requirements varied by location and experience.

Editorial control: these are **individual vacancy examples, not an official national wage statistic**. They may expire quickly and must be labeled as market examples only. The revised guide must not convert them into a promised or national-average salary.

## Latin America — pathway framing

For Latin America outside Colombia, the revised guide should use a jurisdiction-first approach:

- prioritize national public vocational-training services, ministries of labor/education, public employment services, manufacturer/dealer apprenticeships, and employer-sponsored training;
- verify whether automotive repair, emissions inspection, refrigerant handling, electrical work, high-voltage EV work, or roadworthiness inspection requires local licensing or certification;
- treat private-school prices and salary websites as secondary evidence only;
- never imply that a U.S., Canadian, SENA, ASE, manufacturer, or other credential automatically transfers across borders.

The guide should point readers to official national training and employment portals for their country and encourage free/low-cost public options before private tuition.

## Required editorial additions for English v2.0

The English reconstruction must incorporate the following evidence-backed controls:

1. Separate **official U.S. BLS wage/outlook data** from **Indeed's current non-government estimate**.
2. Explain common training routes: community/technical college, employer training, Registered Apprenticeship, pre-apprenticeship, and structured on-the-job progression.
3. Include **WIOA / American Job Center** funding checks before self-funding U.S. training.
4. Include **Canada NOC 72410**, Job Bank wage lookup, apprenticeship/provincial requirements, and Red Seal pathway.
5. Include **SENA/Betowa** and **SENA Agencia Pública de Empleo** as Colombia-first training/employment pathways.
6. Include an explicit **Latin America jurisdiction-check** rather than generalizing one country's rules.
7. Address EV/hybrid, high-voltage, ADAS, diagnostics, digital service information, and environmental/safety requirements as modern skill areas.
8. Preserve a clear boundary around regulated work, especially **EPA Section 609 MVAC work in the United States** and local licensing/certification elsewhere.
9. Encourage employer-paid certifications, tuition assistance, apprenticeships, public workforce funding, grants/scholarships, and used/shared tool strategies before taking on debt.
10. State that tools can be a substantial worker expense in some shops and that readers should verify employer tool policies before accepting a role or buying equipment.
