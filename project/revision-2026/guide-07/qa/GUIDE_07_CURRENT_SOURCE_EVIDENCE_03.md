## United States — official occupational evidence

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, Customer Service Representatives:

- Median hourly wage: **US$20.59 in May 2024**.
- Lowest 10 percent: below **US$14.75/hour**; highest 10 percent: above **US$30.16/hour**.
- Employment projection: **-5% from 2024 to 2034**.
- Replacement demand: about **341,700 openings per year** on average over the decade despite projected occupational decline.
- Typical entry: **high school diploma** plus **on-the-job training**.
- Source: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/customer-service-representatives.htm

Publication rule: describe the decline and replacement openings together. Do not turn a national median into a promised starting wage or local wage.

## Canada — official occupational evidence

Government of Canada Job Bank maps customer-service/contact-centre work to **Other customer and information services representatives, NOC 64409**.

National wage table updated **2025-11-19**, reference period **2023–2024**, Labour Force Survey:

- Low: **C$16.00/hour**
- Median: **C$22.00/hour**
- High: **C$33.14/hour**
- Job Bank reports **84.1%** of workers in this occupation receive at least one non-wage benefit.
- Typical requirements: secondary school usually required; some college/post-secondary programs or clerical/sales experience may be required.
- Job Bank states the occupation is **not regulated in Canada** according to its records.
- Sources: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/15201 and https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/15201/ca

Publication rule: identify the NOC explicitly and avoid implying that every job titled Customer Service Specialist maps perfectly to NOC 64409.

## Employer-supported education — United States

IRS Fact Sheet **FS-2026-10 (April 2026)** states that qualifying benefits under an Internal Revenue Code Section 127 educational-assistance program may be excluded from gross income up to **US$5,250 per calendar year for 2025 and 2026**. The IRS states the amount is adjusted for cost-of-living increases for taxable years beginning after 2026.

Source: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/updates-to-frequently-asked-questions-about-educational-assistance-programs

Publication rule: present this as a U.S. tax rule for qualifying employer plans, not as a universal employer benefit, scholarship, or guaranteed funding source.

## Colombia — official low-cost/public training pathway

SENA's Betowa catalogue currently lists **Servicio al cliente** as a **48-hour complementary, in-person special course**, with minimum age shown as 14+ and enrollment requirements including current SOFIA Plus information and completion of the program.

Source: https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/servicio-al-cliente?modality=P&offertype=open&programId=116454

Publication rule: describe SENA as an official Colombian training pathway whose offerings, modality, cohorts, locations and availability must be checked at enrollment time. Do not promise a seat, completion, employment, salary, or permanent availability.

## Controlled drafting requirements

The English v2 master must:

1. Separate official occupational wage evidence from any current non-government market estimates.
2. Explain automation and AI as task-changing forces without claiming the occupation is disappearing entirely.
3. Cover phone, chat, email, ticketing, CRM, accessibility, de-escalation, privacy, authentication, payment-data boundaries, fraud/social-engineering escalation and approved AI-tool use.

5. Include U.S., Canada, Latin America and Colombia localization guidance rather than assuming U.S. rules apply globally.
6. Require local verification of wage, labor, privacy, recording, consumer-protection and payment-handling rules.
7. Preserve explicit non-guarantee and stop/escalate language.
