## Occupational-risk classification

Guide 19 is a higher-risk legal-career guide because paralegals can perform substantive legal work under delegated authority, while the boundary between permitted paralegal work, unauthorized practice of law, licensed limited legal services, court/agency representation, title regulation, and voluntary certification varies by jurisdiction.

The 2026 revision therefore requires explicit fail-closed controls for:

- lawyer or authorized-professional supervision where required;
- jurisdiction-specific scope-of-practice rules;
- prohibition on implying independent lawyer authority;
- distinction between an education certificate, individual certification, state regulation/registration, and limited legal-service licensure;
- protection of client confidentiality, privilege, work product, credentials, filings, and matter information;
- responsible use of generative AI in legal work;
- verification of education-program recognition before payment;
- no assumption that U.S., Canadian, Colombian, or other Latin American occupational models are interchangeable.
