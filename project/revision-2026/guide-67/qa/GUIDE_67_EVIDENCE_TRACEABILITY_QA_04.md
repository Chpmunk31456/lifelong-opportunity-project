## Food-safety, allergen, and worker-safety traceability

PASS. The FDA **2022 Food Code** remains described as a model code whose adoption and enforcement vary by jurisdiction. The master does not invent a universal national food-handler credential.

The guide preserves controls for handwashing, employee illness reporting, time/temperature, cross-contamination, cleaning/sanitizing, labeling/rotation, allergen communication, pest control, waste handling, incident escalation, burns, knives/slicers, machines, lifting, spills, fire, PPE, and chemical handling.

Allergen guidance remains non-diagnostic and directs uncertainty to approved ingredient/recipe information and escalation.

## Authority and regulated-scope traceability

PASS. The master retains the researched prohibition on assuming that every cook may independently:

- diagnose allergies or medical conditions;
- design therapeutic diets without qualified clinical authority;
- declare food safe when required controls are not met;
- override health-department or employer food-safety requirements;
- hire/fire or formally discipline staff;
- sign contracts or approve major spending;
- alter payroll/time records outside authorization;
- access unrestricted employee/customer/payment/business information;
- represent the employer to regulators without formal designation;
- conceal food-safety incidents, injuries, harassment, theft, or other reportable events.

## Privacy, cybersecurity, accessibility, and AI traceability

PASS. The English master preserves role-based access, credential protection, suspicious-request verification, secure handling of employee/customer/payment/business information, accessible service/workplace language, and public-AI restrictions.

AI is not permitted to independently determine food safety, allergen presence, medical status, legal/regulatory applicability, or whether reportable safety incidents may be ignored. Human review remains required for AI-assisted recipes, menus, training materials, schedules, labels, customer communications, and operational decisions.
