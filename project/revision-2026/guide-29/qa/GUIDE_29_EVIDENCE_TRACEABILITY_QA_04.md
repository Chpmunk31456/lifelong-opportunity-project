Canada Job Bank is presented as Government of Canada market reporting. No private figure is described as an official wage, minimum wage, entry wage, or guaranteed offer.

### Licensing, certification, and legal authority

PASS. The candidate distinguishes EPA Section 608 from state/local HVAC contractor, trade, electrical, gas, business, permit, and employer-authorization requirements. Red Seal is not treated as universal legal authority. Colombia and broader Latin America sections require verification with the applicable jurisdiction.

### Safety-critical claims

PASS. The candidate uses the research record's safety boundaries and does not provide instructions for defeating lockout/tagout, bypassing safety controls, venting refrigerant, improvising charging/recovery, overriding combustion or electrical protections, or performing unverified pressure work. ### Time-sensitive claims

PASS. The candidate identifies time-sensitive program availability, SENA cohorts, public job postings, salary estimates, tax-year amount, and jurisdiction requirements as items to recheck. No expired opening is represented as currently guaranteed available.
