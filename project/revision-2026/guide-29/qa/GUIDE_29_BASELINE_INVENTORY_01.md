## Controlled-revision gaps

Before Guide 29 can advance to publication, the controlled revision must independently verify and document:

- current U.S. HVAC/R occupation, wage, outlook, education, training, apprenticeship, work-environment, and employment evidence;
- clearly separated current non-government compensation estimates;
- U.S. EPA Section 608 refrigerant-handling certification requirements and current refrigerant-management rules, without implying that Section 608 replaces state or local occupational licensing;
- occupation-specific worker-safety boundaries, including electrical hazards, lockout/tagout, pressurized systems, refrigerant exposure, ladders/falls, confined or hot spaces where applicable, lifting, combustion hazards, and employer/site procedures;
- free/low-cost training, workforce funding, scholarships, employer support, apprenticeship, and work-based-learning pathways;
- current Canada occupation classification, wage, apprenticeship/certification, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic Red Seal or provincial/territorial pathways where applicable, and jurisdiction-specific verification guidance;
- current Colombia SENA and public-employment pathways relevant to refrigeration, air conditioning, HVAC/R installation, maintenance, or related occupations;
- a Latin America locator framework that does not transpose U.S., Canadian, or Colombian licensing, refrigerant, environmental, worker-safety, or employment rules across jurisdictions;
- ethical AI, privacy, accessibility, connected-building controls, diagnostic data, smart HVAC systems, and verification guidance appropriate to modern HVAC/R work;
- English evidence traceability and source freeze before localization;
- es-419 and pt-BR parity against the frozen English source;
- trilingual structural, terminology, link, encoding, DOCX, PDF, metadata, rendering, checksum, publication, and release-audit controls.
