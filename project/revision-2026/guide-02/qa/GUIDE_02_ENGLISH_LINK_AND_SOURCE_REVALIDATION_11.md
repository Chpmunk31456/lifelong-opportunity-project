### 1. SAMHSA — Core Competencies for Peer Workers

Working-master URL:
`https://www.samhsa.gov/substance-use/recovery/peer-support-workers/core-competencies`

Result: **PASS — current and relevant.**

Search-index revalidation on 2026-08-07 confirms the page title `Core Competencies for Peer Workers`, last updated 2026-03-24. ### 9. Government of Canada Job Bank — wages

Working-master URL:
`https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/24973/ca`

Result: **PASS — current table, with precision note.**

Revalidated on 2026-08-07. National low/median/high wages are C$19.00/C$26.00/C$36.06 per hour. Job Bank states that these wages were updated **November 19, 2025**, use a **2023–2024 reference period**, and are sourced from the **Statistics Canada Labour Force Survey**.

### 14. World Health Organization — peer-support/community mental-health framework

Working-master URL:
`https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/policy-law-rights/omhc-knowledge-platform`

Result: **REPLACE WITH MORE DIRECT SOURCE BEFORE FREEZE.**

The broad knowledge-platform URL was not reliably retrievable in this run. A more direct and stronger WHO source was revalidated:

`https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240025783`

Title: `Peer support mental health services: Promoting person-centred and rights-based approaches`.

WHO describes this as one of the technical packages accompanying its community mental-health guidance. It directly supports the guide's conceptual use of peer support, person-centred approaches, recovery orientation, and human-rights-based community services. WHO guidance does not create a portable national occupational credential.

Recommendation: replace the broad WHO knowledge-platform ledger URL with the direct peer-support publication URL in the frozen English source.
