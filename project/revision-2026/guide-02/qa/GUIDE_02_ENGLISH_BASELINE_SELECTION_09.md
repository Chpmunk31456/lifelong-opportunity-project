## Evidence

The existing version 1.0 DOCX and PDF did not pass the strict equivalence gate. Strict normalized similarity was 0.809991 against the controlled 0.970000 threshold.

After removing known extraction noise, the semantic diagnostic measured token sequence similarity of 0.967406, DOCX token coverage by PDF of 0.980461, PDF token coverage by DOCX of 0.955446, DOCX five-token n-gram coverage by PDF of 0.898694, PDF five-token n-gram coverage by DOCX of 0.884002, DOCX heading coverage by PDF of 0.581395, and PDF heading coverage by DOCX of 0.446429.

These results establish substantial shared content but sufficient unmatched wording and structure to retain the substantive-divergence HOLD.

## Controlled reconstruction method

Neither version 1.0 artifact is designated the sole authoritative source.

The 2026 English working master will use the DOCX extraction as the structural spine because it preserves editable paragraph and heading order. The PDF extraction remains a mandatory completeness cross-check. Substantive PDF-only material must be incorporated, superseded by verified revision material, or documented as intentionally omitted before the English master is frozen.

This decision does not relax the existing equivalence threshold and does not convert the prior HOLD into a PASS. It only establishes the reconstruction method for the next controlled gate.

## Reconstruction controls

1. Preserve the most complete verified occupational content from either artifact unless outdated, duplicated, misleading, or superseded by current sourced revision material.
2. Do not infer credential portability between jurisdictions.
3. Do not infer one country or region's wages from another. Clearly distinguish official occupation data, official proxy data, and current non-government estimates.
4. Correct spelling, grammar, encoding, accessibility, readability, links, version statements, and terminology during reconstruction rather than reproducing known v1.0 defects.
5. Integrate the approved Guide 02 official-source, funding/training, apprenticeship, income, Colombia, and Latin America evidence controls before freezing the English master.
6. Record unresolved substantive DOCX/PDF differences in QA evidence rather than silently selecting one artifact.
