## Interpretation controls — PASS
- “Assistant,” “coordinator,” “planner,” and “manager” authority distinctions are explicitly examples because employer titles vary.
- Certifications are described as employer/market dependent, not universal legal credentials.
- WIOA, Canadian supports, SENA, and regional vocational systems are locators; no eligibility, admission, seat, scholarship, or funding guarantee is made.
- Registered Apprenticeship is not asserted to be routinely available.
- Accessibility guidance does not claim legal compliance or certification.
- OSHA material is used for emergency-planning principles, not as a claim that every event is governed by the same standard.
- NIST AI RMF is described as voluntary.
- Cybersecurity advice directs learners to employer-approved systems and escalation rather than creating a substitute security policy.
