## Controls reviewed

### Structure — PASS

The working master contains the project/status header, promise, AI-assistance disclosure, ethical/practical limits, numbered Sections 1 through 19, a controlled source ledger, and version/maintenance controls.

### Bookkeeping/payroll separation — PASS

The guide does not collapse bookkeeping and payroll into a single official wage series. It preserves separate U.S. official occupational data, separate current non-government market estimates, and separate Canadian NOC mappings.

### Income labeling — PASS

- Bookkeeping BLS data are identified as Bookkeeping, Accounting, and Auditing Clerks, not a generic combined-role salary.
- Payroll BLS data are identified as Payroll and Timekeeping Clerks.
- ZipRecruiter Bookkeeper and Payroll Specialist figures are explicitly labeled non-government market estimates with dates.
- Canada bookkeeping and payroll wage data remain tied to NOC 12200 and NOC 13102 respectively.
- Colombia/Latin America text prohibits inventing a directly comparable national wage where one has not been verified.

### Education and credential controls — PASS

The working master does not represent FPC, CPP, private bookkeeping credentials, SENA complementary courses, apprenticeships, or employer training as universal legal licenses. Program and employer recognition must be verified before payment.

### Employer-support control — PASS

The unsupported legacy statement that a service period may last up to two years has been removed. Employer support is tied to the actual written plan/agreement. The source ledger identifies current IRS Section 127 guidance and preserves the distinction between general education information and individualized tax advice.

### Colombia and Latin America — PASS

The guide includes current SENA technical, technologist, and complementary-learning examples; distinguishes short complementary courses from full occupational programs; directs readers to Servicio Público de Empleo for current title-specific local research; and warns against assuming cross-country portability.

### Ethics, privacy, cybersecurity, and AI — PASS

The guide includes controls against fabricated or altered records, unauthorized payment/payroll changes, control bypass, unsupported professional advice, disclosure of payroll/banking/tax data, and use of confidential information in unauthorized public AI tools.

### Accessibility and natural readability — PASS for intake

The working master uses descriptive headings, short paragraphs, bullet lists, sequential numbering only where sequence matters, direct warnings, and plain-language explanations. Detailed sentence-level editorial review, link checking, final Markdown/encoding inspection, and publication-artifact accessibility remain separate gates.
