## Editorial and terminology review — PASS

The working master was reviewed for spelling, grammar, sentence flow, natural human readability, role boundaries, and consistent treatment of bookkeeping versus payroll work. The guide preserves the important distinction between:

- bookkeeping/accounting-clerk duties and payroll/timekeeping duties;
- occupational statistics and exact-title non-government market estimates;
- public or employer training and optional private credentials;
- short complementary courses and longer technical/technologist programs; and
- general career information and individualized accounting, tax, legal, benefits, or financial advice.

The legacy generic claim that an employer education service obligation may last up to two years was intentionally excluded. Readers are instead told to obtain and evaluate the actual written employer plan or agreement.

## High-impact U.S. wage and outlook traceability — PASS

Fresh review on 2026-08-08 reconfirmed:

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Bookkeeping, Accounting, and Auditing Clerks (SOC 43-3031): **$49,210 median annual wage in May 2024**, **6% projected decline from 2024 to 2034**, and about **170,000 openings per year** on average over the projection period.
- ZipRecruiter Bookkeeper: **$50,573/year ($24.31/hour), as of July 18, 2026**. This remains explicitly labeled a non-government market estimate.
- ZipRecruiter Payroll Specialist: **$56,982/year ($27.40/hour), as of July 26, 2026**. This remains explicitly labeled a non-government market estimate.
- The controlled evidence intake separately records the May 2024 BLS Payroll and Timekeeping Clerks reference of **$55,290 median annual wage**. It is kept separate from bookkeeping data and is not represented as a guaranteed Payroll Specialist salary.

No market-source date correction was required during this gate.

## Canada traceability — PASS

Government of Canada Job Bank continues to map:

- bookkeeping to **Accounting technicians and bookkeepers, NOC 12200**, with national wages **C$19.55 low / C$28.02 median / C$45.07 high per hour**;
- payroll administration to **NOC 13102**, with national wages **C$20.00 low / C$30.00 median / C$43.27 high per hour**.

Job Bank identifies the wage tables as updated November 19, 2025, using a 2023–2024 reference period. The master correctly treats these as separate occupational groupings rather than combining them into one wage.

## Colombia and Latin America controls — PASS

The master:

- uses SENA and Servicio Público de Empleo as public-first Colombian pathway locators;
- distinguishes SENA technical and technologist programs from shorter complementary courses;
- does not invent a Colombian national Bookkeeping and Payroll Specialist salary;
- requires title-specific local wage and duty research; and
- warns that payroll, labor, tax, social-security, accounting-profession, and credential rules vary by country.

## Funding, employer support, and work-based learning — PASS

The guide includes free-first learning, public workforce resources, scholarships/institutional aid, employer-paid education, and work-based learning. U.S. employer educational assistance is tied to current IRS Section 127 guidance and the actual written employer plan rather than a generalized repayment duration. Registered Apprenticeship is presented as something to verify through Apprenticeship.gov or the responsible registration authority, not as a guaranteed pathway for every locality.

## Ethics, privacy, cybersecurity, accessibility, and AI — PASS

The master retains explicit controls against fabricated or altered financial/payroll records, unauthorized bank or payee changes, disclosure of confidential employee or financial information, and unsupported professional advice. It requires employer-approved systems and data handling, treats payment-destination changes as high-risk, and prohibits use of public AI services with confidential records unless explicitly authorized. Accessibility is addressed across education, testing, work-based learning, and employment rather than only at admission.
