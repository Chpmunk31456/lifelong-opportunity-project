## U.S. occupation mapping and current official evidence

`Patient Services Representative` and `Medical Office Coordinator` do not map one-to-one to a single federal occupation. For nonclinical medical-office administration, **Medical Secretaries and Administrative Assistants, SOC 43-6013**, is a useful broad benchmark.

BLS May 2025 OEWS national data for Medical Secretaries and Administrative Assistants report:

- employment: **961,610**;
- mean hourly wage: **$22.50**;
- mean annual wage: **$46,800**; and
- median hourly wage: **$22.08**.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2025 OEWS Table 1  
https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ocwage.t01.htm

The BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook reports for Medical Secretaries and Administrative Assistants:

- **850,000** jobs in 2024;
- **885,300** projected jobs in 2034;
- **4%** projected growth; and
- **35,300** projected numeric growth.

The same OOH reports a **$44,640 median annual wage in May 2024** for the medical-secretary subtype and says medical secretaries need familiarity with medical terminology/codes, records, and hospital or laboratory procedures. These occupational statistics are benchmarks, not guaranteed Patient Services Representative or coordinator compensation.

Source:  
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/secretaries-and-administrative-assistants.htm

## Colombia

## Healthcare privacy and security boundaries — United States

For organizations subject to HIPAA, HHS states that the Security Rule establishes national standards to protect electronic protected health information and requires appropriate **administrative, physical, and technical safeguards** for its confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Source: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services  
https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/index.html

HHS states that the HIPAA Privacy Rule's minimum-necessary standard **generally** requires reasonable efforts to limit uses, disclosures, and requests for PHI to what is necessary for the intended purpose, but the standard has important exceptions, including certain treatment disclosures/requests and disclosures authorized by the individual. The guide must not simplify this into an absolute rule applying identically to every disclosure.

Source:  
https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/minimum-necessary-requirement/index.html

These U.S. rules do not automatically apply outside their legal scope, and this guide is not legal advice.

## U.S. funding and work-based learning

Federal Student Aid depends on student and program eligibility; no private short course should be assumed eligible.

https://studentaid.gov/articles/fafsa-student-steps/

State/local workforce systems may support eligible adult or dislocated-worker training under applicable WIOA rules and local funding.

https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/training/adulttraining

IRS guidance states that under a compliant Section 127 employer educational-assistance program, for calendar years **2025 and 2026** up to **$5,250** of qualifying educational assistance may be excluded from an employee's gross income. This is a tax-rule ceiling, not a guaranteed employer benefit.

https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-updates-frequently-asked-questions-about-section-127-educational-assistance-programs

https://www.apprenticeship.gov/career-seekers

## Research controls

- The role is treated as **nonclinical administrative support unless the actual job requires separately qualified clinical duties**.
- No medical diagnosis, treatment recommendation, medication advice, independent clinical triage, interpretation of test results, or alteration of clinical records is authorized by this guide.
- Privacy, identity-verification, release-of-information, scheduling, payment, insurance, referral, and emergency-escalation procedures must follow current employer policy and applicable law.
- No compensation figure is a promise or individualized forecast.
- Government and private-market compensation evidence remain separate.
- No course, credential, funding source, employer benefit, apprenticeship, or accommodation is guaranteed.
- No claim of independent human review, professional translation certification, accessibility certification, accreditation, legal review, clinical authority, or privacy compliance certification is authorized.
