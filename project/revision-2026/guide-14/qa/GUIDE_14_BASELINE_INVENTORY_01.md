## Occupation-mapping caution

`Patient Services Representative` and `Medical Office Coordinator` are practical healthcare-administration titles rather than a single government occupation. The controlled revision must distinguish nonclinical administrative work from medical assisting, nursing, coding, billing, clinical triage, health-information management, insurance adjudication, and licensed clinical authority.

A useful U.S. administrative benchmark is **Medical Secretaries and Administrative Assistants, SOC 43-6013**, but not every patient-service position maps exactly to it.
