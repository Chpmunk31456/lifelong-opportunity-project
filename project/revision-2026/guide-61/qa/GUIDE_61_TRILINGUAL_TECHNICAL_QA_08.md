### Safety and regulated-scope controls — PASS

All three editions preserve explicit controls for:

- no independent developmental/behavioral/medical diagnosis;
- no therapy, special-education, nursing, or medical treatment without separate qualification/authorization;
- medication only when law, employer policy, training, and authorization permit;
- no unauthorized changes to care, allergy, diet, behavioral, individualized-education, or medical instructions;
- no unauthorized restraint, seclusion, punishment, transport, photography, recording, livestreaming, or private messaging;
- mandatory-reporting and safeguarding escalation;

- allergy, food-safety, hygiene, safe-sleep, infection-control, first-aid/RCP, and emergency boundaries;
- protection of identifiable child, family, custody, medical, attendance, photo/video, and incident data.

### Responsible-AI and cybersecurity controls — PASS

Cybersecurity controls consistently cover MFA, credentials, approved applications/devices, phishing, parent communication, role-based access, cameras/recordings, incident reporting, remote access, and secure record disposal.
