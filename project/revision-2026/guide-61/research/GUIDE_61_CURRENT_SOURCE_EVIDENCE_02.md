# Guide 61 — Current-Source Evidence 02

**Occupation:** Preschool Assistant and Childcare Worker  
**Stage:** Current-Source Research — **PASS**  
**Research date:** 2026-08-20

## Controlled scope

Guide 61 covers support-level preschool assistant and childcare-worker work: supervising children, supporting routines, play and learning activities, assisting with meals/hygiene/rest, observing and documenting routine information, maintaining safe environments, and working under employer and jurisdictional rules.

This guide must not present a support-level worker as automatically equivalent to a licensed/certified early childhood educator, lead preschool teacher, special-education professional, nurse, therapist, social worker, or other regulated professional. Credential, background-check, ratio, first-aid, safeguarding, reporting, food-handling, and facility rules vary by jurisdiction and setting.

## United States — O*NET / BLS

Primary occupational mapping: **O*NET-SOC 39-9011.00 — Childcare Workers**.

O*NET, updated in 2026, describes childcare workers as attending to children in schools, businesses, private households, and childcare institutions, including dressing, feeding, bathing, and overseeing play.

O*NET reports 2025 BLS wage data:

- **Median hourly wage: USD $16.82**
- **Median annual wage: USD $34,980**
- **Employment in 2024: approximately 991,600**
- **Projected openings, 2024–2034: approximately 160,200 per year**
- O*NET characterizes projected growth as decline (-1% or lower).

The BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook page for Childcare Workers reports May 2024 median pay of **USD $15.41/hour / $32,050/year**, employment of approximately **991,600**, projected employment decline of **3% from 2024 to 2034**, and approximately **160,200 openings per year**. This older BLS wage figure should not be blended with the newer 2025 O*NET/BLS wage data; retain dates and sources clearly.

BLS states that education and training requirements vary by setting, state, and employer, ranging from no formal education to certification in early childhood education. The Quick Facts page lists high school diploma or equivalent and short-term on-the-job training as the typical profile.

Primary sources:

- https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/39-9011.00
- https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/39-9011.00
- https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/childcare-workers.htm

## Canada — NOC 42202

Closest broad mapping: **Early childhood educators and assistants, NOC 42202**.

Government of Canada Job Bank distinguishes assistants from early childhood educators. It describes early childhood educator assistants as providing care for infants and preschool- to school-age children under the guidance of early childhood educators.

National wage data updated November 19, 2025:

- **CAD $16.95/hour low**
- **CAD $22.30/hour median**
- **CAD $30.03/hour high**

Underlying national wage reference period: 2023–2024 Labour Force Survey data.

Job Bank notes that employment requirements differ between early childhood educators and assistants and that licensing/certification requirements may apply to educator titles by province or territory. Guide 61 must therefore avoid implying that a generic assistant title automatically grants educator licensure or certification.

Primary sources:

- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/5196/ca
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/requirements/5194/ca
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/297647

## Colombia — SENA CNO

Primary support-role mapping: **CNO 4311 — Auxiliares de educación para la primera infancia**.

SENA describes this occupation as providing support in educational activities under the supervision of an early-childhood educator, oriented toward the integral development of children in initial and preschool education. Listed denominations include Asistente de preescolar, Asistente de primera infancia, Auxiliar de educación para la primera infancia, Auxiliar de jardín infantil, Auxiliar de preescolar, and related titles.

Related childcare-support mapping: **CNO 6372 — Auxiliares del cuidado de niños**. SENA lists duties such as caring for children during play and other activities, feeding/dressing, helping children develop social/hygiene habits, recording observations, supporting records, and supervising children in residential or childcare contexts.

Primary sources:

- https://observatorio.sena.edu.co/Clasificacion/CnoDetalle?tags=4311
- https://observatorio.sena.edu.co/Comportamiento/CnoDetalleFunciones?tags=6372

## Colombia — current public training examples

SENA Betowa lists **Atención integral a la primera infancia**, a technical program of **2,208 hours**, with competencies including caring for children according to national policies and legal requirements, supporting pedagogical processes, health actions, communication, ethics, and practical-stage outcomes. Availability depends on location, modality, cohort, admission process, and seats; it must not be described as universally open or universally required.

A current Betowa page also lists **Estimulación integral en la primera infancia**, a **432-hour** technical-deepening program for people who already have relevant technical/technologist/professional background. It is an advanced/adjacent option, not an entry requirement for every support role.

Sources:

- https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/atencion-integral-a-la-primera-infancia?modality=P&offertype=company&programId=182575
- https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/estimulacion-integral-en-la-primera-infancia?modality=P&offertype=open&programId=200177

## Editorial controls for the English master

The revised Guide 61 English source must:

1. distinguish childcare/preschool assistant work from licensed/certified ECE or lead-teacher work;
2. preserve jurisdiction-specific credential, screening, ratio, safeguarding, first-aid, food-safety, and mandatory-reporting caveats;
3. preserve source dates when wage figures differ;
4. use current O*NET 2025 wage data as the primary U.S. wage benchmark while labeling the older BLS OOH 2024 wage data separately;
5. use NOC 42202 for Canada and distinguish assistant requirements from educator licensing/certification;
6. use CNO 4311 as the primary Colombia assistant mapping and CNO 6372 as a related childcare-support mapping;
7. not invent a national Colombia wage figure where no clean current comparable official series has been established in this research pass;
8. treat SENA program availability and eligibility as conditional, never guaranteed;
9. include privacy, child-safeguarding, emergency, digital-safety, and responsible-AI boundaries appropriate to work with minors; and
10. preserve the project assurance boundary: internal AI-assisted controlled revision does not equal independent human certification, accredited training, legal review, childcare licensure approval, or guaranteed employment.
