## U.S. occupation mapping

The practical title combines quality-assurance coordination with process-improvement support and therefore does not map one-to-one to a single federal occupation.

For product/material inspection and operational quality-control duties, the closest BLS benchmark is **Quality Control Inspectors, SOC 51-9061**. BLS reports a **$47,460 median annual wage in May 2024**, approximately **$22.82/hour**, about **598,000 jobs in 2024**, little or no projected employment change from 2024–2034, and about **69,900 openings per year**. BLS describes a high-school diploma plus on-the-job training as typical for entry-level inspector work. These figures are relevant only where the job actually includes inspection/testing duties.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics  
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/quality-control-inspectors.htm

For process-analysis and organizational-efficiency work, **Management Analysts, SOC 13-1111** is a useful higher-level comparator, not a coordinator wage proxy. BLS reports a **$101,190 median annual wage in May 2024**, **9% projected growth from 2024–2034**, and about **98,100 openings per year**. Management-analyst roles generally carry higher education and experience expectations, so these figures must never be presented as expected pay for a junior coordinator.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics  
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/management-analysts.htm

## Canada

Canada Job Bank mappings vary sharply by industry, which reinforces the duty-level mapping rule. For example, `Quality Assurance Officer` is mapped to a professional/scientific occupation family and Job Bank reports Canada-wide wages updated November 19, 2025, with a 2023–2024 reference period, of **C$24.00 low, C$36.10 median, and C$67.18 high**. This should be used only where the underlying duties match that Job Bank occupation.

Source: Government of Canada Job Bank  
https://www.on.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/18126/ca

Manufacturing inspector titles can map to different NOCs and materially lower wage bands. The guide therefore must not present a single Canadian quality-assurance wage as universal.

## Colombia pathway

SENA Betowa currently lists **Calidad: Gestión del Mejoramiento Continuo**, a **48-hour virtual complementary program**. Its published content includes identifying improvement actions from diagnostic results, implementing solutions, evaluating results using management indicators and customer requirements, and formulating improvement plans. Admission depends on current availability and SENA procedures.

Source: SENA Betowa  
https://betowa.sena.edu.co/oferta/calidad-gestion-del-mejoramiento-continuo?modality=V

This course can support free-first skill development but must not be represented as equivalent to a regulated professional license, engineering qualification, industry-specific auditor certification, or employer guarantee.

## U.S. funding and employer support

For eligible students and eligible postsecondary/career-school programs, Federal Student Aid may be available; no short course should be assumed eligible.

https://studentaid.gov/articles/fafsa-student-steps/

State and local workforce systems may fund eligible training under applicable WIOA rules and local policy.

https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/training/adulttraining

Under a compliant employer educational-assistance program, IRS Section 127 guidance states that for calendar years **2025 and 2026**, up to **$5,250** in qualifying educational assistance may be excluded from an employee's gross income. This is a U.S. tax-rule ceiling, not a guaranteed employer benefit.

https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-updates-frequently-asked-questions-about-section-127-educational-assistance-programs

Registered Apprenticeship can be relevant to some quality-control occupations, particularly manufacturing, but availability is employer- and location-specific. Search actual openings.

https://www.apprenticeship.gov/career-seekers

## Research controls

- Do not collapse inspection, quality systems, software QA, laboratory QA, healthcare quality, regulated manufacturing, engineering quality, or business-process improvement into one occupation.
- Distinguish coordinator/support duties from authority to release product, certify conformity, sign regulatory records, conduct independent audits, approve engineering deviations, or make clinical/safety decisions.
- Keep official government statistics separate from private title-based market estimates.
- No wage, credential, funding source, apprenticeship, certification, or course is a guarantee.
- Current links and dated facts must be revalidated before final publication.
