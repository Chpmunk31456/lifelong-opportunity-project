## Confirmed claims

1. Registered Apprenticeship is correctly described as paid work with structured learning, mentoring, wage progression, and a recognized credential.
2. FAFSA is correctly described as a free application that may provide access to grants, work-study, and loans, subject to eligibility and deadlines.
3. SENA may be used as an official Colombia training starting point, but continuous enrollment must be tied only to the specific course family that states it.
4. SNIES remains an active official source for institution and program checks and supports the requirement to verify active program status.
5. ICETEX remains an active official portal, but scholarships, funds, and repayable credit must not be conflated.

## Access limitations

- A 403 response from CareerOneStop may reflect automated-access controls rather than a broken public link.
- The Canada Job Bank endpoint requires a separate browser-based or alternate-validator check.
- The Servicio Público de Empleo returned a 502 during this validation and must be rechecked before publication.
- No failed automated request is treated as proof that the public resource is permanently unavailable.
