# 7. Funding, free or low-cost training, financial aid, and apprenticeship locator

Start with free, publicly supported, employer-supported, or low-cost pathways before considering debt. A program appearing online does not mean that it is open, recognized, affordable, available in your location, or suitable for your intended occupation.

Distinguish among:

- Free training
- Subsidized or low-cost training
- Grants and scholarships that normally do not require repayment
- Conditional or forgivable aid
- Employer-paid or reimbursed education
- Paid apprenticeships, co-ops, traineeships, and other work-based learning
- Unpaid internships or volunteer experience
- Loans and other repayable financing

Do not describe a loan as a scholarship, a stipend as salary, a training record as a professional license, or registration with an employment service as a guarantee of placement.

For every listed opportunity, record the country or region, provider, support type, basic eligibility, current application status, paid or unpaid status, known repayment obligation, official link, and last-verified date.

## United States

Use official federal and state systems as starting points:

- CareerOneStop and American Job Centers for workforce services, local training support, and WIOA-eligible provider directories
- Apprenticeship.gov for Registered Apprenticeship information and active listings
- Federal Student Aid for grants, Federal Work-Study, and federal student loans
- State vocational rehabilitation agencies for eligible people with disabilities
- SNAP Employment and Training where available
- Public libraries, community colleges, unions, nonprofit organizations, and employer training programs

Registered Apprenticeship normally combines paid employment, structured workplace learning, related instruction, mentorship, wage progression, and an industry-recognized credential. Availability, employer selection, work authorization, location, and entry requirements still apply.

Federal Work-Study provides earned wages through eligible part-time work. It is not a tuition grant, and an award does not guarantee that a job will be available. WIOA, vocational rehabilitation, and SNAP Employment and Training services also depend on state or local eligibility, funding, and program capacity.

Official starting points:

- CareerOneStop training resources: https://www.careeronestop.org/FindTraining/find-training.aspx
- WIOA training-provider finder: https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/EmploymentAndTraining/find-WIOA-training-programs.aspx
- Apprenticeship career seekers: https://www.apprenticeship.gov/career-seekers
- Apprenticeship listings: https://www.apprenticeship.gov/finder/listings
- Federal Student Aid: https://studentaid.gov/
- SNAP Employment and Training: https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap-et
- Rehabilitation Services Administration: https://rsa.ed.gov/

## Canada

Use Job Bank for official occupation, wage, outlook, skills, and job information by occupation and location. Confirm whether an employer can consider applicants who do not already have Canadian work authorization.

Apprenticeship generally combines registered workplace training and in-class technical training under provincial or territorial rules. Red Seal pathways apply only to designated trades and do not replace immigration, employer, safety, or other regulatory requirements.

Current support may include:

- Provincial or territorial apprenticeship services
- Employment Insurance during eligible full-time technical training
- Canada Apprentice Loans for eligible apprentices in designated Red Seal trades
- Student grants, loans, scholarships, disability-related education support, and repayment assistance
- Employer, union, or organization funding programs

The Canada Apprentice Loan is repayable credit, not a grant. The former Apprenticeship Incentive Grant and Apprenticeship Completion Grant closed to new applications on March 31, 2025 and must not be presented as active.

Official starting points:

- Job Bank occupation search: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/occupation_search-eng.do
- Job Bank wage search: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagessearch.xhtml
- Job Bank outlook search: https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/trend-analysis/search-job-outlooks
- Become an apprentice: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/training/support-skilled-trades-apprentices/become-apprentice.html
- Employment Insurance for apprentices: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/training/support-skilled-trades-apprentices/ei-apprentices.html
- Canada Apprentice Loan: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/training/support-skilled-trades-apprentices/loan.html
- Student aid: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/student-aid.html
- Apprenticeship funding opportunities: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/training/support-skilled-trades-apprentices/funding-opportunities.html

## Colombia

Colombia provides several public starting points, but each course, call, vacancy, scholarship, fund, and credit product must be checked separately.

### SENA

The Servicio Nacional de Aprendizaje offers technical, technological, complementary, and virtual learning pathways. Many offerings are free, but admission rules, enrollment windows, modality, location, immigration status, and program availability vary.

Do not describe an expired SENA call as currently open. Time-limited calls must include the closing date and a last-verified date.

Official starting point:

- SENA training access: https://www.sena.edu.co/es-co/formacion/Paginas/acceder-cursos.aspx

### Servicio Público de Empleo

The Servicio Público de Empleo provides access to authorized employment-service providers, vacancy systems, résumé registration, orientation, and service points. The service does not guarantee a vacancy, interview, or hire. Job seekers should not pay for access to a vacancy represented as an official public-employment-service opportunity.

Official starting points:

- Main portal: https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/
- Job-seeker services: https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/busca-empleo/

### Recognition and educational financing

Before enrolling in Colombian higher education, verify both the institution and the exact program in official systems. Institution recognition, program Registro Calificado, and high-quality accreditation are different concepts.

ICETEX administers scholarships, funds, and educational credit. Credit is repayable unless the specific official terms state a scholarship, forgivable component, or other non-repayable support.

Official starting points:

- SNIES: https://snies.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/
- ICETEX: https://web.icetex.gov.co/portal

## Brazil

### Public professional qualification

Brazilian federal pathways include professional-qualification services, QualificaPro, and Caminho Digital / Escola do Trabalhador 4.0. Course availability, prerequisites, delivery format, and certificate conditions must be checked on the live platform.

A course-completion certificate is evidence of learning. It is not automatically a degree, professional license, regulated credential, or employment guarantee.

Official starting points:

- Professional qualification: https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/servicos/trabalhador/qualificacao-profissional
- Caminho Digital: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/inscrever-em-curso-do-caminho-digital

### Professional apprenticeship

Aprendizagem Profissional generally applies to adolescents and young people ages 14 through 24. The same maximum-age limit does not apply to people with disabilities. Participation requires an authorized apprenticeship course and compliance with current education and labor rules.

Do not present this as an all-age pathway.

Official starting point:

- Professional apprenticeship: https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/assuntos/aprendizagem-profissional

### Higher-education pathways

Keep these categories separate:

- Prouni: full or partial scholarship pathway, subject to eligibility and selection
- Sisu: admission and selection system for places in participating public institutions; not itself a scholarship
- FIES: educational financing for eligible non-free private higher-education programs; repayable unless current official terms state otherwise

Official starting points:

- Prouni: https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/assuntos/es/prouni/prouni
- Sisu: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/inscrever-se-no-sisu-sistema-de-selecao-unificada
- FIES: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/obter-financiamento-do-fies

## Mexico

Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro is a government-supported workplace-training pathway generally intended for people ages 18 through 29 who are not studying and do not have employment when entering the program. Training may last up to 12 months at a participating work center.

Official 2026 information states that participants receive a monthly government stipend and IMSS medical coverage during training. This is workplace training with public support, not a regular employment contract or a guarantee of later hiring.

Registration and placement periods may open in stages and may be limited by municipality or available work centers. A training record may be issued after participation. A separate competency certificate through CONOCER may require evaluation and must not be described as automatic.

Official starting points:

- Program platform: https://www.jovenesconstruyendoelfuturo.stps.gob.mx/
- Program information: https://programasparaelbienestar.gob.mx/jovenes-construyendo-el-futuro/

## Argentina

Progresar Formación Profesional / Progresar Trabajo supports eligible people enrolled in approved vocational-training courses. It is conditional educational support, not wages or guaranteed employment.

The official 2026 application period was announced as April 27 through November 27, 2026. That date must be treated as a dated example and rechecked before publication or application. Eligibility depends on current age, residence, income, priority-group, and validated-course rules.

Official starting points:

- Application service: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/inscribirme-en-progresar-trabajo
- Requirements: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/educacion/progresar/requisitos

## Chile

Chile's Servicio Nacional de Capacitación y Empleo administers training pathways funded through specific calls. Some benefits are linked to unemployment-insurance status and are not general scholarships available to every job seeker.

The Programa Becas Fondo Cesantía Solidario page reviewed for this revision stated that applications were closed on May 28, 2026. Do not present that call as open unless ChileAtiende or SENCE confirms a new active period.

Official starting point:

- ChileAtiende program record: https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/20814-programa-becas-fondo-cesantia-solidario

## Regional verification rule

For any country not listed above, identify the official labor ministry, public employment service, education ministry, technical-training authority, scholarship portal, apprenticeship authority, disability-employment service, and licensing body relevant to the occupation.

Recheck all application windows, amounts, eligibility, residency or immigration requirements, program recognition, and links immediately before publication or application.
