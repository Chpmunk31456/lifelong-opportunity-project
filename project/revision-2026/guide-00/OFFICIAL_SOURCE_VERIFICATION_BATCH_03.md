# Guide 00 Official Source Verification — Batch 03

**Guide:** 00 — Lifelong Opportunity Foundation Guide  
**Verification date:** 2026-08-02  
**Scope:** Colombia and Brazil employment, training, apprenticeship, education-recognition, scholarship, and financing pathways  
**Status:** Controlled evidence batch; not a publication certification

## Verification standard

This batch records official-source checks that may be incorporated into the Guide 00 English working master and later translated into neutral Latin American Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. It does not claim independent human, legal, accessibility, or accreditation review.

For every pathway, the final guide must distinguish:

- free training from paid training;
- scholarships or grants from repayable credit;
- open enrollment from time-limited calls;
- institution recognition from program authorization or accreditation;
- employment services from guarantees of placement;
- apprenticeship eligibility from general training eligibility.

Readers must recheck dates, eligibility, immigration or residency requirements, and application status immediately before acting.

# Colombia

## 1. SENA technical, technological, and virtual training

**Official sources checked**

- SENA training access page: https://www.sena.edu.co/es-co/formacion/Paginas/acceder-cursos.aspx
- SENA July 2026 virtual-program call: https://www.sena.edu.co/es-co/Noticias/Paginas/noticia.aspx?IdNoticia=9632

**Verified facts**

- SENA provides technical, technological, complementary, and virtual learning pathways.
- SENA describes its listed virtual programs as free.
- The July 2026 titled virtual-training call offered more than 27,000 places in approximately 45 technical and technological programs.
- That specific July 2026 call was open only through July 24, 2026 and must not be presented as currently open after that date.
- The July 2026 call stated that Colombians and foreigners with regular migration status could apply, subject to the stated program requirements.
- Some short virtual offerings, such as the cited bilingualism courses, are described as having continuous enrollment; each course must still be checked individually.

**Publication control**

Use SENA as an official starting point, not as a promise that a particular course is open or that every reader qualifies. For time-limited titled programs, publish the current call link and exact closing date only after a fresh check. Do not copy an expired 2026 call into future editions as active.

## 2. Servicio Público de Empleo

**Official sources checked**

- Main portal: https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/
- Job-seeker page: https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/busca-empleo/
- Official FAQ for job seekers: https://www.serviciodeempleo.gov.co/atencion-a-la-ciudadania/preguntas-frecuentes/preguntas-y-respuestas-para-personas-que-buscan-empleo/

**Verified facts**

- The Servicio Público de Empleo provides a national network of authorized employment-service providers, vacancy access, résumé registration, orientation, and physical service points.
- The official portal states that the service does not charge job seekers for vacancies.
- Job seekers can use the official employment-search systems and can also receive services through authorized providers, including territorial governments, compensation-fund agencies, SENA, and authorized private agencies.

**Publication control**

Describe the SPE as an employment-access and intermediation system, not a guarantee of a vacancy, interview, or hire. Warn readers not to pay for access to a vacancy represented as an SPE opportunity.

## 3. SNIES and higher-education recognition

**Official sources checked**

- SNIES public consultations: https://snies.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/CONSULTAS-PUBLICAS/
- Ministry explanation of SNIES: https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/m/1757/w3-article-156293.html
- Ministry SACES FAQ on Registro Calificado: https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/micrositios-superior/SACES/Preguntas-frecuentes/

**Verified facts**

- SNIES provides official public consultation of higher-education institutions and academic programs.
- Registro Calificado is the authorization showing that a higher-education program meets the legal quality conditions required for it to be offered.
- Institution listing, program Registro Calificado, and high-quality accreditation are different concepts and must not be treated as synonyms.

**Publication control**

Before recommending a Colombian higher-education pathway, verify both the institution and the specific program. Do not describe a program as “accredited” merely because it appears in SNIES or holds Registro Calificado. Use “high-quality accreditation” only when the official record specifically confirms it.

## 4. ICETEX scholarships, funds, and educational credit

**Official source checked**

- ICETEX portal: https://web.icetex.gov.co/es/portal

**Verified facts**

- ICETEX administers multiple categories, including scholarships, funds, and educational credit.
- The official portal was advertising a 2026-2 educational-credit call on the verification date.
- Credit products are financing and may require repayment; they must not be labeled as scholarships or grants.
- Individual funds and scholarship calls have their own eligibility, deadlines, conditions, and possible service or academic obligations.

**Publication control**

Classify every ICETEX opportunity by its actual financial structure. State “repayable educational credit” unless the official terms expressly establish a scholarship, grant, forgivable component, or other non-repayable support. Recheck the specific call rather than linking only to the home page.

# Brazil

## 5. Federal professional-qualification pathways

**Official sources checked**

- Ministry of Labour and Employment qualification page: https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/servicos/trabalhador/qualificacao-profissional
- QualificaPro launch notice dated March 4, 2026: https://www.gov.br/secom/pt-br/acompanhe-a-secom/noticias/2026/03/governo-do-brasil-lanca-qualificapro-plataforma-de-cursos-gratuitos-que-conecta-trabalhadores-ao-mercado-de-trabalho
- Caminho Digital service: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/inscrever-em-curso-do-caminho-digital

**Verified facts**

- Brazil’s Ministry of Labour and Employment maintains public professional-qualification policy and links qualification to employability and decent work.
- The federal government announced QualificaPro on March 4, 2026 as a platform connecting workers with free remote and in-person courses and labor-market information.
- Caminho Digital / Escola do Trabalhador 4.0 offers free digital-skills and technology courses at different levels.
- The Caminho Digital service page states that completed courses generate a certificate from the Ministry of Labour and Employment and Microsoft.

**Publication control**

State that course availability, prerequisites, modality, and certification must be checked on the live platform. A course certificate is evidence of completion; it is not equivalent to a regulated professional license, degree, guaranteed employer recognition, or guaranteed employment.

## 6. Professional apprenticeship in Brazil

**Official source checked**

- Ministry of Labour and Employment apprenticeship portal: https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/assuntos/aprendizagem-profissional

**Verified facts**

- Aprendizagem Profissional is a public labor-market inclusion policy for adolescents and young people generally from age 14 through 24.
- The official page states that people with disabilities are not subject to the same maximum age limit.
- Apprentices must be enrolled in an authorized professional-apprenticeship course.
- The Ministry maintains the Cadastro Nacional de Aprendizagem Profissional and official authorized-course information.

**Publication control**

Do not present Brazilian apprenticeship as an all-age general pathway. State the age limits and the disability exception accurately, and require readers to confirm authorized providers, course status, employer conditions, school-attendance rules, and local labor requirements.

## 7. Prouni, Sisu, and FIES

**Official sources checked**

- Prouni: https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/assuntos/es/prouni/prouni
- Sisu service: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/inscrever-se-no-sisu-sistema-de-selecao-unificada
- FIES student-financing service: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/obter-financiamento-do-fies

**Verified facts**

- Prouni awards full or partial scholarships for eligible students in participating private higher-education institutions and uses ENEM and income-related eligibility rules.
- The official Prouni page states that applications are free and conducted through the federal Acesso Único portal during selection periods.
- Sisu is the federal electronic selection system for places offered by public higher-education institutions.
- FIES is educational financing for eligible students in non-free private higher-education programs and must be treated as financing, not a scholarship.

**Publication control**

Keep the categories distinct:

- **Prouni:** scholarship pathway, subject to eligibility and selection.
- **Sisu:** admission/selection pathway for public higher education; not itself a scholarship.
- **FIES:** repayable educational financing unless current official terms expressly provide a different treatment.

Always verify the current selection calendar, ENEM requirements, income rules, participating programs, and repayment terms.

# Cross-language terminology controls

The following concepts must remain distinct in all three editions:

| English | Neutral Latin American Spanish | Brazilian Portuguese |

| scholarship | beca | bolsa de estudos |

| educational credit / loan | crédito educativo o préstamo educativo | financiamento estudantil ou empréstimo educacional |
| apprenticeship | aprendizaje remunerado / formación de aprendiz, according to country | aprendizagem profissional |
| institution recognition | reconocimiento de la institución | reconhecimento da instituição |
| program authorization | autorización o registro del programa | autorização ou reconhecimento do curso |
| high-quality accreditation | acreditación de alta calidad | acreditação de alta qualidade |
| employment service | servicio de empleo | serviço de emprego |

Country-specific legal program names must remain in their official language, with a plain-language explanation rather than an invented literal translation.
