# Guide 00 Official Source Verification — Batch 04

**Guide:** 00 — Lifelong Opportunity Foundation Guide  
**Verification date:** 2026-08-02  
**Scope:** Mexico, Argentina, and Chile employment, training, apprenticeship, scholarship, and time-limited funding pathways  
**Status:** Controlled evidence batch; not a publication certification

## Verification standard

This batch records official-source checks that may be incorporated into the Guide 00 English working master and later translated into neutral Latin American Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. It does not claim independent human, legal, accessibility, immigration, accreditation, or professional review.

For every pathway, the final guide must distinguish:

- permanent registration from a time-limited application or placement window;
- a training stipend or scholarship from wages, salary, or guaranteed employment;
- a course-completion record from a professional license or regulated credential;
- free training from repayable financing;
- eligibility by age, residency, income, employment status, or study status;
- an employment or training service from a promise of placement.

Readers must recheck live application status, current amounts, eligibility, residency or immigration rules, and official terms immediately before acting.

# Mexico

## 1. Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro

**Official sources checked**

- Program platform: https://www.jovenesconstruyendoelfuturo.stps.gob.mx/
- Program description updated January 2026: https://programasparaelbienestar.gob.mx/jovenes-construyendo-el-futuro/
- June 2026 placement-window notice: https://programasparaelbienestar.gob.mx/jovenes-construyendo-el-futuro-abre-periodo-para-ingresos-el-1-de-junio/
- July 2026 competency-certification notice: https://programasparaelbienestar.gob.mx/eres-aprendiz-de-jovenes-construyendo-el-futuro-tramita-tu-certificado-de-competencias-laborales/

**Verified facts**

- The program is directed to people generally between ages 18 and 29 who are not studying and do not have employment when entering the program.
- It provides workplace-based training for up to 12 months through participating work centers.
- The official 2026 program pages state that participants receive a monthly government stipend of MXN 9,582.47 and IMSS medical coverage during training.
- Registration may remain available while placement or linking periods open in stages and may be geographically focused. A prior 2026 placement opening must not be represented as currently open without a fresh platform check.
- Participants receive a training record. A separate competency certificate through CONOCER may require evaluation and must not be described as automatic for every participant.

**Publication control**

Describe this as a government-supported workplace-training pathway with a stipend and medical coverage, not as a regular employment contract or a guarantee of later hiring. State the age and current-status rules clearly. Recheck the live map, municipality availability, placement window, stipend amount, and documentary requirements before publication. Keep the official program name in Spanish in all editions.

# Argentina

## 2. Progresar Formación Profesional / Progresar Trabajo

**Official sources checked**

- Application service: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/inscribirme-en-progresar-trabajo
- 2026 FAQ and application dates: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/educacion/progresar/preguntas-frecuentes-progresar/progresar-formacion-profesional-faq
- 2026 opening notice: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/498526
- Requirements portal: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/educacion/progresar/requisitos

**Verified facts**

- Progresar Formación Profesional supports eligible people enrolled in approved vocational-training courses.
- The official 2026 notice states that the application period runs from April 27 through November 27, 2026.
- General age eligibility is 18 through 24, with the upper limit extended to 35 for people without formally registered employment and with additional extensions for some priority groups.
- Applicants generally must be Argentine, naturalized, or foreign residents with at least two years of legal residence and a DNI, subject to the current rules.
- The 2026 notice states an income ceiling of three times the Salario Mínimo Vital y Móvil and requires enrollment in a validated training pathway.
- Payments are conditional educational support. The official notice states that 80% is paid during participation and 20% is retained pending completion certification.

**Publication control**

Describe this as a conditional scholarship or educational-support pathway, not wages and not guaranteed employment. State the 2026 closing date only in a dated resource table and require a fresh check for later editions. Verify the selected course is formally validated before recommending the pathway. Do not generalize age or residency rules beyond the official current terms.

# Chile

## 3. SENCE-funded training and unemployment-linked scholarships

**Official source checked**

- ChileAtiende summary for Programa Becas Fondo Cesantía Solidario: https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/20814-programa-becas-fondo-cesantia-solidario

**Verified facts**

- The pathway provides fully financed training through Chile's Servicio Nacional de Capacitación y Empleo (SENCE) for eligible active beneficiaries of the Fondo de Cesantía Solidario.
- The official ChileAtiende page, updated May 28, 2026, states that the application was closed on that date.
- The support is tied to unemployment-insurance status and specific eligibility conditions; it is not a general scholarship available to every job seeker.
- The cited route includes online training options, but availability and course structures can change by call.

**Publication control**

Do not list this specific scholarship as open unless the official page later confirms a new active call. Use it as an example of a time-limited, unemployment-linked training benefit and direct readers to current SENCE and ChileAtiende services for active offerings. State eligibility and application status explicitly.
