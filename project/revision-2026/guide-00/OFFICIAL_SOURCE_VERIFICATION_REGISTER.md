## Editorial controls derived from the verification

1. Do not say that a funding program is available merely because an information page still exists.
2. Mark the Canadian Apprenticeship Incentive Grant and Apprenticeship Completion Grant as closed as of 2025-03-31.
3. Describe WIOA, student aid, vocational rehabilitation, loans, scholarships, and employer support as eligibility-based, not guaranteed.
4. Describe Apprenticeship.gov and other vacancy portals as discovery tools; the employer or sponsor controls the actual opportunity.
5. For Colombia, date-stamp SENA calls and verify enrollment status because official schedules may change.
6. Distinguish nonrepayable support, conditional or forgivable support, reimbursement, and loans.
7. Recheck all URLs during final publication QA and record redirects, access barriers, and replacement pages.
