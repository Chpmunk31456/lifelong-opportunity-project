## Controlled revision requirements identified at baseline
Guide 98 requires a new evidence-led reconstruction that includes at minimum:

1. current U.S. occupation identity, duties, employment, wage, outlook and education evidence;
2. current Canadian occupational mapping, wages, requirements and outlook context;
3. current Colombia occupational mapping and training routes;
4. broader Latin American training/funding locators where relevant;
5. explicit pesticide-worker versus pesticide-handler/applicator boundaries;
6. agricultural machinery, tractor/PTO, guarding, stored-energy and maintenance safety;
7. heat, field, animal, biological, laboratory, chemical and ergonomic hazards as applicable to the target function;
8. safe sampling, measurement, chain-of-custody/data-integrity and field/laboratory documentation practices;
9. cybersecurity and data-governance controls for connected farm/lab equipment and research data;
10. responsible-AI controls prohibiting invented pesticide rates, treatment instructions, veterinary diagnoses, agronomic prescriptions, regulated decisions or fabricated field/lab results;
11. accessible learning and work-planning guidance without unsupported accommodation guarantees;
12. a complete **six-step action plan with measurable milestones**;
13. trilingual semantic/value/link parity and controlled publication QA.
