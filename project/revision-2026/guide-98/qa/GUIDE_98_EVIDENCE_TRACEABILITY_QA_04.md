## Controlled distinctions verified

1. **Official vs private compensation:** official O*NET/BLS/Job Bank values are primary; ZipRecruiter and Computrabajo material remains clearly secondary.
2. **Current vs historical date:** 2025 U.S. wages are not conflated with May-2024 OOH figures.
3. **Occupation vs broader group:** agricultural technician figures are not replaced by broader agricultural-and-food-science-technician values.
4. **Canada mapping:** NOC 22110 is labeled as a broad biological-technologist/technician family, not a perfect equivalence.
5. **Colombia wage caveat:** the OCUPACOL range is not presented as statistically representative.
6. **Training vs guarantee:** WIOA, Canada supports, SENA and OIT/Cinterfor are locators/pathways, not guarantees of admission, seats, funding or employment.
7. **Pesticide scope:** technician title does not confer handler/applicator authority.
8. **Animal scope:** technical animal work does not confer veterinary diagnosis/prescription authority.
9. **Machinery scope:** technician title does not confer unrestricted tractor/machinery operation or maintenance authority.
10. **AI scope:** AI is restricted from regulated, safety-critical, diagnostic, treatment, pesticide, laboratory-result and compliance decisions.
