All editions direct pesticide tasks to the current label, employer procedures, WPS requirements, required training/certification, PPE and applicable jurisdictional rules.

### Machinery, PTO and field safety
All three editions preserve equivalent controls for:
- PTO and rotating-shaft entanglement;
- guards and shields;
- clearing people before start-up/engagement;
- energy control before service/maintenance;
- unsupported raised equipment;
- tractor/operator authority and ROPS/seat-belt context where applicable;
- heat/weather and remote field conditions;
- chemical, biological, zoonotic and sharps risks.

No edition teaches bypassing guards, approaching an energized PTO, servicing running machinery, operating equipment without authorization or defeating required safety controls.
