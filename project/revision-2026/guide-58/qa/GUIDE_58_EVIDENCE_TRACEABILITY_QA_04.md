## Scope and assurance traceability

The master preserves the research controls requiring:

- assistant/caretaker work to remain distinct from veterinarian and veterinary technician/technologist practice;
- supervision/delegation language for clinical-adjacent support;
- no independent diagnosis, prescribing, surgery, medication decision, anesthesia/sedation decision, or emergency disposition;
- no implication that SENA animal-care training grants professional veterinary authority;
- no claim that NOC 65220 makes all veterinary-clinic tasks unrestricted;
- no guarantee of WIOA, Canadian student aid, employer funding, employment, salary, credential portability, or advancement;
- explicit workplace animal-handling, zoonotic/infection-control, sharps, PPE, lifting/restraint, and incident-reporting safeguards;
- AI and privacy limits appropriate to animal-health records and client data; and
- an explicit AI-assisted/internal-review assurance boundary.
