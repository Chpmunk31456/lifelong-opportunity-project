## Localization rules after freeze

Localization may adapt natural phrasing, punctuation, number separators, currency presentation, and jurisdiction-appropriate explanatory language, but it must not:

- add unsupported clinical authority;
- remove supervision or escalation requirements;
- alter a controlled numeric value or date;
- substitute a different source endpoint without corrective traceability review;
- turn conditional funding into a promise;
- imply cross-border credential portability;
- translate internal automated QA into independent human, legal, veterinary, accreditation, accessibility, or certified-translation approval.
