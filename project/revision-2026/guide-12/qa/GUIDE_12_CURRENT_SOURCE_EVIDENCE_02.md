## Occupation mapping — United States

The practical titles `Compliance Coordinator` and `Compliance Assistant` do not map one-to-one to a single occupation. The closest broad U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics benchmark is **Compliance Officers**. BLS describes compliance officers as workers who examine whether organizations conform to laws and regulations. The guide must distinguish coordinator/assistant support work from independent legal interpretation, formal investigation authority, regulator-facing accountability, or specialized regulated-domain roles.

BLS current Occupational Outlook Handbook evidence:

- median annual wage: **$78,420 in May 2024**;
- projected employment growth: **3% from 2024 to 2034**; and
- about **33,300 openings per year** on average over the decade.

These are occupation-level benchmarks, not guaranteed coordinator or assistant compensation.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics  
https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/compliance-officers.htm

## Canada mapping caution

Canada does not provide a single perfect national mapping for every generic compliance-coordinator title. Job Bank currently maps `Regulatory Compliance Officer` to **NOC 11201** and reports Canada-wide hourly wages updated November 19, 2025, reference period 2023–2024:

- low: **C$27.00/hour**;
- median: **C$44.10/hour**;
- high: **C$69.71/hour**.

This mapping is appropriate only for regulatory-compliance duties that fit the NOC. A junior compliance assistant, financial-compliance role, privacy role, AML role, safety role, healthcare compliance role, or industry-specific coordinator may map differently. The English revision must explicitly warn against using this wage table as a universal coordinator rate.

Source: Government of Canada Job Bank  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/27323/ca

Job Bank separately maps `Financial Compliance Officer` to **NOC 11109**, with Canada-wide wages of C$24.04 low, C$38.46 median, and C$66.67 high for the same 2023–2024 reference period. This illustrates why domain-specific mapping matters.

Source:  
https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/296408/ca

## Colombia and Latin America research rule

Do not invent a single `compliance` occupation, salary, license, or credential for Colombia or Latin America. Duties may sit within cumplimiento, cumplimiento normativo, riesgos, control interno, ética, anticorrupción, protección de datos, SARLAFT/AML, calidad, seguridad, ambiente, salud, legal, or industry-specific functions.

For Colombia, the controlled guide should direct readers to current SENA and Servicio Público de Empleo offerings that match the actual domain rather than imply that one SENA course qualifies a person for all compliance roles. Any cited program must be verified on the official catalog at source freeze and publication.

## Education and role-boundary control

Compliance work often requires understanding policies, records, evidence, reporting, training, issue tracking, and escalation. Some roles also require specialized legal, accounting, financial-crime, privacy, healthcare, environmental, occupational-safety, engineering, or regulatory knowledge. A coordinator or assistant must not be presented as authorized to provide legal advice or independently certify compliance unless the role and jurisdiction actually grant that authority.

## U.S. funding and employer support

For eligible postsecondary or career-school programs, Federal Student Aid depends on student and program eligibility; no short course should be assumed eligible.

Source:  
https://studentaid.gov/articles/fafsa-student-steps/

State and local workforce systems may support eligible adult/dislocated-worker training under applicable WIOA rules and local funding.

Source:  
https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/training/adulttraining

Under a compliant U.S. employer Section 127 educational-assistance program, IRS guidance states that for calendar years **2025 and 2026**, up to **$5,250** of qualifying educational assistance may be excluded from an employee's gross income. This is a tax-rule ceiling, not a guaranteed employer benefit.

Source: Internal Revenue Service  
https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-updates-frequently-asked-questions-about-section-127-educational-assistance-programs

Registered Apprenticeship is employer-driven and availability is local. Search actual current openings rather than implying a generic compliance apprenticeship exists everywhere.

Source:  
https://www.apprenticeship.gov/career-seekers

## Research controls

- Official government evidence and private-market estimates must remain separate evidence classes.
- No compensation figure is a promise or individualized forecast.
- No generic coordinator title should be mapped to a regulated specialty without duty-level evidence.
- No course, certification, apprenticeship, employer benefit, scholarship, or funding program should be described as available to a specific reader without current eligibility verification.
- No claim of independent human review, certified translation, accessibility certification, accreditation, legal review, or professional compliance advice is authorized by this ledger.
- Time-sensitive links and figures must be revalidated at English source freeze and before final publication.
