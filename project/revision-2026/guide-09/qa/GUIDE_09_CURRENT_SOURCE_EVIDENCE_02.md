## Evidence-use rule

This ledger separates official occupational evidence from current non-government title estimates. A `Logistics Coordinator` or `Supply Chain Coordinator` title must not automatically be represented as identical to the broader U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupation `Logisticians (SOC 13-1081)`. Local duties, employer expectations, education requirements, industry, and jurisdiction must be checked before drawing conclusions.

## United States — official occupational evidence

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — Logisticians (SOC 13-1081)

Official source: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/logisticians.htm

Verified 2026-08-08:

- Logisticians analyze and coordinate an organization's supply chain.
- BLS reports **2024 median pay of $80,880 per year ($38.89/hour)**.
- The lowest 10 percent earned less than **$49,260** and the highest 10 percent earned more than **$132,110** in May 2024.
- Employment is projected to grow **17% from 2024 to 2034**.
- BLS projects about **26,400 openings per year**, on average, over the decade.
- Typical entry-level education is a bachelor's degree, although BLS states that less education may be sufficient for some jobs and that relevant work experience may substitute for education in some cases.

**Use boundary:** These figures are official evidence for SOC 13-1081, not a guaranteed wage or education requirement for every job titled Logistics Coordinator.

## Canada — official occupational evidence

### Government of Canada Job Bank — Production and transportation logistics coordinators (NOC 13201)

Official sources:

- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/summary-occupation/296538/ca
- https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/296538

Verified 2026-08-08:

- Job Bank maps `logistics coordinator` to **NOC 13201**.
- Production logistics coordinators coordinate and expedite work flows, prepare schedules, and monitor progress; transportation logistics coordinators coordinate and monitor product movement and scheduled shipping/receiving.
- The national wage table, updated **November 19, 2025**, reports **C$21.63 low / C$29.49 median / C$44.79 high per hour**.
- The wage reference period is **2023–2024**, sourced from the Labour Force Survey, Statistics Canada.
- Job Bank states that this occupation usually requires a college diploma, an apprenticeship of less than two years, or more than six months of on-the-job training; requirements vary by employer and jurisdiction.

### IRS Section 127 educational assistance

Official source: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-updates-frequently-asked-questions-about-section-127-educational-assistance-programs

Verified 2026-08-08:

- For calendar years **2025 and 2026**, up to **$5,250** of qualifying employer educational assistance under a compliant Section 127 plan may be excluded from gross income.
- The IRS states the amount is adjusted for cost-of-living increases for taxable years after 2026.
- Employer-plan terms and eligibility must be checked in writing; the guide must not characterize the benefit as universally available.

### SENA

SENA remains a key public training pathway for logistics-related education in Colombia. Because specific cohorts and availability change, the guide should direct readers to the current SENA/Betowa catalog and verify the exact program, modality, dates, requirements, and cost before enrollment. No private training vendor should be presented as required when a free public pathway may be available.

7. Treat customs, import/export, hazardous materials, regulated goods, food/pharma, data/privacy, workplace safety, and trade-compliance duties as jurisdiction- and employer-specific and outside the guide's authority to provide individualized legal advice.
