# Guide 15 — English v2 Editorial and Accessibility QA

**Guide:** 15 — Insurance Claims and Policy Processing Specialist  
**Controlled revision:** August 2026  
**Source reviewed:** `english-v2-working-master.md`  
**Gate:** English editorial, readability, accessibility, scope, and current-fact review  
**Result:** PASS  

## Review scope

The English v2 working master was reviewed as a controlled source before localization. This gate covers spelling, grammar, sentence clarity, human readability, heading/list structure, accessibility language, occupational-scope boundaries, regulated-duty cautions, funding language, responsible-AI safeguards, income-label separation, and current-source spot verification.

This report does **not** certify translation quality, DOCX/PDF rendering, metadata, publication packaging, accreditation, or independent human review. Those remain separate downstream gates.

## Editorial and readability findings

PASS — The working master:

- uses a clear 19-section progression from role definition through pathways, funding, work-based learning, application planning, accessibility, privacy, AI, advancement, and source notes;
- uses plain-language sentences and explains specialized insurance boundaries rather than assuming prior industry knowledge;
- distinguishes clerical/processing work from regulated sales, underwriting, adjusting, coverage, liability, fraud, and legal determinations;
- avoids employment, income, funding, credential, admission, licensing, or promotion guarantees;
- uses explicit cautions before readers pay for credentials, examinations, memberships, or training;
- uses fictional-data-only portfolio exercises and warns against exposing claimant, policyholder, customer, employer, health, financial, or identity data;
- includes accessibility considerations for headings, meaningful links, keyboard access, screen readers, captions/transcripts, alternative formats, testing accommodations, and disability-support procedures; and
- uses responsible-AI language that limits public-AI use with confidential information and prohibits treating AI output as authoritative for consequential insurance determinations.

No spelling, grammar, encoding, or readability defect requiring a source-text correction was identified in this gate.

## Geographic and pathway coverage

PASS — The master contains distinct, non-interchangeable pathways for:

- United States occupational research and funding;
- Canada using NOC/OaSIS occupational mapping;
- Colombia using SENA and Servicio Público de Empleo resources; and
- Latin America beyond Colombia with jurisdiction-specific regulator, employment-service, training, and work-based-learning verification guidance.

The guide correctly warns readers not to convert U.S. wage statistics into a purported Latin American salary benchmark.

## Income-research labeling

PASS — Income evidence is separated by source type and geography:

- official U.S. BLS May 2025 OEWS figures are labeled as survey estimates for the occupation and not guaranteed individual pay;
- the ZipRecruiter figure is explicitly identified as a non-government estimate using a different title/methodology and is not merged with BLS data; and
- the Colombia figure is labeled as a single recent public-employment vacancy example, not a national salary benchmark or pay promise.

Current spot verification performed during this QA gate confirmed the BLS May 2025 national row for Insurance Claims and Policy Processing Clerks at 214,260 employment, $25.44 mean hourly, $52,920 mean annual, and $23.67 median hourly.

## Funding and employer-support controls

PASS — The guide covers FAFSA/federal student aid, WIOA/American Job Centers, employer educational assistance, scholarships, Registered Apprenticeship, employer trainee programs, paid internships, returnships, and public training. It repeatedly requires eligibility/program verification before spending money.

Current spot verification performed during this QA gate confirmed that IRS Publication 15-B (2026) states educational assistance can be excluded up to $5,250 per employee per year under a qualifying educational-assistance program. The guide appropriately notes that employer programs are optional and plan-specific.

## Canada occupational mapping control

PASS — Current OaSIS 2025 search results confirm profile 14201.02, Insurance clerks, under NOC 14201, with the lead statement that insurance clerks compile, process, and maintain insurance information. The master does not present Canadian and U.S. classifications as statistically or legally interchangeable.

## Claims and publication controls

PASS — No claim of independent human certification, accreditation, regulator endorsement, guaranteed employment, guaranteed funding, or guaranteed income was found in the v2 working master.

The source remains a working master after this gate. Passing editorial QA does not by itself authorize publication freeze.
